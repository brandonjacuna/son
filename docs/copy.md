# Site copy

The approved copy for the Sŏn investor site, slot by slot, in the locked order.
Written against `docs/structure-decision.md` (the funnel and slot map) and
`docs/content-architecture.md` (the build-ready layout, type steps, and the two
canon registers). Every line here is approved. Where a line depends on a fact or
a sign-off outside this copy pass, it is flagged as verify-pending, which is a
check on the underlying fact, not on the wording.

Canon in force: customer never guest, no em dashes, sentence case, declarative
over aspirational, no performed conviction, we voice. No address, neighborhood,
or property identifier until the lease is signed; the site says Austin, Texas.
No daypart code names on any external surface; band labels are descriptive. No
financials on the surface; the amount, the structure, and the returns live in
the briefing.

Links: the only two links in the copy are the header affordance (A1) and the
model soft link (A2), both pointing at the in-page `#ask` anchor. The form (A3)
is the terminal surface. There are no external URLs anywhere in the copy.

Eyebrows are semantic, no numbers (settled at the copy layer; the fold removed
the standalone concept eyebrow).

## 2. Why this

Ground: Plum Ink. Theme: dinner. The one section allowed to run long. Oversized
선 behind the text at low opacity.

**Eyebrow:** Why this

**Body:**

> Every good restaurant has a Renata. She's the floor manager, and on a full Friday she's the reason the room works. She's the one who knows the couple at table nine are there for a promotion, not the anniversary the reservation says, because the husband mentioned it at the bar a month ago. Which regulars never call ahead, and never need to. Which table wants to be left alone. Whose name to have ready at the door next time.
>
> One day she leaves. There's always a better offer across town.
>
> The owners tell each other they'll be fine. There's a reservation book, a point of sale, a training binder. What they don't say is that the facts are walking out in her coat. Next month that couple comes back to a room that's forgotten them.

**Pull-line** (Display, top voice):

> A restaurant is a memory business. Most forget.

**Blockquote** (Headline, one step below):

> The restaurant remembered nothing.

**Body, continued:**

> Three things in a restaurant compound. The organization, whose system keeps what the team learns. The customer, whose relationship deepens with every visit. The brand, whose name travels further with every night. Everything else a good competitor copies in a season.
>
> We're building the restaurant where a Renata can leave and the table is still held, the promotion still remembered. The knowledge was never only in her head.

**Approved.**
