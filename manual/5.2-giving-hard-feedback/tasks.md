# 5.2 Giving hard feedback: the work

The book's hard conversation (pp. 391 to 395): be an explorer, not a lecturer; open with a neutral question or a specific observation owned as your perception; cycle until you share an account of the problem; only then solve. At Sŏn this chunk also settles what the book leaves to the manager's discretion: where such a conversation may happen, whether it leaves a record, and how "blame the process" (WP p. 17) decides what is said to a person at all.

### 5.2.5 Decide the form of the hard conversation: the two openings, the cycle, the last resort, and what it never concludes with
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Hiring and training
- Book: pp. 392 to 395 (a neutral open question; if denied, a specific empathetic observation owned as perception; cycle; the plain statement as last resort; people are more self-aware than you expect)
- Default assumption (WP p. 19): develop rather than judge; no surprises. Silent on form
- Depends on: 5.1.5, 5.1.7, 5.2.1
- Done when: the form is written in the house's words: the first opening (a neutral question about a specific recent service), the second (a situation-behavior-impact observation citing a dated record, owned as the lead's perception), the cycle between them, the last resort (the lead's plain impression with an example), and the list of what the conversation never concludes with (candidates: a verdict, a label, a prediction about the person's future, a mention of pay, a mention of any count); it states the sequence "shared account first, solutions second" and that the person's own proposal is asked for before the lead's; agreed by both seated founders
- Replaces old items: 17tn048qcjd (the form half), 86akh67y5 (the hard-feedback half)

### 5.2.9 Decide the failure types and the response to each, including a judgment call inside someone's range that went wrong
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Hiring and training
- Book: pp. 393 to 394 (a delivery issue is raised as a specific observation with an offer of help)
- Default assumption (WP pp. 13, 14, 17, 26, stated outright): the server resolves within their authorized judgment and generosity range, the shift close captures it, the lead reviews, the process is updated (WP p. 13); identify the failure mode before intervening at the people level (WP p. 14); the first question is what allowed this, not who failed (WP p. 17); comps are a tool to be used, not minimized (WP p. 26)
- Depends on: 2.3.8, 1.2.3, 3.3.17, 5.2.1
- Done when: the types are named in the house's terms (candidates: a basic failure, a known process not followed; a complex failure, several factors and no single wrong step; an intelligent failure, a judgment call inside someone's range that did not work) and the response to each is written: which get a process fix only, which reach the person and only after what (candidate: a basic failure reaches the person as a hypothesis only when it recurs on the same role after the fix), and that a judgment call inside the range is never corrected as an error; whether the types are ever counted by person (candidate: never; by type per period only); agreed by both seated founders, with the kitchen's version pending agreement from the chef partner
- Replaces old items: 17tn048qcjf (the types half), 86akht34y (the discretion half)
