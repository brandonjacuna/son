# 3.1 Recruiting: the work

The book's recruiting section (pp. 167 to 192) covers why hiring has to be rigorous at every level, how a pipeline is built and fed, what a role needs settled before it opens, referrals and screening, and how leaders are found. The white paper commits Sŏn to a specific hiring system (WP pp. 17 to 18) that several of these tasks turn into policy before the first candidate sees it.

Work order, not book order: the first hires are the two operating leads, so the leadership decisions sit early, and the frontline pipeline follows. A "seat" here means one position in Sŏn's structure, as in 2.1 and 4.1. A "read" means one thing an interview scores.
