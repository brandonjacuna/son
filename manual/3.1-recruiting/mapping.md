## 3. What was dropped and why

Three kinds of material left the working manual. First, everything resting on the brand guidelines or the experiential deck: the photographer-selection form used as a seat-sheet template, the content register and its rules for candidate-facing copy, the voice rules and binary copy tests, the forbidden-word list for postings and correspondence, the Korean-terminology display rules, the canon wording of the non-negotiables, floor-protocol rules quoted as hiring criteria, the uniform and role-naming system, the named untrainable qualities in their canon framing, and the canon rationale for the candidate pulse. Second, program machinery: the Recommended, Founder-gated, Chef-gated, and Team-filled marks and the marks register; position and item numbering; readiness rows and their pass or fail mechanics; session routings, amendment histories, and "field 15" style references; the "founder load modules" tracking scheme; the document-methodology gate; and the field-level instrument specifications. Third, vocabulary and framing: daypart code names (service periods are referred to by time of day), "team home," "reach read," and every "ratify" or "rule" verdict, now stated as decisions with options. Nothing that survives on white-paper or book grounds was removed: the four reads, the refusal of fit and typology scoring, the seat classes, the paid practical and its supervision rule, the candidate content rule, the mutual commitments, the recruiting seat question, the founders' stage and its retirement, both chef-search branches, the pre-room final round, and the succession rule all remain, restated as decisions and options.
