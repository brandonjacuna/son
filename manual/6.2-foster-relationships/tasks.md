# 6.2 Foster relationships: the work

Every decision, action, and deliverable this section of the book calls for, in the order they would naturally be done. Reasoning and options are in `considerations.md`; the book's guidance is in `book.md`. "WP p. N" is the white paper. This is a Conclusion chunk: the book's relationships (with your manager, your peers, the founders) have no direct match at Sŏn, where the founders have no manager and the leads have no one above them but the founders. The tasks turn each relationship into what the house can build and leave the rest to the people in it; nothing here describes how any founder relates to anyone as a person. The founders' working-with-me exchange is 1.1; their rule for hard things is 1.2; how they decide is 2.1.13; their rhythm is 2.3.4; their norms for working apart are 4.6.3.
