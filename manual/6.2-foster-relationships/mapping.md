## 3. What was dropped and why

No old item in this table was dropped whole. What left the working manual: first, the verdicts, every "ratify" bundle and recorded resolution, now decisions with the old answer as one option (the three relationship mechanisms, the refusal of the 1:1 and the offsite for a lead with a founder, the joint two-lead read of a concern about a founder, the inward-first rule). Second, anything about a founder as a person: how the partners treat each other, a founder's feedback style, a founder's values, and a founder's friendships, all of which are the founders' own and are recorded nowhere. Third, brand-dependent material: canon's non-negotiables as the wording of the founder stage's read and of the company's intent. Fourth, program machinery: position numbers, prompt letters as destinations, session cross-references, and the marks. Coined terms ("the why block," "the change line," "the two-kinds rule") are replaced by plain words: the why session, the change channel, and the floor rule on what may be said live during a service.
