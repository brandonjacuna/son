### 0.1 Get counsel's list of the founders' and leads' legal responsibilities as employers and managers in Texas
- Type: Action
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: p. 31 (you are aware of your legal responsibilities as a manager in the jurisdiction in which you operate)
- Default assumption: None; the white paper routes compliance logging, food safety, and traceability through the stack (WP pp. 10, 21) and is silent on the founders' obligations as employers. The labor-law exposure of compensation built into the menu price is 5.5's question and also goes to counsel
- Depends on: None
- Done when: a short list from employment counsel exists naming what a founder and a lead must know and do as employers and managers in Texas, and which items the leads must be trained on before they manage anyone; the list is handed to 3.3 (lead onboarding) and 5.9 (managing managers); nothing in this manual asserts a legal position
- Replaces old items: None (the old page flagged it to counsel and left it there)

### 0.3 Decide whether the partners hold the checklist's basics for each other
- Type: Decision

- Book: p. 32 (goals agreed with your own manager; 1:1s with your own manager; context on priorities from above)

- Depends on: 0.2
- Done when: the founders have stated whether they stand in as each other's manager for goals agreed each quarter, a standing partner conversation, and priorities shared in writing (options in `considerations.md` section 4); the rule is stated in one sentence a third partner can read on day one; the rhythm itself is handed to 2.3 to design and the goals cycle to 2.2; agreed by both seated founders
