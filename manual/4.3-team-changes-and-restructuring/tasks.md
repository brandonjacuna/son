# 4.3 Team changes and restructuring: the work

Every decision, action, and deliverable this section of the book calls for, in the order they would naturally be done. Reasoning and options are in `considerations.md`; the book's guidance is in `book.md`. "WP p. N" is the white paper. A deliverable marked "Repeatable" is one others will later produce for themselves; building it with Brandon is also how the process gets built for everyone else, and each such deliverable has a paired kit task.

Vocabulary, in plain words: a **structure change** is any change to seats, leadership lines, domains, or who reports to whom, recorded on the structure change entry (4.1.33). A **departure read** is what the house reads in the week someone leaves, before and instead of treating the seat as a vacancy to fill. The **ready-now pool** is the white paper's list of people who have completed the next track and are waiting for a seat (WP p. 11). The failure modes and the evidence line are as 4.2 defines them.

### 4.3.8 Decide the departure read: what is read in the week someone leaves, and when the read closes

- Depends on: 3.1.38, 4.2.7, 4.1.15, 4.3.14

### 4.3.12 Decide how a domain or a service period that repeats is read, before any hiring question
- Type: Decision

- Default assumption (WP p. 17): "universal problems are not people problems. They are design problems"; (WP p. 34) a period added without its own reason becomes a worse version of dinner
- Depends on: 4.3.11, 4.2.10, 2.1.15, 2.2.34
