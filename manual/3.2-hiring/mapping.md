## 3. What was dropped and why

No old item is dropped outright; every one either lands in a 3.2 task or routes to the chunk that owns it. What left the manual is material inside the items and the page. First, everything drawn from the brand guidelines and experiential deck: the canon citations behind the non-negotiables, nunchi and jeong as named concepts in the third read, the canon framing of felt knowledge, the voice and correspondence rules, the "photographer selection" analogue, the performance-refusal language, and the rule against spectacle during dinner (the creator-access concern survives on WP p. 31). Second, program machinery: the Recommended, Founder-gated, and Chef-gated marks on every position; the marks register and readiness-row identifiers; session and carryover cross-references; the sixteen-field numbering of the seat sheet and the ten-entry list for the person page; and the carryovers' framing of Session 5 positions as settled, which are reopened here as Brandon's decisions (who decides, the committee, the founders' stage, what a declined candidate is told). Third, the private vocabulary ("the record," "the stack," "the four reads" as fixed) is replaced with plain words, and the number of reads is his to set in 3.2.2. Nothing that stands on the white paper or the book was removed: the paid practical, the gates-not-dates calendar, trained interviewers before a platform exists, the language promise, the dissent line in place of a committee, reference consent, the calibration read, and the internal loop all remain, as decisions with options.
