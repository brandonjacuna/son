# 4.4 (Re)building the team: the work

Every decision, action, and deliverable this section of the book calls for, in the order they would naturally be done. Reasoning and options are in `considerations.md`; the book's guidance is in `book.md`. "WP p. N" is the white paper. A deliverable marked "Repeatable" is one others will later produce for themselves; building it with Brandon is also how the process gets built for everyone else, and each such deliverable has a paired kit task.

Vocabulary, in plain words: a **career conversation** is the book's one-off conversation between a lead and a person about that person's career and what they want next (pp. 294 to 296). An **assignment** is a piece of work outside a seat's ordinary scope, handed to a person for a bounded time (the book's second kind of delegation, p. 297). A **trapdoor** is a decision that is hard to reverse once made (p. 300). A **named owner** is the one person accountable for an assignment or a goal getting done; the book's term is DRI, directly responsible individual (p. 298). The decision-rights register is 2.2.4's; the check-in and person page are 2.3's.
