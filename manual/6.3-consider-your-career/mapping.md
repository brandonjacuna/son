## 3. What was dropped and why

No old item in this table was dropped whole. What left the working manual: first, the verdicts, every "ratify" bundle and recorded resolution, now decisions with the old answer as one option (the path as a sequence of hand-overs, the private career document, no founder review under any name, the three-part absorber, continuity-only departure, the refusal of the stepping-back rhythm). Second, anything about a founder's own path, pay, or standing as a person: where a path leads, pay and equity, and any reading of a founder's performance; these belong to each founder and to the operating agreement. Third, program machinery: the six principles' marks, readiness rows, position and question numbers, session cross-references, and the marks register. Coined terms ("the absorber," "the ledger," "prompt H") are replaced by plain words: what absorbs a founder's failure, the founder seat inventory, and each founder's own account of what being under load looks like.
