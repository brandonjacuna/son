# 6.3 Consider your career: the work

Every decision, action, and deliverable this section of the book calls for, in the order they would naturally be done. Reasoning and options are in `considerations.md`; the book's guidance is in `book.md`. "WP p. N" is the white paper. This is the book's last section and the last chunk of the manual: a founder's own path, and the founders' standing in a house that reads everyone else. The answers belong to each founder. These tasks prepare the conversation and the house's side of it; nothing here states where any founder's path leads, what they are good at, what they enjoy, or what they are paid. The team's path is built elsewhere (4.1.7, 4.1.11, 3.1.40, 4.4.3, 5.6) and is not rebuilt here.
