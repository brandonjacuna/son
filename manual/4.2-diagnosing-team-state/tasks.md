# 4.2 Diagnosing team state: the work

Every decision, action, and deliverable this section of the book calls for, in the order they would naturally be done. Reasoning and options are in `considerations.md`; the book's guidance is in `book.md`. "WP p. N" is the white paper. A deliverable marked "Repeatable" is one others will later produce for themselves; building it with Brandon is also how the process gets built for everyone else, and each such deliverable has a paired kit task.

Vocabulary, in plain words: a **failure mode** is one of the five named conditions the white paper says to identify "before intervening at the people level" (WP p. 14): tension slack, node overload, sequence corruption, skill invisibility, cooling failure. An **evidence line** is one written observation that a mode is present on some part of the house, with its source. The **diagnostic** is the whole practice of reading the house's state, whatever frame 4.2.3 settles on. Seat, domain, strand, leadership line, and designation are as 4.1 defines them; the check-in, person page, leads' review, house review, gate review, and mechanism reset are as 2.3 defines them.

### 4.2.6 Decide the evidence each mode is read from, and what is never evidence
