## 3. What was dropped and why

No old item in this table was dropped whole. What left the working manual: first, the verdicts, every "ratify" bundle and every proposed answer recorded in comments, now decisions with the old answer as one option (the refusal of "discipline," the three-reader design, the refused carriers, the owning-versus-running line, the floor rule). Each founder writes their own on the structure in 6.1.13 and 6.1.14. Third, brand-dependent material: the specialist brand-build seats in 17tn048qr40, and the findings that read the white paper against the brand documents. Fourth, program machinery: the ledger's marks, position numbers, readiness rows, the monthly knowledge-transfer threshold, the strengths-dependency feed, session cross-references, and the marks register. Coined terms from the old page ("the ledger," "the read on the center," "prompt H," "the stack" where unexplained) are replaced by plain words: the founder seat inventory, how a founder's load is read, the founder's own account of what being under load looks like, and the scheduling and records system.
