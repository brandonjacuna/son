## 3. What was dropped and why

No old item in this table was dropped whole. What left the working manual is framing and machinery. First, the verdicts: every "ratify" bundle and every "refused" or "absorbed" ruling is now a decision with the old answer as one option and its reasoning (a six-month interval, the check-in holder as review holder, no rating, no peer input, no calibration committee, the candor count on the holder). Second, program machinery: readiness rows 155 to 168, position numbers and session cross-references, carryover routing notes, the marks register, and the retired marks. Third, brand-dependent material: citations to the excluded brand documents, including a canon-sourced tension about how two service concepts relate to tenure, and findings that read the white paper against those documents. Fourth, the figure in the session task's text, since financials are not a source. Coined terms from the old page ("the stack," "the candor line" as a proper noun, "the advocate's record") are replaced by plain words or defined where they appear in considerations.
