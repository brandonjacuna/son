# 5.9 Managing managers: the work

The book's short section on the shift from managing people to managing the people who manage them (pp. 449 to 454), turned into what Sŏn must decide and build. At Sŏn the "managers" are the two leads, the kitchen's station leads under the chef partner, and later any lead of a second property; the people managing them are the founders, each holding one lead's monthly conversation (2.2.5, 2.3.14). Most of the policy here is promised to a lead at their first interview, so it sits before the first hire; the builds follow; the practice is after opening. A deliverable marked "Repeatable" is one others will later produce for themselves, and each has a paired kit task.
