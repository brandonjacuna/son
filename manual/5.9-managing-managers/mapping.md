## 3. What was dropped and why

No old item owned here was dropped whole. What left the working manual is framing and machinery. First, the verdicts: every "ratify" bundle and every "refused" or "not adopted" ruling (no skip level, no action items, no time-on-the-ground block, no leadership program, the word "manager" retired) is now a decision with the old answer as one option and its reasoning. Second, program machinery: session and position citations, readiness rows, decision-rights entry numbers, instrument numbers, and the marks. Third, brand material: any argument that treated a vocabulary choice or a sentence to a lead as a brand surface. The old program's coined terms ("node overload wearing a taste," "the extreme coach's tell," "the permanent room holder as a refused carrier") are replaced with plain words where the idea survives: a standard held only in one person's head, a lead scheduling too many check-ins, one person always given the same designation.
