## 3. What was dropped and why

No item was dropped whole from this chunk's twenty-two. Three kinds of material left them. First, brand-dependent material: the formal writing-rule question for recruiting surfaces, the untranslated-terminology question, brand rules applied to internal surfaces and to a peer's wording, and the brand principle that framed the phone voice as concealment. Second, pre-decided tool binding: the four named vendor products and their detailed configuration as a given; the policy questions under them (a chat tool, recognition without rankings, the channel's rules, a disclosing phone voice) survive as decisions. Third, program machinery: the architecture page, the ledger bindings, decision-rights entry numbering, readiness-row numbers, instrument IDs, the marks, and session and finding cross-references.
