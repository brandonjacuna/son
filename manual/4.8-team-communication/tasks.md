# 4.8 Team communication: the work

The book's closing note on the chapter (pp. 360 to 362): set expectations, share the same information with everyone, build an environment for open exchange; pass information down with framing; keep a snippets document; think each day about what to tell people. The book assumes a desk-based team reached by meeting, email, and chat. Sŏn's team is reached in short bursts on a phone before and after shifts, in more than one language, and the channels, the transparency line, and the destinations already exist upstream (2.2.6, 2.2.11, 2.2.12, 4.5.11, 4.5.17). This chunk owns the language list, the record rule, the pass-down, the recognition and feedback rules, the phone, the exit conversation, and the review of whether anything actually reached anyone.

### 4.8.5 Decide the record rule: one home per kind of message, written before spoken for a change, and a chat message is never the record
