# 4.5 Creating the team environment: the work

The book builds a team's fabric through two rituals, offsites and meetings (pp. 304 to 330). Most of Sŏn's standing structures are already decided upstream (the brief, the close, the check-in, the leads' review, the house review, the reset); this chunk decides how people are treated inside those rooms, what the house does that no review does, and what a person on the floor can count on.

- Type: Action
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: pp. 304 to 305 (rituals and common practices weave the fabric of a team; offsites and meetings are the two she relies on)

- Depends on: 2.2.29, 2.3.7, 2.3.8, 2.3.9, 2.3.13, 4.1.2, 4.1.19
