# 5.10 Managing out, firing, and layoffs: the work

The book's process for the departures a company causes (pp. 454 to 466, checklist pp. 481 to 482), turned into what Sŏn must decide and build: how employment ends, who decides, on what, the conversation, the day, pay, the reduction that is not a departure, and what the house does when something bad happens to a person. The policy is what a candidate reads and what counsel will ask about, so most of it sits before the first hire; the practice is after opening. 5.8.14 hands this chunk a closed package; 5.2.2 hands it a serious-breach report; 4.3 owns what the house reads around a departure and 4.8.14 the exit conversation. This chunk builds the exit itself and references the rest.
