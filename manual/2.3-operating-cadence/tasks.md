# 2.3 Operating cadence: the work

Every decision, action, and deliverable this section of the book calls for, in the order they would naturally be done. Reasoning and options are in `considerations.md`; the book's guidance is in `book.md`. "WP p. N" is the white paper. A deliverable marked "Repeatable" is one others will later produce for themselves; building it with Brandon is also how the process gets built for everyone else, and each such deliverable has a paired kit task.

Plain words used throughout, each defined once: the **shift brief** is the meeting before a service opens and the **shift close** is what happens after the last table leaves (WP p. 26 calls this "beginning of shift, prepare for the customer... end of shift, reflect"); the **check-in** is the standing conversation between a person and the lead of their domain, Sŏn's form of her 1:1; the **person page** is the record of those conversations that the person can read; the **leads' review** is the weekly meeting of the two leads; the **partners' review** is the weekly meeting of the founders; the **house review** is the quarterly review of the whole restaurant, her QBR; the **gate review** is the review that decides whether the next service period opens; the **mechanism reset** is the periodic re-examination of every rhythm on the calendar; the **cadence calendar** is the one table of every rhythm, its owner, and its record.

### 2.3.1 Decide whether Sŏn runs one cadence with two phases or two rhythms that coexist
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: p. 136 (the cadence mirrors the company cadence and is agreed up front; have one but do not get stuck in it)
- Default assumption: (WP pp. 05, 26, 36) the white paper describes two tempos without naming them: a build that runs on milestones and proof (the phased opening, dinner alone for at least a quarter, later periods once dinner is steady) and a service day whose unit is the shift. The build does not end at opening; each later period is built while the one before it runs. No default on whether that is one cadence or two
- Depends on: None
- Done when: the founders have said whether the pre-opening build and the running restaurant share one calendar with two phases, or run two rhythms that coexist until the last service period opens, and have named what the two share (the partners' review, the goal cycle, the gate review, the brand cadence) and what they do not (the daily unit, the leads' review, the season); agreed by both seated founders
- Replaces old items: 86akh672x (the shape half)
