# 5.8 Managing low performers: the work

The book's process for the person whose work is not holding (pp. 432 to 448, templates pp. 474 to 482), turned into what Sŏn must decide and build. The policy here is what a candidate relies on and what counsel will ask about, so most decisions sit before the first hire; the practice is after opening. A serious act is not this chunk's: 5.2.2 draws that line and 5.10 holds what follows.

### 5.8.1 Read what 5.1, 5.2, 3.3, 3.4, 4.2, and 4.3 already settle for work that is not holding, and note the gaps
- Type: Action
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: p. 432 (when one-off feedback becomes a pattern, you are managing a low performer; the manager diagnoses skill, will, or wrong job)
- Default assumption (WP p. 14): "before intervening at the people level, identify which failure mode is actually present"
- Depends on: 5.1.9, 5.2.2, 5.2.4, 5.2.8, 3.3.12, 3.4.5, 3.4.6, 4.2.5, 4.2.6, 4.2.10, 4.3.7, 4.3.12, 4.4.2

- Replaces old items: None
