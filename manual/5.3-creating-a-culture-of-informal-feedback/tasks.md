# 5.3 Creating a culture of informal feedback: the work

The book's point (pp. 395 to 398): companies build the formal review and neglect the everyday flow of observations between colleagues, and then the review feels destabilizing because no one knows where they stand. The way to build the flow is for leaders to model it and ask for it. At Sŏn the white paper already promises feedback in both directions and a channel that closes the loop (WP pp. 19, 22); this chunk decides what "feedback" means here, which direction runs on which surface, and what the founders do first.

### 5.3.6 Decide the bounds on peer-to-peer feedback

### 5.3.8 Decide what a recognition that is really a correction gets, and who tells its writer
