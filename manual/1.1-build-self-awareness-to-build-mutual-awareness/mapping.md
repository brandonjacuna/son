## 3. What was dropped and why

Three kinds of material left the working manual. First, brand-dependent material: the canon's non-negotiables and its citations in the trust argument; the "master register" as the tone authority for the document; the brand document's account of the chef seat that made the "two accounts" conflict; the brand's culinary philosophy session as a link in the kitchen chain; and "both canon versions" in a lead's pre-reads. Second, program machinery: the Recommended, Founder-gated, Chef-gated, and Team-filled marks; the document-methodology gate on the instrument and the lead issuance; Session 16's load ledger, row numbers, protection-order appendices, and stack-placed issuance conditions; the partners' tracking page and gate-review rows; session routings; and the profile-allowlist reconciliation. Third, verdicts: every "ratify" position (the grid's team use, the one spine for three founders, the issued lead version) is now an option with what it commits Sŏn to. Nothing that survives without brand support was removed: the eleven prompts, the three exercises with their additions, the refusals, the lead version, the chef timing question, the knowledge-transfer list, and the boundary that no one writes a founder's answers all remain.
