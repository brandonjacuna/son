## 9. So what for Sŏn

**Demand.** Metro alcohol receipts are +3.8% YoY for Jun to Aug 2026, but venues open in both years are flat (-0.1%) and only 41% of them grew. Growth is coming from new venues taking share, not from rising spend per venue. Hubs (+4.8%) are running slightly ahead of off-hub clusters (+3.0%).

**Direct comp.** Oseyo's alcohol receipts stepped up from about $25K to $35K a month in mid-2025 to $100K to $120K a month since December 2025 ($343,804 for Jun to Aug 2026 vs $77,465 a year earlier, +344%). The only direct comp is running at roughly four times last year's alcohol volume.

**Home zone.** The St. Elmo cluster (East Congress / Sweetbriar / West Congress) is -29.1% YoY, and the decline sits in three large sports and entertainment venues: Pins Mechanical at 4323 S Congress (0.17 mi, -40%), Twin Peaks at 701 E Stassney (1.1 mi, -83%), and Ojos Locos at 901 Little Texas Ln (1.3 mi, -36%). Bar-forward venues next to the site are growing: Skeleton Key at 4720 S Congress (+84%) and Fortune Teller at 5604 S Congress (+58%). Two new food trucks registered within 2 miles this week.

**Cost and labor.** Food away from home prices in the South are +2.9% YoY (Aug 2026). Texas service firms report wage pressure (18.8) running about twice the pace of selling-price increases (8.9) on the Dallas Fed index, which squeezes margins. Austin leisure and hospitality employment is 156.0k, +2.4% YoY (Aug 2026, preliminary), with metro unemployment at 4.0% (Jul 2026).

**Coming up.** ACL Weekend 1 (Oct 2 to 4) and Weekend 2 (Oct 9 to 11) at Zilker, UT home games Oct 17, 24, and 31, and the F1 United States Grand Prix Oct 22 to 25, which shares a weekend with Texas vs Ole Miss.

**Recommended actions**
- Visit Oseyo this month and document what changed since December 2025: menu, bar program, hours, and pricing.
- Add Twin Peaks (701 E Stassney) to the Watchlist as At-risk and confirm its status with a second source.
- Study Skeleton Key and Fortune Teller as the evidence of bar demand within a mile of 207 E St. Elmo Rd.
- Build the opening labor plan against wage growth outpacing menu price growth.
