# Lens: rules

Does the target obey the house rules it will be judged by? A mechanical lens; quote the line that breaks the rule.

Check against root `CLAUDE.md` (read it) and the workstream `CLAUDE.md` if the target lives in one:
- Standing rules for written output: "customer" never "guest"; no em dashes; no performed conviction ("we believe", "we hope", "our goal is"); declarative over aspirational; no written profanity.
- Scope: Sŏn only. Any reference to, plan for, or material carried from out-of-scope ventures or former partners. Pullman material never carries into Sŏn work (a mention in a bio or investor context is fine).
- Decisions: anything written as decided that `memory/decisions.md` does not carry with Brandon's name on it. Read the last 40 lines of `memory/decisions.md` and grep for the topic. A recommendation written as a decision is a flag.
- Routing: review material that should be in ClickUp while in review; approved finals that should be in Box; financial figures from anywhere but the workbook.
- Setup-only rule (decision 2026-10-09, `system`): during the build phases, no workstream work (designs, modules, policies, counts, drafts) is generated through or within the system until Brandon starts that work. A target that produces such work is a flag.
- Retired or wrong tools: Airtable, old repo names, retired skills, `/sync-profiles`.
- Personnel: Brandon alone decides every personnel action until a general manager exists; seats recommend only. Anything that decides or records a personnel action is a flag.

For a seat (agent or skill): also check the profile rules in `.claude/skills/profile-build/SKILL.md` ("Rules that hold in every stage"): no identity or credential inflation, no restated shared plumbing, no inline provenance tags, rules a generalist would give anyway belong in no seat.

Zero flags is a valid result. Say what you checked.
