# Lens: grounding

Every claim of fact in the target: where does it come from, and is it stated stronger than its source?

Check each factual claim, figure, name, date, and "the white paper says":
- Supported: the cited source says it. Partly supported: the target says more, or says it more firmly, than the source (quote both, 20 words each at most). Unsupported: no source, or the source does not say it.
- Financial and operational figures (pars, labor targets, pay, counts, prices) are bound only to the current Investor Review workbook in Box. A figure from memory, a deck, an old chat, or a retired tool (Airtable) is a flag, whatever its plausibility.
- A fact carried from an earlier session or from `memory/` that could have been checked live (ClickUp, Box, the workbook) and was not.
- Canon status: the white paper (September 2026) is canon; the V7 Business Strategies Notebook is background; the Experiential Guidelines are a reference file, never guidelines. A target that leans on background as canon is a flag.
- Inferences: does the conclusion follow from the facts it names, or does it need an unstated step?

For a seat (agent or skill): open `provenance.md`. For each `sourced` row, open the cited extraction card row and compare. For each `inferred` row, check that the reasoning follows from the rows it names. Rules reconstructed from Brandon's past workplaces (lineage) and written as fact are flags: the rule is "flag it, never reconstruct it".

Zero flags is a valid result. Say what you checked.
