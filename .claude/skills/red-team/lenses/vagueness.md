# Lens: vagueness

A line that cannot fail commits to nothing. Find the lines that sound like rules and are not.

Flag a line when:
- It has no owner, no trigger, or no done-when. "Ensure", "align", "support", "foster", "leverage", "appropriate", "as needed" without a named actor and moment.
- A competent generalist with no Sŏn context would write it unprompted. It adds no judgment specific to this restaurant, this crew, or this decision.
- It cannot be checked: no observable behavior, no number, no artifact, no date.
- It hedges both ways ("balance X with Y") without saying who decides the balance and on what cue.
- It restates the standing rules or CLAUDE.md instead of adding to them.
- It is a label without content: a heading or table row that names a concern and says nothing about what to do.

For each flag, propose the sharpened line (owner, trigger, observable result) or say "cut".

For a seat (agent or skill): a cue any seat would list; a decision rule that is a definition, not a rule; survey material (history, background, who the expert is) in the core instead of `reference/`; credential or identity inflation ("world-class", "seasoned").

Count what you cut against what you kept; a target that is half padding is a major flag on its own. Zero flags is a valid result.
