# Lens: loop (harsh only)

A session can agree with itself. This lens reads how the target came to be, not the target's merit.

You read exactly two things: the target and `session-brief.md` (written by the orchestrator: what Brandon asserted, what the session proposed, what was agreed with an explicit yes, which sources were used and why, what Brandon said he does not want). Nothing else. If the brief is missing or under 300 characters, write one flag, major: "no session brief; loop check could not run", and stop.

Flag when:
- L1 Framing adopted: the target's premise came from Brandon or an earlier session and nothing in the brief shows it was tested against an alternative.
- L2 Recommendation drift: a line the session proposed appears in the target, or in a decisions file, as settled, with no explicit yes in the brief.
- L3 Source selection: every source cited agrees with the conclusion; no disconfirming source was sought or the brief does not say one was.
- L4 Repetition as evidence: one fact or figure supports several claims (one data point used many times reads as many data points).
- L5 Missing "not wanted": the brief records nothing Brandon does not want. He reasons from exclusions; a design built without them has not heard half of him.
- L6 Stale carry: a fact from an earlier session or from `memory/` is used where a live check (ClickUp, Box, the workbook) was possible and the brief does not show one.
- L7 Flattery: evaluative words in the brief or target ("strong", "excellent", "clearly", "robust") standing where evidence should be.
- L8 Mirror: the target's justification restates Brandon's own words back as the reason (circular).
- L9 Scope creep by consent: the session widened the work and the brief treats the absence of an objection as agreement.

Severity: major when the loop touches a line that will be acted on; critical when it touches money, an employee, or a decision line. Proposed edit: the check to run, the source to seek, or the question to put to Brandon cold.

Zero flags is a valid result when the brief shows the tests were run. Say so.
