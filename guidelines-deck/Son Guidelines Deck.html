02 — Cultural frameworks 선
Complete · Tier 1

Three frameworks with veto power over everything downstream.

The authority

When a decision violates a framework, the framework wins. Cost, aesthetics, and photography do not change that.

These are not references or themes. They are operational constraints that govern at Tier 1.

자연미

Jaeyeonmi

Beauty of the natural

Nothing conceals what it is, where it came from, or how long it has been here.

Applies to every material, every surface, every object, every copy surface, every photograph.

Prohibited
  • Faux textures and simulated foil
  • Grain or patina applied in post
  • Drawn imitations of a real material
Required
  • Materials chosen for their actual nature, not their appearance
  • Evidence of time read as a feature, not a maintenance problem
여백의 미

Ma · Yubaek-ui-mi

Beauty of meaningful negative space

Empty space is active, held, intentional. Empty is a specification, not a default.

Surface-and-Zone Baseline Document of record: ClickUp 2ky45bmy-15773.

Prohibited
  • Filling a surface because it reads unfinished
  • Decorative elements added to a sparse surface
Required
  • Space protected against the constant pressure to fill it
  • Sparse treated as correct, the empty surface treated as a decision
맛

Mahk

Taste as memory

Taste is relational. A dish carries memory. The work is a flavor specific enough to become one.

A generic experience produces Mahk for no one.

Prohibited
  • A perfect flavor profile pointed at no one in particular
  • The generic standing in for the specific
Required
  • Specificity as the mechanism of memory
  • The one thing no other sentence could name
A named forbidden category

The bent spoon.

A thing the customer already knows how to receive, delivered without intention, producing no pause and no memory.

The bent spoon is the design target. Objects, moments, service behaviors, and copy specific enough to require the customer to stop, consider, and be present.

Applies across every discipline.

03 — Brand philosophy 선
Complete

The obligation is to build conditions for agency.

Playground Philosophy

The operational obligation across every concept and daypart

A playground is a structured space with legible rules and open-ended results. Clear enough to feel safe. Open enough to stay.

The test: does this build or foreclose conditions for agency?

Prohibited
  • Rules so tight the result is foreclosed
  • Openness so loose the space stops feeling safe
Required
  • Conditions for agency for customers, founders, and employees alike
  • Structure legible enough to be trusted, open enough to be inhabited
The human expression at the floor level

Jeong and Nunchi.

정

Jeong

A deep affectionate bond developed through shared meaningful time. Accumulated relationship, not performed welcome. It cannot be scripted. It requires time, repetition, and memory.

눈치

Nunchi

The intuitive reading of what someone needs before they ask. Not diagnostic inquiry. Not a checklist. Developed through mastery, exercised through judgment.

A substantive distinction

This is not omotenashi. The Japanese model anticipates and perfects. Jeong accumulates and Nunchi reads.

One is a standard of service. The other is a relationship that remembers.

12 — Photography 선
Complete

Photographs are memories, not documents.

Photography · the philosophy
People in every frame. Found light only. Decentered, closer than comfortable. Grain preserved, never applied.
선
Three contexts

The room, the table, the people. People animate all three.

A · the room

Space, atmosphere, time of day. People as the animating evidence.

B · the table

Food in the context of consumption or service. Vessel and hands always present. Food alone is prohibited.

C · the people

Portraits and relationships. No role photography. No subject as a backdrop.

Across all three: no image where the subject has adjusted posture, expression, or gaze in response to the camera.

Light and composition

The camera adapts to the room. The room is never lit for the camera.

Light

Window light, candlelight, firelight, installed 2700K, ambient outdoor glow. Found sources only.

No strobe, flash, ring light, softbox, beauty dish, reflector, or any crew-introduced source.

Composition · three rules
  • Decentered. The primary element at or outside the inner-thirds intersection.
  • Imperfect. Horizon off level. Motion blur and grain allowed.
  • Closer than comfortable. Move forward until something important is cropped.
Post-production and AI

Capture geometry is the limit.

Grade warm: skin to Onggi, shadows to Aubergine, highlights holding in Parchment and Bone, outdoor greens in Jade and Peacock. Grain at ISO 800 to 3200 is a specification, kept. Cooling and blue shadows are prohibited.

PermittedNoise reduction, lens and perspective correction within actual capture geometry, under disclosure.
