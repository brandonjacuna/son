## 4\. The separation protocol

### 4.1 What it is called, and the three kinds

This is the first description on the record of the house ending anyone's employment, and it is written in the record's terms and not hers. A departure is a tension re-establishment (WP p.9). The building is blamed first (WP p.17). The web loses tension and the adjacent nodes compensate at cost (WP p.8). Dignity is protected at every turn (WP p.26). Nothing conceals what it is (Canon 1.0, section 01). No disposable people (Canon 1.0, section 01; Deck 3.0, p.4). The house's words for this on every surface are "departure," "leaving," "the last day," "the decision about staying," and "a reduction." Her words, fire, terminate, let go, manage out, layoff, severance, gross misconduct, appear on no house surface, on no page, and in no conversation, except where counsel requires the law's word on a document counsel writes.

Three kinds of departure, each designed end to end below.

The person's choice (4.2). The person decides to leave. This is the only departure the record describes, and the house's part is to make it clean, paid, and dignified, and to learn from it.

The house's decision (4.3, 4.4). The partners decide, as a reserved class with counsel, that a person does not stay, on one of two records and nothing else: the hand-off package after a horizon window closed not held with no move open or chosen (S14 I9), or a non-negotiable line with the flag's record and the person's words (S14 P26).

A reduction (4.5). The house changes a period, its hours, or its seats for a reason that is not about any person: a period that thins, a period that closes for a season or for good, a seat retired. Almost all of it is not a departure. Its last step can be.

What every kind shares: the departure's date as a state on the person page written by the stack; the tension-loss row, field 15, written the day the departure is known (S5 P12); the three-pass read (S9 P30); the pool read before the seat opens externally (S5 P60); coverage at the brief and nothing about the person (S11 P25); final pay as the days worked and the accrued share to the last pay date (4.7); the exit interview offered (5.4); the person's words as theirs (S11 P17); the departure record's lawful holder and retention as counsel's (14).

### 4.2 The first kind: a departure the person chooses

Saying it. A person who decides to leave says so to their check-in's holder, in person or in writing, on the clock or off it, because a person may speak of their own life whenever they choose; the house asks it of no one at any time. Nothing is required of the person before they say it, and the house does not ask why. The check-in holder records the last day as a state on the person page from the person's words and nothing else, read back before saving, in the person's language.
