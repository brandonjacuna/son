amendment 10; scratch 3h amended, not replaced). 2026-07-23.

The condition, measured per live swap at the moment of the swap: if the
card-centered line would land inside the visual viewport (24px
breathing), the confirmation centers in the held card at the isolate's
optical seat (center slightly high, 47%); otherwise it renders at the
act's position. If visualViewport is unavailable or unusable, the swap
renders at the act — degrade toward the safe case (the answer is in the
standing view regardless), never the elegant one. Load path untouched.

Measured on the built code (headless chromium, dpr 2):

- 1440x900, ask seated, live submit: centered; conf center at 0.474 of
  card height (optical seat), marginTop 227.7px, card 590px.
  Frame: desktop/0001-centered-isolate.png
- 1440x900, visualViewport overridden to undefined (fallback probe):
  act position; marginTop 410.17px == submit-top minus card padding
  (expected ~410). Frame: desktop/0002-fallback-act.png
- 390x844, standing at the submit control, live submit: centered at
  0.474 — emulation has no keyboard, the whole 644px card is in view,
  so the centered line lands in the standing view and the condition
  centers. This is the condition working, not leaking (ruled). On a
  real phone the keyboard shrinks the visual viewport at the submit
  moment and the act position fires; that path is exercised by the
  fallback probe's branch and should be eyeballed at the next device
  session. Frame: mobile/0001-standing-at-submit.png

Width boundary finding that killed the breakpoint: the card never
exceeds the layout viewport at any tested width (590px at 1440x900,
644px at 390x844) — a width breakpoint would encode a boundary that
does not exist in layout. The boundary lives in the visual viewport.
