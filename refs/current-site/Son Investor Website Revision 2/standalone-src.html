02 · Why this

In every good restaurant there is someone like Renata. On a full Friday she is the reason the room works. She is the floor manager, and she knows the couple at table nine are celebrating a promotion, not the anniversary the reservation noted, because the husband mentioned it at the bar a month ago.

Tonight is her last night. She has a better offer across town.

The owners will be fine, they tell each other. They have a reservation book, a point of sale, a training binder. What they do not say is that the few hundred facts are leaving in her coat. Next month the couple at table nine arrives to a room that has forgotten them.

What looks like a people business is a memory business, and the restaurant remembered nothing.

Only two things in a restaurant compound. The organization, when its system keeps what the team learns. And the customer, whose relationship deepens with every visit. Everything else a good competitor copies in a season.

We are building the restaurant where, the night Renata leaves, the table is still held and the promotion still remembered. The knowledge was never only in her head.
