A held content surface. Hairline border, no drop shadow, generous padding.

```jsx
<Card surface="secondary" padding="lg">
  <h3>Pyeong-sang</h3>
  <p>Granted, not found.</p>
</Card>
```

Props: `surface` (primary | secondary) · `padding` (none | sm | md | lg) · `as` (tag). Never add a colored left-border accent or shadow — those are named anti-patterns.
