# Allergy at the table

**Every mention is an allergy until the customer says otherwise.**

1. Ask: What exactly? Allergy or preference? Traces, shared equipment, oil?
2. Repeat it back.
3. Never judge severity.
4. Flag: {{bind:tool.pos.allergen_flag}}
5. Confirm with the kitchen: {{bind:workflow.kitchen_allergy_confirm}}
6. No confirmation, no fire.

**Missed one?** Stop the dish. Tell {{bind:people.allergy_escalation_contact}}. Go back to the table.
