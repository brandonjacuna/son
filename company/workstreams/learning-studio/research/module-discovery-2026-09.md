## One-screen summary

**The shape.** One program, one data model, one sky. At the center is the **trunk**: the shared core (why the house exists, what the bar is, how to learn here, how to decide, how to critique, the safety floor) plus the Foundation of every discipline, which everyone takes. Around it sit **seven disciplines**, D29's soft-locked seven with one rename proposed: service craft and movement; the room and time; flavor and perception; liquid craft; teaching and leading; decisions; and food and the kitchen (was back of house), drafted now under D30 and redeveloped by the executive chef. Each discipline is drawn as up to three constellations, and each has four **depths** defined by what a person can do, never by who needs it: Foundation (explains the why, recognizes the moment), Working (performs unaided at tempo on a full night), Deep (adapts when conditions break, diagnoses others' misses, coaches), Student (extends what the house knows, teaches it, and is proven before a panel). A **role** is a required set: named capabilities at stated depths across several disciplines, plus its gates. Job descriptions render from the required set. Cross-training is walking to a neighboring constellation; a role move is one conversation.

**What the sky shows.** Capabilities, not modules. A star is a verb someone would be proud of on a real shift; many modules feed one star, and a star lights only from the gate record or a proven conversation, never from completion (D8; design language §1). The default view is one lit next step. A person's position is private and unshareable (D12, D31).

**How many.** 274 rows carry an ID. The trunk core (the house and the bar, how to learn here, safety) holds 27; each discipline's Foundation rows are trunk as well. The seven disciplines hold 247: service craft and movement 73 (movement 20, ensemble 16, hosting the visit 37); the room and time 28 (reading 14, the room's senses 7, tempo 7); flavor and perception 13; liquid craft 22; teaching and leading 54 (teaching 16, critique and culture 19, leading 19); decisions 16; food and the kitchen 41 (the chef-gated kitchen draft 34, the menu for the floor 5, the Korean table 2). Twelve rows are library tracks. Forty-nine of the fifty-three Phase 2 candidates keep their IDs (the other four became job aids or a card); the rest are new from the sweeps, the back-of-house draft and the red teams, after the merges listed in section 3. The number is not a ceiling; it is what the sweeps surfaced and the red teams left standing after the merges in section 3.

**The most important additions beyond the Phase 2 list.**
- Food and the menu as a discipline the floor must hold: every dish to its roots, re-proving on every menu change, producers, vessels and the Korean table. The Phase 2 list had one line of chef descriptors; the best houses in the country expect the floor to know the food as the kitchen does.
- The exact standard as a craft: precision in the fixed layer (glass, silver, linen, the place setting, the smallest task nobody sees), taught as the form care takes, not as fussiness.
- Sensory craft for everyone who describes food or drink: a smell library, a daily palate check before any quality judgment, the house tasting grid.
- Flavor and perception as a full discipline, from a trunk foundation to a student who teaches the house (D28's own example).
- Perception trained at the entry seat: scanning, reading the plate, the table's state, the whole room, the team's load, listening, and a video club for learning to notice.
- Decision craft as Brandon's own way of deciding, made teachable: commander's intent, judging the call not the result, your range, the Critical Decision Method that grows the decision library past Brandon, and a route by which any rank can beat the panel.
- Consistency over time as designed rhythms, not hope: the standing recheck for every released person, the nightly review of the service against the standard, house inspection from the customer's chair, company class at every rank, the annual recommitment, invited outside eyes.
- A teaching discipline that certifies teachers, assessors, coaches and mentors, with leading (open to anyone) separated from managing (designation-gated).

**How the inventory meets the best-in-the-country bar.** The best-in-the-country read found the merged inventory ahead of the field on culture and method (critique across levels, the drill method, decision capture, studio nights, honest gates) and behind it in five places where the best houses put the most work. Each is now closed or framed: menu and product knowledge (the MNU rows, plus depth raises on describing and recommending); the exact standard (ORI-029, SVC-071, SVC-072); sensory training for the whole floor (FLV-002 and BEV-008 required for anyone who describes, FLV-005 daily); the nightly review of the whole service (LEA-046, feeding pre-shift); and keeping the whole team at the bar over time (CUL-023, CUL-024, LEA-045, CUL-016, ORI-031). Two further gaps closed: every positional lead is required to be Deep in their own craft, not only trained as a supervisor; and the house studies the field it competes in (LIB-009 widened, LIB-012), with peer houses studied as a customer and never adopted as Sŏn's standard. What stays honest: no public source documents how a top house reviews a night after service or whether its floor tastes every dish; that content comes from Brandon's own account, elicited by interview, never reconstructed.

## 1. Sŏn's teaching style, as understood

For Brandon to confirm or correct. Each line traces to the intake or a decision.

1. Training aligns people with how Brandon decides. Skill is hired-for capability that the house develops; the target on top of skill is decision-making like his, so the business runs without him in the room (intake group 7; D14).
2. Knowledge can be learned at a screen. Understanding is proven by talking, and practicals by doing at a table. Nobody is released by clicking through (intake groups 4 and 5; D8).
3. Physical macro movements are drilled apart from decisions: isolate the pattern, run it at many tempos until it is automatic, so attention goes to the decision. The two merge in performance (intake group 3).
4. A trunk of core modules, then endless branches organized by discipline, not by role. Interest unlocks depth; a short conversation unblocks a move; cross-training is designed in, never gritted through (intake group 2; D3, D28).
5. Learning is paid. Take-home practice is optional, invited and unpaid, and gates nothing (intake group 3; D7).
6. Efficacy decides length. One objective per critical-path unit with spaced recall; long-form off-path content is unrestricted (intake group 3; D15).
7. Everyone critiques everyone, across levels, as a normal culture, with two rituals kept apart: private incident review and open everyday feedback. Constant improvement is the principle; complacency and mere participation are the failure modes (intake groups 4 and 7; D22, D14).
8. Outsiders' views are applied to Sŏn through their lens (Eliasson, Devlin, Rockwell, the Adrià brothers, Holman, Spence, psychotaste as the house label). The material that teaches a goal does not have to come from hospitality (intake group 8).
9. Sŏn is a hyper-informed late entrant aiming at the top of the field. The program should be the best in the field, measured against the country's best, and never locked into traditional thinking (system design, "The bar"; intake group 7).
10. Safety and the why come before any customer contact. Testing happens before impact, never after (intake group 4; D11).

**How this shapes the inventory.**
- Every row names a learning job (know, see, decide, do, hold, find, teach) and a proof that is a conversation, a drill check or a practical, never a screen check. Every critical-path row carries a practice form (classification drill, expert comparison, branching scenario, modeling with role-play, drilled rehearsal, étude, discussion) inside its approach.
- Every movement row is a drill by Brandon's method, filed by movement family, with the decision it later merges with named, and an étude that puts the two back together before any gate.
- Every judgment row rests on Brandon's own decisions, elicited by the Critical Decision Method, and keeps separate practice, gate and measure banks (D14). The elicitation program is the first critical dependency of the whole decisions discipline and most of reading and hosting.
- Every discipline opens to anyone by interest and ends in a Student tier that produces something the house keeps, proven before a panel.
- Every outside carrier must earn its place by supplying a practice form or a cleaner instance of the idea, applied to a Sŏn moment in the same unit; one carrier per critical-path unit; a carrier that is only an analogy moves to the library.
- The front-end check runs first: gaps that are really tools, conditions, tempo, policy or motivation are routed out (section 6).
- Complacency is designed against with rhythms that never finish and always produce an output (the recheck, the nightly review, company class, studio nights), never with completion counts.
- The bar is named in the trunk (ORI-030), so the team can hold a standard it can describe, and consistency across nights and people, not the peak night, is the target.

## 2. The program structure

### 2.1 The directive, and what the red teams agreed

Brandon's directive (2026-09-28, recorded as D28): branches are disciplines, not roles; a foundation of each discipline belongs in the trunk; going all the way into a discipline as a student fits no role branch; roles become required sets; job descriptions render from them; better structures and visuals than the tree are welcome, and Brandon picks one. The design language (`framework/skill-web-design-language.md`) already sets the grammar of the visual: one sky, disciplines as color-batched constellations drawn as figures, depth as rings, node kinds by shape, roles as threads. This section fits the inventory to that grammar and names where the inventory strains it.

All four red teams found the merged inventory only half-escaped role pockets. The fixes adopted here:
- **Depth is defined by capability, never by which role requires it.** "Working means some role requires it" rebuilt the ladder under new names.
- **Stars are capabilities, not modules.** A sky of two hundred module-stars is the bloat the skill-tree research names as failure mode one.
- **Hosting is cut by the customer's arc** (first touch, threshold, the wait, opening the table, choosing, the meal's tempo, recovery, the close, the return), with door, table and counter as banks inside each moment, not sub-departments.
- **Movement is cut by movement family** (carry, pour, the build, clear and reset, the walk, body at the table, glass and silver), with role banks inside each family.
- **Nineteen disciplines collapse to D29's seven**, each drawn as up to three constellations. Safety becomes a non-compensatory floor under every sector; learning-to-learn and the house's ideas sit in the trunk; tools become job aids.
- **Every discipline has a real Student tier** that ends in a contribution proven before a panel, not a reading list.
- **Every positional lead is Deep in their own craft**, and the panel that measures decision alignment can be joined from any rank by beating it with a case.
- **Routes are required sets plus a few hard prerequisites**, not a total order. The default view is a lit next step; the sky is the zoom-out.

### 2.4 Recommendation: one data model, one sky, conservatory words

1. **The grid (D) is the source of truth.** Every module carries `discipline`, `depth` and `routes`; role records hold required stars at stated depths plus gates; job descriptions render from the role record (D28; design language §9.1). The Organizational Systems Architect reviews the `framework/module-spec.md` change (D18). IDs keep their domain prefix and move to neutral serial IDs before drafting only if Brandon wants it (open call).
2. **The sky (A) is the map,** drawn exactly as the design language specifies: seven equal sectors in D29's order, figures not wheels, capability stars, bridges on boundaries, one thread at a time, a lit next step as the default view, private position, no sharing (D31). The two states the red teams asked for exist already: "recheck due" as a house rhythm (§5.2) and "not yet open" that is future, not failure. One state is added as a proposal: **waiting on the house** (a booking link is out but no talent block is open), distinct from not-yet, so a scheduling gap never reads as the learner's failure (D13).
3. **Conservatory words (B) carry the program's vocabulary** in job descriptions, gate specs and pre-shift: études for drills, repertoire for what a person may perform alone, juries for gates and rechecks, masterclass for LIB-008, recital for the Student proof at a studio night, company class for the daily warm-up. Plain labels on the canvas ("proven before a panel"). "Chair" is used only for a discipline steward, with no rank implied. Brand and Editorial seats review the words against BG 11.
4. **Alternative if the live page is refused or delayed:** one constellation per discipline as tiles (the skill-tree research's Alternative A), the static fallback.

**Why.** The sky carries the culture (open depth, self-direction) and the operation (the thread is the critical path; the next step is the day's work); the conservatory words carry the standard and the idea that nobody is finished; the grid keeps every surface honest. It is the only shape that holds all of D28 at once and it is the picture Brandon asked for.

### 2.6 The flow inside the structure

**The trunk.** The shared core (ring 0) plus every discipline's Foundation (ring 1). It runs in layers, which are placement around a first whole task, never grades. The pre-contact set is deliberately lean, by the rule the floor and craft red teams proposed: T0 and T1 hold only units whose absence could harm someone before release, units a new person needs to act in their first shifts, and the reason the house exists and the bar it aims at, each told once.
- **T0, remote and paid:** know and see only, proven later in one T0 conversation, not a spoken check per unit. Roughly ten units: why before how with the house's choices in short; two layers; the floor-minimum house words; talking about the house; what "best" means; how you get better and how readiness is proven; your map and the learning system; the kitchen for everyone (know); certification enrollment; the allergy frame (know).
- **T1, on site before any customer contact:** the safety floor, one whole night seen before any part, the drill method with composure, learning beside an expert, calls and speaking up, a short decision frame with your range, the smallest task, the room walked, the registers for the dayparts worked, pre-shift and critique. Roughly twenty units, proven in one T1 conversation with private rehearsal first and a show-don't-tell route.
- **T2, while branching, after first release:** the depth of the house's ideas, care, the business, critique upward, the outsider's lens, flavor and the Korean table, sitting in the chair. Spaced to the learner's shifts (D15).
- **T3, standing rhythms that never finish and always produce an output:** pre-shift with one teaching item a day, the after-action review, company class, the nightly review into pre-shift, the standing recheck, studio class, studio nights, the video club, the panel series, the teaching rotation, the annual recommitment. Framed as open rooms a person walks into, never as obligations on a learner surface; the D14 watchlist reads these rhythms, never completions, and is never named on any learner surface.

**The challenge route.** An experienced hire can challenge any Working-tier drill or knowledge unit at entry by G1 conversation or banked G2 demonstration instead of sitting through the instruction (D27's prior-learning logic applied to the floor). Safety elements are never challenged away: SAF-001 and SAF-004 pass in every attempt (D11). Experienced hires also take the contrast-and-unlearn unit (ORI-033), because a hire whose prior house anticipates before the customer signals must unlearn, not skip.

**The disciplines and their depths.** Each discipline section in 3 lists Foundation, Working, Deep and Student rows and names its stars. Foundation rows are trunk; Working rows are required by some role and open to all; Deep and Student rows are open by interest to anyone whose Foundation is lit, with a conversation at the door. A few Deep rows are also required for leads and managers, which is a fact about the role, not about the depth.

**Gates.** Every role's home nodes run the gate spine: G1 spoken check, including the full current menu and pairing at depth (a founder-gated raise, section 7); G2 small-menu practical, with drills banked and the integrated practical never banked, safety elements non-compensatory (SAF-001, SAF-004, BEV-014, SAF-009, SAF-013); G3 angel shift with the mentor (D21), preconditioned on CUL-002 and CUL-006 for the learner and CUL-004 on the receiving floor (D9); G4 the eyes-off window, which must include the hardest night type the house runs (`workflow.g4_night_profile`, a founder-gated raise); then release; then the standing recheck on `workflow.recheck_cadence` for every released person (CUL-023). Gates sit on the integrated node, never per drill, so gate bandwidth at opening is survivable (D19); a module's "G2" in the tables means banked-drill evidence toward the node's practical. Every gate keeps separate practice, gate and measure banks (D14), with a practice item retired from gates once used.

**Bridges and crossings.** Bridge stars open when both neighbors' Foundations are lit: palate calibration (flavor and liquid); pacing (the room and hosting); the room tastes too (the room and flavor); describing the food honestly (flavor and the menu); the service bar handoff (ensemble and the counter). A crossing for learning needs no key. A crossing for a role move is the unblock conversation, auto-booked on a talent block, ending in one of four recorded outcomes (D13, LEA-006); "learning or move" is recorded, and crossing is chosen, never conscripted. Named crossings: runner to back waiter to front server (in-route); host to runner or barback; front server to host, lead host and over time Maître d' (WP p.19); front server into beverage (learning through flavor and the cellar, or a move through barback); barback to bartender; barista and bartender (in-discipline); a floor person into the kitchen and a cook onto the floor (KIT-060, KIT-061, chef-gated). Discipline depth never requires a crossing: a runner can go to Student in flavor without leaving the runner route.

**Stewards.** Each discipline has a named steward: the house's deepest person in it, whatever their role and rank. The steward keeps the discipline's modules current, runs its studio class, and signs Student contributions. Pay is `founder.teaching_pay` (D6). This replaces the "discipline apprenticeship" mechanic with an owner; the apprenticeship itself survives as the Student tier's form.

### 3.2 Service craft and movement

Three constellations: **movement** (the house études), **ensemble** (calls, handoffs, the pair, the pass), **hosting the visit** (the customer's arc, with door, table and counter as banks). Hue: clay.

**3.2a Movement: the house études**

Cut by movement family. Every drill follows the movement library: isolate the movement; run a tempo ladder (slow, fast, slow to fast); vary conditions (surface, load, light, a teammate crossing); mark choreography before running it full out (Warburton 2013, VP per the walk's read of the primary); check automaticity with a dual task; get feedback from a clip against an exemplar or a peer check, never a self-rating; name the decision the drill later merges with. Serial parts (pour, carry, set-down) are drilled in isolation; concurrent parts (scan while carrying, glance while greeting) are trained together from early on, and an **étude** (a short composed sequence pairing one automated movement with one light decision) reassembles the parts before any gate (Wightman and Lintern 1985, VP abstract; the constraints-led warning that fully isolated practice decouples perception from action, Renshaw and Davids, VS). Drills are scheduled as movement sets inside paid setup time and company class, never as separate sessions (intake group 5). Take-home kits are optional, unpaid, gate nothing (D7), and wait on the wage-and-hour question in section 6.

**Stars.** Working: "carries and sets down steadily at any tempo while talking"; "pours to the line by feel at any tempo"; "builds a drink with both hands to the target temperature and dilution"; "clears and resets quietly, without reaching across"; "walks a customer in at the house pace while scanning"; "holds still at eye level at the table"; "polishes and sets to the house standard, zero defects". Signature: "moves the room at tempo, in ensemble". Deep: "keeps composure when the tempo breaks"; "uses their body so it lasts a late shift". Student: "composes the station's études and extends the house movement vocabulary".

### 3.7 Decisions

### 3.9 Design patterns shared across modules (not rows)

- **The movement library and the étude** (3.2a): isolate; tempo ladder; vary conditions; mark before running full out; check automaticity with a dual task; feedback from a clip or a peer, never a self-rating; reassemble in an étude before the practical; drills scheduled as sets inside paid setup time and company class.
- **Separate banks.** Every "decide" module with expert comparison keeps separate practice, gate and measure banks (D14); a practice item used is retired from gates; practice instructions ask for "the most effective move" (Lievens, partially verified, carried from the starting structure).
- **One carrier per critical-path unit**, practiced or compared with an explicit mapping prompt; interesting but non-essential material lowers retention and transfer (Rey 2012, VP abstract; Gick and Holyoak 1980, VS; Gentner, Loewenstein and Thompson 2003, VP abstract). At T0 and T1 a carrier is at most one spoken line. Anything else moves to the library band.
- **Role banks.** One module serves several routes through banks (door, table, counter), instead of duplicate modules: ORI-020, SVC-017, SVC-037, SVC-038, SVC-047, SVC-033, SAF-001, SVC-003, BEV-002, SVC-001.
- **Layer and knowledge kind** are recorded at `/identify` for every row: service standard, hospitality perception, or both; tellable, bodily, or collective. Collective and relational craft (SVC-006, SVC-027, SVC-035, SVC-036, SVC-060, SVC-063, LEA-029, LEA-024) is written as preparation plus debrief around articulated trailing, carries "released, reads still maturing" (D10), and never promises what only the trail delivers.
- **Feedback condition.** Every elicited read is sorted by whether the floor gives fast feedback (pace, refills, course timing: trained as recognition) or delayed feedback (how the evening felt, will they return: taught with a check, look again, ask, confirm).
- **The house recall deck.** Each critical-path unit contributes practice-bank items, run as call-and-response at pre-shift and in the rehearsal studio, with the longest gap capped (D15); menu facts, specs and scripts blocked; near-miss visual sets interleaved.
- **Prebrief, freeze rule, debrief** on every role-play, with a debrief strategy matched to the gap (facilitate judgment, teach knowledge, self-assess only where the gap is visible). Highest felt risk: CUL-013, CUL-007, SVC-009, SAF-009, LEA-017, CUL-026. Private first pass before any group pass.
- **One G1 conversation per trunk layer**, sampling across the layer's units, rated from a gate bank by LEA-014 raters; spoken recall of each unit rides the recall deck.
- **Try-this-shift card and a lead's role** on every reinforce row, or the row states that transfer is not expected.
- **Non-drinking and non-eating routes** on every tasting module; no gate requires swallowing alcohol (`compliance.alcohol_tasting_on_duty`).
