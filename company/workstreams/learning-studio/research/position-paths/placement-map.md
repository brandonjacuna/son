# Placement map: where each discovery module lands in the spine (working draft, 2026-10-01)

Status: a working draft for Brandon, built to D59. It places every one of the 274 modules in section 3 of `research/module-discovery-2026-09.md` against the spine in `research/position-paths/spine.md`. Nothing here is decided until Brandon signs the spine. None is silently lost: every discovery ID has exactly one row below.

How to read it:
- Rows run in the order of the discovery inventory, group by group. Discovery titles are quoted as the inventory wrote them, so a few carry vocabulary the rebuild no longer uses; the new titles are in the spine.
- Section numbers in the last column point to the spine's module lists: 5.1 the house, 5.2 service, 5.3 food and menu, 5.4 beverage, 5.5 culinary, 5.6 leadership and teaching, 5.7 administration, 5.8 systems and technology, 5.9 compliance.
- Position codes and hours follow the spine. Hours are design estimates, not business figures.

Outcomes:
- **Kept (readiness):** the module keeps its ID and is taken before working solo, inside the window (D55).
- **Kept (readiness and ongoing):** the module keeps its ID, with a readiness part and an ongoing part, or it is readiness for one position and ongoing education (often a promotion gate) for another.
- **Moved to ongoing education:** the module keeps its ID and sits after release, as ongoing education, a standing practice, an elective or a lead's development.
- **Absorbed:** the content is folded into one module that holds it.
- **Merged:** the content is split across two or more modules, or combined with others into a new structure.
- **Parked:** kept, waiting on a named policy, space or decision.
- **Dropped:** no longer a module, with the reason. The reading tracks are dropped as modules only; their sources travel as a reading list on the module each one serves.
