## Light Investor CRM (design, for review)
- **Where:** Founding Sŏn space → list **Investor CRM**. Default task type **Investor**; one task per investor or lender (person or firm).
- **Statuses:** Prospect → Contacted → Meeting Scheduled → Diligence → Soft Commit (active) · Passed (done group) · Committed (closed).
- **Fields (Claude/REST):** Organization (text) · Email · Phone · Investor Type (dropdown: Angel/Individual, Family Office, Fund, Lender, Strategic Partner) · Check Size (currency) · Committed Amount (currency) · Intro Source (text) · Last Contact (date) · Next Follow-up (date) · Related Meetings (relationship → Meetings list) · Created By Notes (existing).
- **Follow-ups:** subtasks under the investor (e.g. "Send investor packet") with due dates. No separate to-do list.
- **Views (REST):** Pipeline board (default, by status) · Table · Follow-ups Due (Next Follow-up this week or overdue) · Committed.
- **Dashboard (Brandon, UI, optional later):** pipeline by stage; committed vs $2.5M.
- **Migration:** preview maps every investor-related Punch List task (the Person-type investor tasks + their subtasks + the 12 `investor`-tagged tasks) to: investor record, follow-up subtask, or stays in the Punch List (e.g. "Create Pitch Deck 1.0" is fundraising work, not an investor). After approval Claude moves them with the API and verifies fields/status carried over. Scaling People is never included.
