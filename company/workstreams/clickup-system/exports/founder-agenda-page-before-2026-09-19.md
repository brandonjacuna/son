**Terms used once below:**
*   **Company agreement:** the Texas name for an LLC operating agreement.
*   **Track 1 / Track 2:** Track 1 is the founder-only agreement signed now. Track 2 is the investor package a lawyer reviews at closing.
*   **Rule 506(b):** the federal private-offering exemption that allows no public advertising of the raise and relies on existing relationships.
*   **Accredited investor:** a person who meets the SEC's income or net-worth test.
*   **Supplement:** a written correction sent to everyone who already received the disclosure (today, Robby and Robert only).
*   **Reserved matter:** a decision that needs both founders' approval.
*   **Guaranteed payment:** the usual tax treatment of pay to an owner of an LLC taxed as a partnership, instead of wages.

### 2.2 Profit and distributions are 50/50 (G10, G22)
Texas default today splits profit and distributions by the value each of you contributed, not 50/50.
- [ ] Confirm 50/50 regardless of contributions.
- [ ] Each of you states an exact contribution amount and date for the records.
- [ ] Confirm investors never face a capital call (a demand to put in more money). Future funding comes from new units approved by both of you.

### 2.3 If one of you leaves (G19)
Today a founder who walks away in year one keeps 50% forever.
- [ ] Pick one: (1) keeps all units, loses management rights; (2) company or other founder may buy some or all at fair market value on notice; (3) reverse vesting, meaning unearned units are bought back at cost, over a set schedule. Over what period?

### 2.4 Deadlock exit (G29, G30, G31)
The dispute ladder ends in a "buy-sell" that is named but not defined.
- [ ] Pick the exit: (1) shotgun, where one founder names a price and the other must buy or sell at it; (2) an independent appraiser sets fair value and the initiator may buy or must sell; (3) sell the company if neither can fund a purchase.
- [ ] While a deadlock runs: nothing changes, or the domain owner acts?
- [ ] Inside one person's domain, does the domain owner decide immediately or only after the ladder? (Sections 06 and 10 disagree today.)
- [ ] Name a mediator or service now, and confirm the company pays.

### 2.5 The misconduct buyout (G15, G16, G17, G18, G28)
Today one founder can suspend and judge the other alone, and force a buyout at fair value minus 33%, which a court may refuse to enforce as a penalty.
- [ ] Any suspension beyond a short fixed period (for example 10 days), and any finding, goes to a named neutral. If you accuse each other, neither suspends the other.
- [ ] Price: fair market value from an independent appraiser, no discount, proven damages recovered separately. Installment note with interest, paid in full if the company is sold. Investor money is never used for a buyout.
- [ ] One list of conduct triggers (Sections 03 and 09 conflict today). Is one raised voice at an employee a removal event, or a first warning with a chance to cure?
- [ ] Any hold during an investigation applies to distributions only, not pay.
- [ ] Section 08 (political neutrality) stays a conduct standard with a cure period, never a buyout trigger. Add mutual non-disparagement (neither of you speaks badly of Sŏn or the other publicly) with the legally required exceptions.
