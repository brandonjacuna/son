# ClickUp AI Research Brief
Prepared 2026-09-16 for Sŏn (ClickUp workspace 90131574430, Business plan + AI add-on, Google Calendar connected)

## Key findings

1. **Super Agents are triggered manually (@mention in task/Doc comments or Chat, DM, assign as task assignee), on a schedule (hourly/daily/weekly/monthly/custom), or via Automations** attached to a Space/Folder/Subfolder/List/Channel. [ClickUp Help](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/37092796379927-Super-Agent-instructions-triggers-tools-and-knowledge) — CONFIRMED (vendor docs).
2. **Instructions are required and can be trigger-specific** — you write primary instructions plus additional instructions per manual/scheduled trigger, editable anytime. Same source — CONFIRMED.
3. **Super Agents can read and act on Google Calendar once connected**: "After connecting your Google Calendar account, you can use Google Calendar tools in Brain and Agents, and you can allow a Super Agent to search calendar events" — CONFIRMED capability to *search*; creating/editing events via the agent specifically was not confirmed in vendor docs found (UNCERTAIN — worth verifying live in the workspace before relying on it for the Meetings Super Agent). [ClickUp Google Calendar integration](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/6336507264663-Google-Calendar-integration)
4. **AI Super Credits fuel Super Agents, AI Fields, Automations, and image generation.** Brain AI add-on ($9/user/mo) includes 1,500 credits/user/month; Everything AI ($28/user/mo) includes 5,000 credits/user/month. A Super Agent run costs roughly 100–300 credits. Extra credits: ~$10/10,000. [ClickUp Pricing](https://clickup.com/pricing) — CONFIRMED from vendor pricing page (fetched live).
5. **Business plan is $12/user/mo (annual) / $19/user/mo (monthly)**; the AI add-on stacks on top. For 2 founders + 1 incoming partner, budget ~$21–37/user/mo depending on Brain vs Everything AI tier. Same source.
6. **Super Agents can be built via a natural-language builder or from scratch**, and ClickUp markets the ability to "spin up an entire team of agents from a single prompt" mapping workflows to specialized agents — suggesting multi-agent setups are a first-class pattern, though true agent-to-agent handoff mechanics (one agent programmatically invoking another) were not clearly documented; what's confirmed is that agents can be @mentioned, assigned tasks, and DM'd like teammates, enabling human-mediated handoffs between agents. [ClickUp Super Agents](https://clickup.com/brain/agents), [ClickUp Blog](https://clickup.com/blog/super-agents-launch/) — MIXED: marketing language CONFIRMED, actual agent-to-agent API/handoff mechanics UNCERTAIN.
7. **ClickUp Brain indexes tasks, Docs, comments, Chat, subtasks, and attachments** across a Space/Workspace, plus connected external apps. [ClickUp Help – What is ClickUp Brain](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/12578085238039-What-is-ClickUp-Brain) — CONFIRMED.
8. **Connected Search (part of Brain's enterprise search) reaches Google Drive, Slack, Notion, Gmail** — but for Google Drive specifically, only plain text, HTML, CSV, and PDF file types are actually searchable/indexed (not native Google Docs/Sheets unless exported to those formats — verify current behavior, as this is a meaningful limit). Connections can be personal (only your own access) or Workspace-wide. [Connected Search](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/14642390285463-Connected-Search) — CONFIRMED docs, but UNCERTAIN whether native Google Docs are now supported since this could have changed in 2026.
9. **AI Notetaker joins ad hoc Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams meetings** automatically (requires Calendar connection — Google Calendar or Outlook). **No evidence of phone-call support** — all sourcing is video-conferencing only. [ClickUp Help](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/28928137493015-Use-AI-Notetaker-to-take-notes-and-record-meetings) — CONFIRMED platforms; phone support UNCERTAIN/likely unsupported (absence of evidence, not a documented "no").
10. **Notes, transcripts, and summaries land in a private ClickUp Doc**; action items become a checklist and can be converted into assigned tasks (manually, by highlighting text and using "create task," and/or automatically per ClickUp's marketing copy — vendor blog claims auto-assignment, but the Help article implies a manual create-task-from-highlight step, so treat "fully automatic" as UNCERTAIN). [ClickUp Brain AI Notetaker](https://clickup.com/brain/ai-notetaker) — MIXED confidence.
11. **AI Notetaker has a separate, distinct flow for ClickUp's own SyncUps** (native video calls) vs. external meetings (Zoom/Meet/Teams) — documented in a separate Help article, implying SyncUp support was bolted on later and may lag in maturity. **Known limitation: AI Notetaker cannot capture chat history for channel meetings**, and for Teams meetings chat is only captured if meeting chat is accessible to anonymous/external participants. [SyncUp Notetaker docs](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/30565324050199-Use-AI-Notetaker-to-take-notes-and-record-SyncUps) — CONFIRMED limitation exists; the specific "captures nothing on SyncUps" failure mode you asked about was **not found as a documented or widely-reported bug** in searches (community feedback threads exist requesting the feature/reliability improvements, but no confirmed "nothing captured" bug report as of Sept 2026) — mark this specific claim UNCONFIRMED, recommend a live test before relying on SyncUps for real meetings.
12. **Automations can trigger Super Agents** as an action (from the Action dropdown), and conversely Super Agents can be fired by Automations, on a schedule, or manually — three trigger paths total, matching what you need for the rolling 4-week meeting scheduler. [ClickUp Help](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/37092796379927-Super-Agent-instructions-triggers-tools-and-knowledge) — CONFIRMED.
13. **Automations got a notable feature refresh as of ClickUp 3.63 (~Aug 2026)**: new "Task Name Updated" trigger, "Change Task Name" and "Add Relationship" actions, plus regex conditions — recent enough that older tutorials/screenshots may be stale. [Releasebot ClickUp changelog](https://releasebot.io/updates/clickup) — CONFIRMED, dated Aug 2026.
14. **AI can auto-build an Automation from a plain-language description** (name, trigger, and action all drafted, editable before publishing) — useful for quickly standing up the recurring-meeting Automations without hand-configuring each one. [Build Automations with ClickUp AI](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/20690779238423-Build-Automations-with-ClickUp-AI) — CONFIRMED (title-only fetch; verify UI details live since full page fetch was blocked).
15. **Recurring tasks support fixed schedules (daily/weekly/monthly/custom) and completion-based recurrence**, and ClickUp explicitly recommends creating one recurring task per recurring meeting (e.g., "every Monday at 9:00") as the standard pattern for meeting cadences — directly matches the planned rolling 4-week meeting-task model. [ClickUp recurring tasks](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/6309885016471-Use-recurring-tasks), [ClickUp for meetings](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/6328439306263-ClickUp-for-meetings) — CONFIRMED.

## Detailed notes

**Super Agent creation & config**: Built via a natural-language builder or configured from a blank profile; profile holds instructions, triggers, tools/knowledge sources, and memory settings. Instructions are mandatory; trigger-specific instructions layer on top of primary instructions. [help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/37092796379927](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/37092796379927-Super-Agent-instructions-triggers-tools-and-knowledge)

**Triggers, full list found**: @mention (task comment, Doc comment, Chat), direct message, assigned as task assignee (manual); hourly/daily/weekly/monthly/custom (scheduled); Automation action from Space/Folder/Subfolder/List/Channel (automated). No dedicated "task created/updated" native agent trigger was documented directly — that path goes through Automations acting as the trigger, with "Super Agents" selected as the Automation's action. Same source.

**Credits/pricing detail**: Business plan $12/user/mo annual ($19/mo monthly). Brain AI add-on $9/user/mo → 1,500 credits/user/mo. Everything AI add-on $28/user/mo → 5,000 credits/user/mo, and unlocks unlimited AI Notetaker and unlimited Ambient Answers (vs. metered on Brain AI tier). Super Agent runs ≈100–300 credits each; AI Fields ≈10 credits; image generation ≈100 credits. Extra credits purchasable at ~$0.001/credit ($10/10k). [clickup.com/pricing](https://clickup.com/pricing) (fetched live 2026-09-16).
- Implication: with 1,500 credits/user/mo on Brain AI, a heavy Super Agent user could burn through the monthly allotment in ~5–15 agent runs alone, before AI Fields or automations. For a Meetings Super Agent running on a rolling 4-week schedule creating/canceling/rescheduling meetings across 2–3 founders, Everything AI's larger credit pool (5,000/user) is the safer tier, and also removes AI Notetaker metering — likely the better fit once the 3rd partner joins.

**Brain indexing / enterprise search**: Indexes native Workspace content (tasks, Docs, comments, Chat, subtasks, attachments) plus, via Connected Search, external apps (Google Drive, Slack, Notion, Gmail confirmed as connector options). Google Drive connector currently limited to plain text, HTML, CSV, and PDF — native Google Docs/Sheets files may not be indexed unless exported to a supported format; this is a meaningful gap if Sŏn plans to keep working docs natively in Google Drive. Connections can be personal (searcher's own access only) or Workspace-wide (all members can search a shared connected account). [Connected Search](https://help.clickup.com/hc/en-us/articles/14642390285463-Connected-Search)

**AI Notetaker**: Confirmed platforms are Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams for ad hoc meetings; requires Google/Outlook Calendar connection to the ClickUp Planner. No phone-call support found in any source searched — treat as unsupported until confirmed otherwise in-app. Notes/transcripts/summaries land in a private ClickUp Doc (transcript sometimes on a second Doc page); "Next Steps" is a generated action-item checklist; converting an action item into a real ClickUp task can be done by highlighting text in the Doc/transcript and using a "create task" option — full auto-assignment into tasks is claimed by ClickUp marketing copy but the Help-article workflow reads as a manual/assisted step, so don't assume zero-touch task creation without testing.

**SyncUps vs. external meetings**: ClickUp SyncUps (native ClickUp video calls) have their own, separately documented AI Notetaker flow, distinct from the Zoom/Meet/Teams flow — this split existed as of the docs found and suggests SyncUp notetaking is a newer/less mature path. Documented limitation: chat history isn't captured for channel meetings, and for Teams specifically chat is only captured when the meeting chat is accessible to anonymous participants. No confirmed report (vendor or community) of AI Notetaker "capturing nothing" specifically on SyncUps was found in this research pass — feedback-forum threads request reliability/feature improvements generally, but nothing matching that specific failure mode surfaced. Recommend the team pilot-test a real SyncUp with Notetaker before depending on it for a recorded meeting that matters.

**Automations relevant to meetings**: Automations support AI-assisted authoring (describe the automation in plain language, ClickUp AI drafts trigger + action + name, editable before publish). Super Agents can be the action of an Automation. Recent (Aug 2026, v3.63) additions include a "Task Name Updated" trigger and "Add Relationship" action — the latter is notable for linking a recurring meeting task to its related Doc/notes automatically.

**Recurring tasks**: Two recurrence models — fixed schedule (daily/weekly/monthly/custom) and completion-based (recur N days after the task is marked complete). ClickUp's own guidance is to model each recurring meeting as a recurring task, which lines up directly with Sŏn's planned rolling 4-week meeting-task pattern; combine with Automations/Super Agent triggers to generate the Google Calendar invite and attendee list per occurrence.

## Gotchas / limits

- **Super Agent → Calendar write access is unconfirmed.** Docs confirm agents can *search* Google Calendar once connected; explicit create/modify/cancel of calendar events by a Super Agent was not found documented. This is the single most important thing to verify hands-on before building the Meetings Super Agent, since the whole design depends on the agent creating/canceling/rescheduling calendar invites.
- **Credit consumption adds up fast.** 100–300 credits per Super Agent run against a 1,500/user/month allowance (Brain AI tier) means a Meetings Super Agent handling create/cancel/reschedule across a handful of recurring meetings weekly could hit metering limits; budget for Everything AI or extra credit purchases.
- **Google Drive Connected Search is format-limited** (plain text/HTML/CSV/PDF) — native Google Docs won't be searchable via Brain unless exported/converted.
- **AI Notetaker: no confirmed phone-call support** — plan phone meetings to route through Granola instead, per the existing goal split (Granola for in-person/phone, Notetaker for remote).
- **SyncUps Notetaker is a separate, possibly less mature path** from the main Zoom/Meet/Teams flow, with a known chat-capture gap — pilot before relying on it.
- **Multi-agent handoff mechanics are marketing-forward but technically underspecified** — "spin up a team of agents from a prompt" and @mention/DM/assign-based coordination are confirmed, but a clean programmatic "Agent A hands off to Agent B" API/pattern was not confirmed in docs. Design the Meetings Super Agent as a single agent with broad tool access rather than assuming clean sub-agent delegation.
- **Two Help-article URL variants were found for the same Super Agent page** (titles differ slightly: "tools and knowledge" vs. "skills, knowledge, and memory" vs. "Skills, triggers, knowledge, and memory"), suggesting ClickUp has been actively renaming/reorganizing this doc in 2026 — expect terminology (e.g., "Skills" vs. "tools") to keep shifting; re-check before final build.
- **help.clickup.com blocked direct WebFetch (403)** during this research pass — findings on Super Agents/Notetaker rely on search-result snippets rather than full article text; recommend the team open these Help Center articles directly while logged in for the authoritative, current wording before building.

## Implications for Sŏn

- **Verify calendar-write capability live, first.** Before designing the Meetings Super Agent around "create/cancel/reschedule with Google Calendar invites," open a test Super Agent in the Sŏn workspace, connect Google Calendar, and confirm it can create/modify an event, not just search — this determines whether the whole architecture is agent-native or needs an Automation+Zapier/native-calendar-action fallback.
- **Budget for Everything AI ($28/user/mo), not just Brain AI ($9/user/mo)**, given expected Super Agent usage (recurring meeting management) plus unlimited AI Notetaker — the credit math on Brain AI's 1,500/user allowance looks tight for a workspace running a rolling 4-week meeting scheduler plus general Brain usage across 3 founders.
- **Keep Granola for phone and in-person meetings** — no evidence AI Notetaker handles phone calls, so the existing split (Granola for in-person/phone, Notetaker for remote-by-default) is directionally correct and should stay.
- **Pilot AI Notetaker on both a real Zoom/Meet/Teams call and a SyncUp** before rollout, specifically checking that notes/transcript/action items land as expected — the SyncUp path is less documented and the "captures nothing" risk, while unconfirmed as a widespread bug, is plausible enough (newer feature, thin documentation) to test rather than assume.
- **Use Automations (not raw agent logic) to wire recurring-task creation → Doc linking → Super Agent trigger**, taking advantage of the Aug 2026 "Add Relationship" action to auto-link each recurring meeting task to its notes Doc, and use AI-assisted Automation authoring to speed up building the 4-week rolling schedule logic.
- **Don't rely on native Google Docs being searchable in Brain** for meeting prep material kept in Drive — either keep working docs in ClickUp Docs natively, or export/convert Drive files to PDF/text if Connected Search coverage matters for meeting prep.
- **Treat "agent hands off to agent" as not yet a clean primitive** — build one well-scoped Meetings Super Agent with the necessary tools/permissions rather than planning a multi-agent pipeline (e.g., a "scheduling agent" calling a "notes agent") until ClickUp's docs more clearly confirm agent-to-agent invocation.
