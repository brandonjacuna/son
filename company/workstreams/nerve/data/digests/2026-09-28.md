## 9. So what for Sŏn

**Demand is shifting, not growing.** Metro alcohol sales are up 3.8% (Jun to Aug 2026), but venues open both years are flat and only 41% of them grew. New openings are taking share from existing ones. Sŏn opens into a market where customers are spread across more places, not spending more.

**Oseyo has found a bigger audience.** Its monthly alcohol sales went from about $25K to $35K in mid-2025 to $100K to $120K since December 2025. It is doing roughly four times last year's alcohol volume.

**The blocks around 207 E St. Elmo Rd are changing format.**

| Venue | Distance | Alcohol sales vs last year |
|---|---|---|
| Pins Mechanical, 4323 S Congress | 0.2 mi | -40% |
| Twin Peaks, 701 E Stassney | 1.1 mi | -83% |
| Ojos Locos, 901 Little Texas Ln | 1.3 mi | -36% |
| Skeleton Key, 4720 S Congress | 0.2 mi | +84% |
| Fortune Teller, 5604 S Congress | 1.0 mi | +58% |

Large sports and entertainment venues are losing ground; smaller cocktail bars are gaining it. The St. Elmo area overall is down 29%, entirely from the first three.

**Margins will be tight.** Texas service firms report wages rising about twice as fast as the prices they charge (Dallas Fed, Aug 2026). Restaurant menu prices in the South are up 2.9% (CPI, Aug 2026).

**Coming up**
- ACL Festival at Zilker: Oct 2 to 4 and Oct 9 to 11
- UT home games: Oct 17, 24, 31
- Formula 1 US Grand Prix: Oct 22 to 25 (same weekend as Texas vs Ole Miss)

### Recommended actions
1. Visit Oseyo this month. Document what changed since December: menu, bar program, hours, pricing.
2. Add Twin Peaks (701 E Stassney) to the Watchlist as At-risk and confirm its status with a second source.
3. Visit Skeleton Key and Fortune Teller as the clearest read on bar demand within a mile of the site.
4. Build the opening labor plan assuming wages keep outpacing menu prices.

### How to read this

- **Alcohol sales** are Texas Mixed Beverage Gross Receipts: what each licensed venue reports monthly to the Comptroller. They cover alcohol only, so food-led and BYOB places are under-counted.
- **Same venues** compares only places that filed in both years, which strips out openings and closings.
- Areas with fewer than 8 same venues are combined. Openings and closings are called only with two sources.
