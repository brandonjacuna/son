## 9. So what for Sŏn

**Demand is shifting, not growing.** Metro alcohol sales are up 3.8% (Jun to Aug 2026), but venues open both years are flat and only 41% of them grew. New openings are taking share from existing ones. Sŏn opens into a market where customers are spread across more places, not spending more.

**Oseyo has found a bigger audience.** Its monthly alcohol sales went from about $25K to $35K in mid-2025 to $100K to $120K since December 2025. It is doing roughly four times last year's alcohol volume.

Large sports and entertainment venues are losing ground; smaller cocktail bars are gaining it. The St. Elmo area overall is down 29%, entirely from the first three.

**Margins will be tight.** Texas service firms report wages rising about twice as fast as the prices they charge (Dallas Fed, Aug 2026). Restaurant menu prices in the South are up 2.9% (CPI, Aug 2026).
