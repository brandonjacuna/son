## Protocol

Carryovers are cross-session couplings: an idea, constraint, or decision from one session that affects another. They live as tasks in ClickUp list `901327884538`, each linked to its target session task.

When a session surfaces a carryover:

1. Create a task in the Carryover Register list.
2. Link it to the target session task.
3. Add a row to the table below.

When a session starts, it reads every carryover routed to it. These are mandatory reads, not optional context.

---

## The three states

Not every item routes to a session, and the distinction matters. Flattening it would make a session treat settled material as unread input.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Live | Routed to a specific session. That session must read it before working. |
| Absorbed | Already carried by the three profiles and the tracker protocol. Applies to every session, and no session needs to act on it separately. Kept open in ClickUp for provenance. |
| Parked | Routed out of the program. Not a session's work. |
