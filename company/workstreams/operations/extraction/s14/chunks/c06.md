## 12. Decision rights for this session's mechanisms

Written in the ledger's form: owner, range stated as may and may not, triggers each with a named destination. An entry missing a part is marked incomplete and is not filled by analogy (S3 section 4).

### 12.1 The horizon window

Owner. The stack opens and closes it; the lead who holds the person's check-in holds the block and the check-ins inside it; an assessor from the pool reads the close; the person writes their words and chooses a move.

Range. The lead may move or extend the block by the check-in's range; may read the trigger's records, the horizon, and the building's fixes aloud; may read the state aloud before it is saved; may extend the window once for a building-caused block with the fix named, at a check-in, with the person; may state which moves are open. The lead may not open a window, request one, or prevent one; may not shorten, end early, or extend it twice; may not write anything in the lead's voice on the block or at any check-in inside it; may not say a sentence whose subject is the person and whose predicate is a state the window does not hold; may not predict the outcome or answer whether the person can succeed; may not assign a task, project, or deliverable; may not override the assessor's form in either direction; may not cite the window at a review, a brief, a close, the leads' review beyond the count, or any room; may not carry the block's content to anyone but the person; may not withhold a designation, a paired service, or a module from a person in a window; may not open a second window on a seat that repeats before the diagnostic's seat read. The assessor may not be the mentor, the lead, or a same-section peer that service, and scores the rows in a real service and nothing else. The person may decline a move and may not be moved.

Triggers. A trigger's count reached goes to the stack's placement of the block and to the person's and the lead's schedules, and its citations to the block. A not-supplied line inside the window goes to the window's pause and to the leads' review as an infrastructure line. A window extended goes to the leads' review as an infrastructure line; a second extension request goes to the partners' review as a node-overload or infrastructure entry. A close reading held goes to the block's state and the check-in interval's return to monthly. A close reading not held goes to the block's state, the next check-in's row reading, the moves' count, and, where no move is open or chosen, the hand-off package to the partners' review as a reserved-class item under Session 15's design. A window's block or check-ins displacing the slot or the readiness block goes to cooling failure's line on the window. The counts of windows opened, held, not held, paused, and extended per seat class per period go to the leads' review record and the reset. A first-year window goes to the calibration read (12.5). A window on a seat that repeats goes to the diagnostic's seat read before the block is placed. The kitchen's entry is chef-gated. Complete for the floor.
