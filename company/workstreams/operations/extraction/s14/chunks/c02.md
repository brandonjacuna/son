## 2. The steady middle: a person holding a seat's horizon

### 2.1 What it is at Sŏn

The steady middle is her phrase. Sŏn's is a person holding a seat's horizon: someone who came through the readiness form at field 6's entry horizon or a later one (S7 P23; S8 P37), holds the seat on the schedule, uses the range inside its limits, writes their captures, and comes to the check-in every month with a line about what is working and what is in the way. On any given service that is nearly everyone on the floor and in the kitchen. It is not a category a person belongs to; it is the state of a seat between unlocks, and the same person is in it the week after an unlock and out of it the day the next form reads ready. It has no name on any record, because the record already has the words: the horizon held, since when, and what is scheduled next, which is what the stack writes as the review's dimension 1 (S13 P7).

The record's one description of such a person is the mentor: "a proven cultural steward rather than simply the strongest performer" (WP p.18). That sentence is the record choosing, for the one relationship it hands a new person, the steady over the strong, and it is the closest the record comes to saying what section 2 says.

### 2.2 What the house owes it

Six things, each already a mechanism, listed here because a house that lists what it owes its middle is a house that has noticed it has one.

The horizon's weight and the pool's growth. A person who holds a horizon earns the seat's published weight at that horizon and shares every full night the house fills, for as long as they hold the seat, with no review, rating, or lead's judgment in the arithmetic (S13 P23, P37). Nothing is withheld from a person for not unlocking. The record's "the nights they help fill" (WP p.20) is filled by the seats held on them.

Bandwidth. "Bandwidth, then joy, then retention, then results" (WP p.12) is owed first to the people who are not chasing anything, because they are the ones the industry's model runs "at full capacity until they stop" (WP p.13). The bandwidth ledger, cooling failure's line, and the schedule's shape are read at the house and domain, never at the person (S9 P13; S10 P34), and the allocation consequence, covers before the floor is thinned, is founder-gated on `86akh3t00`.

The schedule as a fact the stack holds. A person holding a horizon is owed a shift shape that matches the one on the sheet's field 11 and the one they stated at the fourth read (S5 4.6; S6 6.2), a check-in placed by the stack and never by a lead's memory (S4 P15), and a review on their own clock (S13 P3). None of those depends on the person asking.

The check-in, the same fifteen minutes for everyone. Her manager "spends a lot more time with their high performers than with their low performers" (Book p.426), and her chapter's whole allocation of attention is a manager's choice. At Sŏn the check-in is placed by the stack at the same interval for everyone in the house, a lead may not shorten or skip one, and a placed check-in is a lead's one discretionary act, counted (S4 P15, P20; S12 P16). A lead cannot spend more time with the top and less with the middle, because the time is not the lead's to allocate. That is the mechanism that keeps the middle from being neglected, and it is the one her chapter cannot build because her time is the manager's.

The mentor relationship's shape. A person holding a horizon is the record's first choice of mentor (WP p.18), from the pool, paid as shift, never scoring and never writing on the mentee's page (S7 P8, P42). After the plan the house records only the shape the mentee stated and takes no further act (S10 P32). Mentoring carries the teaching marker while a plan is open (S13 P32). Being asked to mentor is not a designation and not a reward; it is the pool's draw, and it is the one place the record says out loud that steadiness is the qualification.

A designation as rotation, not as status. The room, the door, the sequence, the pass: each carries a rotation floor so that no eligible person holds it every service while another holds it on none (S10 P33). A person holding a horizon holds designations by eligibility, which is a complete readiness form and range card and no non-negotiable line (S8 P25), and never by a lead's preference. The count is read at the reset and never as the holder's performance (S9 P32).

### 2.3 What it is never asked for

An unlock. The next skill set opens on the sheet's period by itself (WP p.11); completing it is self-paced, and a person who does not complete it holds the seat. No check-in asks why the modules are not done; the third question reads the progression state as the stack shows it and the person says what they want next (S4 4.3; S12 4.4), and "nothing" is an answer that writes nothing.

Ambition, a stretch goal, or a "growth mindset" performance. A person's one goal is set at their check-in by the person with the lead, never by the lead for the person (S4 7.3), and it may be the plan's goal or the seat held; a committed goal is binary (S4 7.4). Her "development plans" for medium performers (Book p.431) are refused as a manager's document: the path line in the career conversation block is in the person's words and may name the seat they hold as the seat they want (S9 P43).

A second strand, a second period, a second domain. Fluency is a marker while current and never a placement tool (S8 P35, P36); a person who does not hold both strands holds one, and the house never reads the absence of a marker as anything.

A designation. The rotation floor is a floor, not a duty: an eligible person who is never named is a rotation question about the lead's naming, and a person who declines a designation for a service declines it, written on no one's page.

To be a mentor. The pool's five conditions sit beneath S5 P16 (S7 P41); a person who does not want to mentor is not in the pool, and the record's "proven cultural steward" is a description, not a draft notice.

### 2.4 How the middle is seen and recognized without a tier

Her "verbal recognition and special assignments" (Book p.432) splits into what the house keeps and what it refuses. Special assignments are refused whole: no task is handed down at a check-in or a brief (S9 P38), an owner is named by domain and never by a person's development (S9 P39), and a designation is never a reward (S10 P33). Recognition survives as the record's own: peer recognition, "which lands differently than management praise because it comes from the people who understand the work" (WP p.19), a peer's words about an act in one service, read verbatim at the brief, never a lead's praise and never a count per person (S11 P11, P13). The steady middle is recognized in the only register the house allows anyone to be recognized in, and by the people who saw the act.

Three more surfaces see it without saying so. The review's dimension 1 is a sentence the stack writes, the horizon held and since when (S13 P7), and the lead reads the record aloud as specific acts on specific services, which Session 13 named as the formal half of "you really have talent" (S13 5.3): for a person holding a horizon, the record read aloud is six months of the captures they wrote, the range they used, and the nights they filled. The pay page every morning shows the days and the seat's weight, which is the house saying, in arithmetic, that the seat held is the whole job (S13 P38). And the customer recognition rate (WP p.22), the leading indicator of whether the building is "actually recognizing and remembering customers," is the middle's own measure: recognition at the fifth visit is produced by the server who was there for the first four.

Her "seek their feedback on how to improve the team" (Book p.432) is the record's "the best ideas and the earliest warnings both come from the people doing the work" (WP p.19): the check-in's upward question and the channel (S4 P15; S11 6.8), asked of everyone at the same interval.

### 2.6 Her middle material, sentence by sentence

Compensation: the horizon's weight and the days are not a reward for consistency; they are the pay for the seat, and consistency is what the seat is.

Her material carries no "rock star versus superstar" framing in these pages; the brief asked that it be tested if present, and it is not present. ## 3. A person moving past their horizon: her high performers, without a list, a label, or a lever

### 3.1 What carries her function

Her high performer is kept by promotion, raises, visibility, projects, and a manager who spends the most time on them. Every one of those is a lever a lead holds, and the record removed the lead from the path on purpose: "No one's advancement waits on a manager's attention or judgment. The system is the advocate" (WP p.11). What carries her function is nine mechanisms nine sessions built, and this section names them once so that the next lead who wants to "do something" for a person moving fast can see that everything is already running.

The unlock. The next skill set opens on the sheet's period, the modules are self-paced, the practical assessment auto-schedules, an assessor from the pool scores it in a real service, and the domain lead records the form and may not override it (WP p.11; S8 P37). A person who wants the next horizon takes it at their own pace, and the weight moves on the form's date (S13 P29). This is her "keep them uncomfortable" (Book p.428) with the discomfort chosen by the person.

The markers. Cross-strand and cross-period fluency, demonstrated by two forms and a seat held on each side inside a window, recorded as connections and priced while current (S8 P35, P36; S13 P29). "Cross-strand fluency, the ability to hold both at once, is the real development marker" (WP p.11). This is her "focus on the type of work, not the title" (Book p.425): the person who wants operational work after relational work opens the inward strand's track.

The second domain. Tracks are visible from day one (S7 P39), and a person may open another domain's track once their own plan closes, assessed by the receiving domain's assessors and recorded by its lead, chef-gated for the kitchen (S8 P39; S7 P40). "A server who learns the kitchen becomes more valuable everywhere" (WP p.19).

Teaching and authorship. "Any qualified team member can create training modules and get paid for teaching everyone else" (WP p.19): authorship is a flat payment per approved version under the module standard (S7 I11; S13 P32), and mentoring carries the teaching marker while a plan is open. This is her "help them develop others" (Book p.422) with the developing paid and never assigned.

The career conversation. Held once, in the month after the plan closes, opening on the path line, the sheet's horizons, the tracks, and the fluency record; writing one track and what the building must supply in the person's words; refusing levels, titles, seat promises, and pay (S9 P43, P44). The check-in's third question. What changed, the progression state, and what the person wants next, every month, in their words (S4 4.3; S12 4.4). This is her "anticipate when the work will become boring" (Book p.423) read monthly from the person rather than guessed by the lead.

Designations. The room, the door, the sequence, the pass, each for one service, each by eligibility and rotation, never a reward (S10 P33). A person with more connections "is asked to hold more designations" (S8 10.2) because they are eligible for more of them, and never because a lead chose them.

The ready-now pool and succession. "If no position is open, the employee enters a ready-now pool" (WP p.11); on any departure the pool is read before the seat opens externally, and an outside hire for a lead seat carries a written reason on the partners' page (S5 P60). Lead seats are filled from the pool by the succession mechanism (S8 P30), and the record's goal is that "the building rarely hires a manager from outside at all" (WP p.19). This is her "consider whether they should have your job" (Book p.425) with the consideration the pool's and not the incumbent's.

The path line and the track. On the person page, in the person's words, re-read at every review (S13 P6). This is where "what a person wants" lives, and it is the only place.
