### 3.4 Retiring a seat whose holder is in it

## 4. The separation protocol

### 4.1 What it is called, and the three kinds

This is the first description on the record of the house ending anyone's employment, and it is written in the record's terms and not hers. A departure is a tension re-establishment (WP p.9). The building is blamed first (WP p.17). The web loses tension and the adjacent nodes compensate at cost (WP p.8). Dignity is protected at every turn (WP p.26). Nothing conceals what it is (Canon 1.0, section 01). No disposable people (Canon 1.0, section 01; Deck 3.0, p.4). The house's words for this on every surface are "departure," "leaving," "the last day," "the decision about staying," and "a reduction." Her words, fire, terminate, let go, manage out, layoff, severance, gross misconduct, appear on no house surface, on no page, and in no conversation, except where counsel requires the law's word on a document counsel writes.

Three kinds of departure, each designed end to end below.

The person's choice (4.2). The person decides to leave. This is the only departure the record describes, and the house's part is to make it clean, paid, and dignified, and to learn from it.

The house's decision (4.3, 4.4). The partners decide, as a reserved class with counsel, that a person does not stay, on one of two records and nothing else: the hand-off package after a horizon window closed not held with no move open or chosen (S14 I9), or a non-negotiable line with the flag's record and the person's words (S14 P26).

A reduction (4.5). The house changes a period, its hours, or its seats for a reason that is not about any person: a period that thins, a period that closes for a season or for good, a seat retired. Almost all of it is not a departure. Its last step can be.

What every kind shares: the departure's date as a state on the person page written by the stack; the tension-loss row, field 15, written the day the departure is known (S5 P12); the three-pass read (S9 P30); the pool read before the seat opens externally (S5 P60); coverage at the brief and nothing about the person (S11 P25); final pay as the days worked and the accrued share to the last pay date (4.7); the exit interview offered (5.4); the person's words as theirs (S11 P17); the departure record's lawful holder and retention as counsel's (14).

### 4.2 The first kind: a departure the person chooses

Saying it. A person who decides to leave says so to their check-in's holder, in person or in writing, on the clock or off it, because a person may speak of their own life whenever they choose; the house asks it of no one at any time. Nothing is required of the person before they say it, and the house does not ask why. The check-in holder records the last day as a state on the person page from the person's words and nothing else, read back before saving, in the person's language.

Notice. The record says nothing. Her chapter's "how do you want to inform people, when will your last day be, how can I help you" (Book p.458), which she frames as letting people control what they still can, is right and is the house's whole ask: the person names their last day. The transparency sheet may state a notice expectation as a fact the person reads at the first interview, with its content and whether any consequence may attach to it counsel's (14); the house's position, recommended, is that no consequence attaches to a short notice, because pay is the days worked and the accrued share and there is no lever to pull (S13 P23), and because a person who leaves without notice has told the house something about the building the exit interview should hear, not something the house should punish. The Realist's read: a server who gives a day's notice costs the schedule a re-section and the adjacent nodes a week, which is real and is the tension-loss row's business, not a reason to withhold anything.

What the house does the same day. The stack writes the last day and the departure state; the domain lead writes field 15; the pool is read and the reading recorded on the sheet (S5 P60); the tension-loss row goes to the leads' review's line (S9 P30); the brief at every open period speaks coverage once at the next brief after the last day, and as the designations' line while the seat is open (S11 P25); the exit interview is offered for the last scheduled shift (5.4); the pay page shows the final pay's composition by the next morning as it always does (S13 P24); the Operations Lead's domain records what counsel requires of the employment record (14). Nothing is said at any room about the person, and the departing person's own goodbye, if they choose one, is theirs in person on a shift they work, recorded nowhere (S11 P25).

A person who leaves and returns. Their person page closed with them and is held as counsel requires; they enter the applicant system as a candidate like anyone, with their fluency record and readiness forms readable to the seat's stages only with their consent, which the applicant system asks; the record's "a candidate treated well at the no returns to the pool next year" (WP p.18) is read as applying to the person who left well, and the house holds nothing against a return.

**P21 (Recommended).** A departure the person chooses is said to their check-in holder at any time, recorded as a last day from the person's words, and asked no reason. No consequence attaches to short notice, and any notice expectation on the sheet is a fact with counsel's content. The same day: the departure state, field 15, the pool read, the leads' review line, coverage at the next brief, the exit interview offered, the pay page, and the employment record's items. A person who returns is a candidate like anyone, with their prior records read only with consent.

### 4.3 The second kind: the decision about staying, designed

The decision is a reserved class under the interim two-partner rule (`86akh5u77`), both partners or neither, the status quo as default, taken at the partners' review with counsel (S14 P26), and Session 14 recommended adding it to the reserved-class list (`86akh5u8m`). This section designs what happens inside that class. The status quo is that the person stays.

The inputs, and nothing else. The hand-off package (S14 I9): the window block whole, the pool and inventory read that showed which seats were open, and the date; or the non-negotiable line, the flag's record, and the person's own words. The partners may not read, and the package may not carry, a review record, an evidence line, a scorecard, a reset record, a coaching record, a hiring packet, the reach read, a recognition, a channel item, a designation count, a work style placement, a lagging figure, or a lead's view (S14 5.8). A lead may bring the package and may not recommend (S14 12.3). No partner's impression, no founder's floor presence, no customer's complaint, no other person's account is a source. The partners may never reach back into the window to shorten, end, or open one (S14 P21).

Who is present. Both partners; counsel; and no one else. Not the lead, not the co-lead, not the person. The person is not present because the decision is on records the person has read and written, and a person asked to argue for their own staying in a room with the partners has been put on trial, which the record's dignity forbids and canon's "present without performing" forbids from the other side. The person's words are in the package and are read aloud.

The order, which is the partners' card (I3). First, the mode line: before any person-side content of the package is read, the partners run the failure-mode reference on the center's own mechanisms as 2.8 states, from the package's building's fixes, the leads' review record, and the partners' page, and write an evidence line for the seat and the domain in the seven fields with no person named (S9 P3). This is `86akh5uy1`'s rule, that the failure-mode reference runs before any managing-out decision, made a step the form enforces. Where the mode line finds an unmade fix or an unread carrier, the item returns to the leads' review as an infrastructure line and no decision is taken; the window's not-held state stands and the person holds their seat; a later window opens by its own triggers or not at all (S14 P19). Second, the package or the line read whole, aloud, in the order the record holds it, with the person's words last. Third, counsel's read: counsel reads for what counsel must read and says what counsel must say about lawfulness, the record, the conversation, and the pay, and says nothing about the person's work, which is not counsel's to read. Fourth, the decision, both or neither.

What the partners may conclude. The person stays, and the seat is held as it is, with the not-held state or the line standing on the page as a dated fact and nothing added; this is the default and it is a real outcome, not a failure of nerve. The person stays, and the partners read the inventory again for a move the window did not surface because a seat opened since, offered to the person by their check-in holder as any move is (S14 P30). Or the person leaves, on a date the decision states. And never: a pay change of any kind (S13 P23); a probation, a warning, a "final" anything, or a second window opened by the partners; a label; a condition on staying that reads like a plan in the partners' voice; a designation withheld; a change of check-in holder as a consequence; anything about the person's will, manner, or life.

Speed. Her "fire fast" is refused as a speed a manager sets and absorbed as a rule of the mechanism: a package or a line is on the agenda of the next partners' review after it lands, and a decision not taken at that review is held to the following one with a reason written on the partners' page (counsel's read outstanding, the mode line's fix pending, a partner absent), and never held past two reviews without the item returning to the leads' review as a record fault. The record's own "speed is respect" (WP p.18) is the reason: a person whose staying is under decision for a month is a person the house is disposing of slowly.

How it is recorded, and in whose voice. On the partners' page as a versioned decision in a closed form (I3): the item's citation (the package's date or the line's date); the mode line's citation; counsel's presence by name; the decision as a state (stays; stays with a move offered; leaves, with the date); the date; and nothing else. No narrative, no reasons in a partner's voice, no severity, no account of the person. It is the reserved-class decision log's entry like any other (S4 9.2). On the person page the stack writes the state: "departure, the house's decision, [date], [citation]," in the person's language, read back to the person at the conversation before it is saved, with the person's own words beside it if they choose. In the leads' review record, a count: decisions taken per period, by kind, and never a name.

**P22 (Recommended).** The decision about staying is designed as a reserved class: inputs only the package or the line with the flag's record and the person's words; present both partners and counsel and no one else; the order the failure-mode reference on the center's mechanisms first with an unmade fix returning the item, then the package whole with the person's words last, then counsel's read on lawfulness only, then both or neither with the status quo as staying. It is on the next partners' review's agenda, held at most one review with a reason written, and recorded on the partners' page in a closed form in no one's voice about the person, with a state on the person page and a count on the leads' review record.

### 4.5 The third kind: a reduction that is not about any person

What it is. A period that thins its schedule (a slow season's shape, the Realist's operating fact), a period that closes for a season or for good under the charter rule (S4 8.1; WP p.36), a seat retired because its row has held no person and its tension has been re-established (S8 18.2), or hours reduced across a period. The order: the structure change entry, proposed through the leads' review and versioned by the partners' review, with its reason in the record's terms (the period closed by charter rule, the class that resolved to nothing) and never a person (S8 17.1; S9 P26); every open move read from the inventory and the pool and offered to the holder by their check-in holder, including a seat in another period, the other strand, another domain by the domain plan, and a return to the pool with their horizon held for a seat that opens; and, where none is open or the person declines them all inside a stated count of services, a reset parameter, the departure of the third kind, decided by the partners as the Class A structure change it already is, with counsel, and recorded as the second kind is recorded, with the entry's citation in place of a package's. The person is first in the pool's read for any seat that opens in the next stated count of months, a reset parameter, and the house tells them so in writing at the conversation; what may be promised, and whether a re-hire priority is lawful in the form the house wants, is counsel's. Her "a layoff is not a firing; it's a role elimination" (Book p.461) is right and is why the record on the person page says what was retired and never what the person did.
