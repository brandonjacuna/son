### 86ajgnhh3 | Work style demand differs by daypart: one labor pool, four profiles
Carryover. Status: to do.

**Routed to: S5 Recruiting (****`86ajgmhm4`****), S6 Hiring (****`86ajgmhnt`****), S8 Team structures (****`86ajgmhxx`****)**

Her work style grid (introvert/extrovert against task/people oriented, producing Analyzer, Director, Collaborator, Promoter) is a self-awareness tool. Used as a team-composition tool in a restaurant it produces a specific, predictable distortion: service pressure selects hard for Director and Promoter. A floor staffed by pattern-matching to the room ends up with almost no Analyzers, at which point nothing gets measured and no one notices.

The four dayparts make this sharper. They do not demand the same profile.

*   **Good Energy, morning.** Repetition, precision, prep, low interaction density, high consequence for small errors. Analyzer and Collaborator work.
*   **Dosi, lunch.** Throughput under a clock. Director work.
*   **Dinner.** The full range. The only daypart that genuinely wants all four.
*   **Luxx, late night.** Energy, presence, the room. Promoter work.

A single house running four dayparts is not staffing one work-style profile. It is staffing four, from one labor pool, with substantial overlap between them.
