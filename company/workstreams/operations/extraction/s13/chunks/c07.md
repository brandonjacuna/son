

### 16.4 Setting or changing a parameter of the architecture

Owner. The founders, as the partners' review, with Dominic's domain supplying each value against the financial model and counsel bounding the wage, rhythm, and closed-day rows; the chef partner for the kitchen seats' weights.

Range. May set any row of the parameters register (P26) by versioned decision; may change a row at the reset or on a versioned decision between resets. May not set a row by one partner alone; may not set a value that a review, a check-in, a designation, a recognition, or a lead's judgment reads; may not set a per-period pool; may not set a training weight below entry without ratifying the alternative in 11.4; may not change a seat's weight for one person.

Triggers. A change to any row reaches every affected person by the check-in, the sheet's version, the pay page, and the mechanics page before the pay date it first applies to (S8 P49; S11 P15). A change that an item 6 already handed to a candidate does not carry goes to the offer as a stated change (S5 I5). The team recommends that setting or changing an architecture parameter is a reserved class under the interim two-partner rule, both or neither with the status quo as default (`86akh5u77`, `86akh5u8m`), because a pay decision made by one partner in the other's absence is the "decision made by a person standing above" (S3 3.4) applied to the whole roster. Incomplete on the reserved-class ratification and on the kitchen seats until the chef ruling.

### 16.5 Moving a person's points

Owner. The stack, on a record.

Range. May move a weight on the date a domain lead records a readiness form reading ready at the next horizon (S8 P37); may turn a marker on when the fluency record shows its conditions met inside the window and off when the window lapses; may turn the teaching marker on at a mentored plan's first paid hour and off at its close; may count a day on a scheduled shift worked. May not move anything on a check-in, a review, a designation, a recognition, a lead's request, a founder's request, or a person's request; may not move a weight down except on a seat change the person made or a window lapsing.

Triggers. A form recorded and a weight not moved by the next pay page goes to the correction path. A weight moved with no form behind it goes to the Operations Lead's record as a system fault and to the partners' review the same week. Complete.

### 16.6 Correcting a pay error

Owner. The Operations Lead's domain, which holds "Cash, payroll, HR, compliance... in software" (WP p.22).

Range. May correct a day not counted, a weight not moved, a marker not on, a base hour missed, and a share miscomputed, on the records that show it, and pay the correction on the next pay date; may decline an item with a written reason. May not change a weight's value, a marker's conditions, or a day's definition to make a correction; may not correct on anyone's word without a record; may not read a person's pay page for any purpose but the correction; may not carry the correction's content anywhere but the item and the pay page.

Triggers. Every item is acknowledged with an owner within seven days and corrected or declined within thirty (S4 P30); an item past thirty days is a standing line at the house review. A correction whose cause is a record's fault (the schedule, the form, the point-of-sale record) goes to the capture review as a process update. A pattern of corrections on one mechanism goes to the leads' review as a mechanism line, never as a person's. Complete.

### 16.7 Answering a pay question

Owner. The destinations page: the pay page, the correction path, the mechanics page, the channel, the check-in for the path.

Range. As 14.4 and 14.5 state: what may be said, by whom, and what may never be. Complete.

### 16.8 The parity read

Owner. Founder-gated with counsel: the founders own whether it runs and on what tags; counsel bounds the data; the Operations Lead holds the record; the reset reads it.

Range. May read the unlock rate, the marker rate, and the days scheduled per seat class by the reach read's tags with no name and no figure; may trigger the reach read's existing acts on the sheet's sourcing and the calibration read's on the assessor pool. May not read a person, a target, a pay figure, or a review; may not act on a candidate, a person, or a bar; may not be a performance input.

Triggers. Incomplete: the tags, the data, and the act are founder-gated with counsel.

### 16.9 Setting the generosity range's value and the recovery threshold

Owner. Dominic's domain.

Range. May set each value against the financial model and version it on the range card; may change it at the reset or by versioned decision. May not set it below a value that makes the range unusable in kind, since the bottle is the record's (WP p.26); may not set it per person or per service; may not withhold it from the holder's card.

Triggers. A change reaches the holder by the check-in and the sheet's version before the brief. Under-use read at the reset goes to the range's design before it goes anywhere else. Complete for the mechanism; the values are founder-gated.
