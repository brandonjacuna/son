## 20. The session task's answer

**The review system, on one page.** A person at Sŏn is reviewed every six months on their own clock, the first time six months after their first paid hour, in a paid thirty-minute block the stack places in a pre-service window with slack or at a closed hour, by the lead who holds their check-in, in their chosen language, with no one else in the room. The review opens on the person page whole: every check-in line since the last review, the path line and the track, the progression state, the connections, the range-used count, the person's own captures, whether a non-negotiable line exists, and what the building supplied. The stack assembles it before the block and the lead adds nothing. It reads the four dimensions Session 6 fixed as states from those records and never as marks: the horizon held and the next unlock's date; the advocate's record as a list; the range used, exceeded, unused, or escalated, and the fact of a non-negotiable line or none; and, per item the person named, whether the building supplied it. It writes one record in two halves, the stack's states with citation dates and the person's own half in their words, and the lead writes nothing in their own voice. The lead reads the record aloud as specific acts on specific services, which is the formal half of "you really have talent." The person's read of the lead and the house is asked as a request for something to change, repeated back, and written only if they want it. A lead holding something not on a record says nothing of it at the review and asks the stack to place a check-in. The review ends with a read-back and one question, whether anything said was not on the page; "something new" is a candor line on the lead's entry and never a read of the person. Nothing in a review reaches pay, advancement, a managing-out decision, or anyone beyond the person and their lead. It may never become a verdict, a rating, a rank, a nomination, a file, a read of Nunchi or Jeong, or a room. Her self-assessment survives as the person's half; her peer reviews, manager narrative, designations, and calibration are refused, each with the mechanism that does its work. The Maitre d holds every floor review and the load is read as counts; a lead's review waits on `86akhb2jg`; the kitchen's is chef-gated with everything else recommended unchanged.

**The compensation architecture, in one paragraph.** Every person on the roster holds two layers on one pay date: base pay (an hourly wage per edge seat or a salary per salaried seat, the same for everyone in the seat, which the record never describes and everywhere assumes) and the pool (the day's built-in share, a defined proportion of a defined base that must not shrink when the generosity range is used, computed by the stack at every close, one pool per calendar day for the whole house across every open period). A person's points on a day are set by four things on records they can read and by nothing else: the seat's published weight at the horizon held, moved by a recorded unlock on its date; the cross-strand, cross-period, and teaching markers while current; the days worked, a day being a calendar day with any scheduled shift, counted once; and the salaried seat's every-open-day rule. Never by a review, a check-in, a rating, a designation, a recognition, any judgment of a person, a lagging indicator, a customer's rating, a sales attribution, hours, tenure, or a negotiation. The share is on the person's pay page by the next morning in their language and is paid with base pay on the same pay date. Mentoring is a marker while a plan is open, authorship and translation are flat payments per approved version, completion earns through shift pay and the unlock, and training pays base plus the seat's entry weight from the first paid hour. Every value (the percentage, the base, the weights, the markers, the wages and salaries, the rhythm, the range's value and the recovery threshold, the benefits gate) is named on a register with its owner and is unset in this program, because financials are not a context source. The person can see the whole arithmetic of their own share, and whether the day's pool total is on it is the founders' under the transparency line.

**The task's premise, in one paragraph.** Her compensation machinery does not transfer as form: no bands, no equity, no bonus, no merit cycle, no rating, no calibration, no raise conversation. Two of its three principles transfer as function: legible, because the pay page and the mechanics page let a person compute and explain their own pay; defensible, because nothing in the computation is anyone's opinion of anyone. The third does not transfer and is refused by design: at Sŏn compensation is not connected to the review, because the record's review is not a verdict, the check-in refuses pay, and no feedback act touches pay; the review is trustworthy because it cannot move a number, and the pay is legible because no review can move it. What carries her "pay for performance" is the unlock, the markers, the pool's weighting, and the days. The task text's figure and excluded source were not used; the record's own word, "still being finalized," is the one this page follows.
