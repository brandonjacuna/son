

The career conversation at Sŏn is one scheduled conversation, placed by the stack in the month after the plan closes and before the person's first review, which matches her timing exactly and the record's review interval. It is paid as shift, scheduled as a block in a pre-service window with slack or at a closed hour, longer than the check-in and shorter than a shift's first hour; its length is a parameter for the reset, set at thirty minutes to start. It is between the person and the domain lead; for a lead, its holder is whoever holds a lead's check-in (`86akhb2jg`). The kitchen's is chef-gated with the kitchen's check-in (S4 P16).

It opens on the person page's path line, the one the fifth check-in wrote in the person's words, and on four documents the person can already read: the seat sheet's field 6 horizons and item 7's advancement path written as outward movement (S8 P30); the tracks, visible from day one including another domain's (S7 P39); the fluency record, where cross-strand and cross-period fluency are connections demonstrated by readiness forms and never asserted (S8 P35, I7); and the seat after theirs, named by them. It adds nothing the person has not heard: no reading of them, no lead's view of their potential, no comparison.

What it does: re-reads the path line with the person and lets them re-write it; names, in the person's words, one track they want next, inside the two strands and the open periods, which is their one goal's development form (S4's count of one per person); names what the building must supply for that track (a form placed on the schedule, a module in their language, a paired service in the other strand), which becomes a training-infrastructure line if the building has not supplied it; and records the mentor relationship's shape after the plan as the person states it, which Session 10 designs.

What it refuses. A five-year plan, "which causes most of us to shut down" (Book p.295), and the record's own rule against asserting the unproven. A title, a level, a rank, or a promise of a seat, because Sŏn has no levels and no ladders and advancement is the advocate's (S8 P30). A promise of pay, which is Session 13's and never a lead's. A date beyond the sheet's unlock period. The person's history before the house: "where you grew up," "where you went to school," "what you did right after college" (Workbook p.91) are refused as questions, because the hiring rule refuses years of experience and credentials as reads (S5 P13), the seat sheet never asked for them, and a lead asking about family and origins after hiring is the intrusion the non-negotiables exist to forbid; if the person brings their own history, it is theirs to bring and is not written. Development goals in the lead's words: the wrap-up's "write down two or three development goals" (Workbook p.92) is refused as the lead's act; the person names one track and it is written in their words and read back. Any use as an input to delegation, placement, or a designation; the record's placement rule is the seat's mark and a complete form (S8 18.9), not a conversation.

Her workbook's questions, translated where they can be: "your favorite job... least favorite" becomes "what work do you want more of here, and less of," inside the strands and periods, which is a question about the seat and not the past; "what sort of work do you see yourself doing in the future" becomes "which seat after this one, which strand, which period," never a title; "who are the people you'll be working with, where will you be living" is refused. Her three purposes: "establishing that you care" is refused as a purpose, because care at Sŏn is the mechanism that advocates without a lead's attention, not a performed conversation; "the person's narrative arc" becomes the person's account of what they want next, inside the house's tracks; "a longer-term career direction that you can both refer to" becomes the path line on the person page, which the check-in re-reads whenever the stack shows a new unlock or a period opens.

### 15.3 Rhythm, and its relation to the review and the diagnostic

Once, after the plan. After that the path is re-read at the check-in, not re-held as a conversation, when the stack shows an unlock scheduled, when a period's charter is ruled and the period plan opens, and when the person asks. The review every three to six months (S13's) opens on the person page and finds the path line there, which is how "no surprises" holds for the path as it does for everything else (S4 P18). The diagnostic reads the conversation's outputs only as building lines: what the building has not supplied for a named track is an infrastructure line, and the same track unsupplied for the same reason across seats is skill invisibility. A career conversation that surfaces a non-negotiable goes to both leads by the flag rule; one that surfaces a friction goes to the infrastructure line; one that names another domain's track goes to that domain's lead through the person's own lead, as Session 7's cross-domain rule states (S7 P39, P40).
