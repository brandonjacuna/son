## Write two files

### `manual/2.1-founding-documents/session.md`

**1. Orientation** (short): what this chunk covers in plain words; what it depends on upstream (nothing, for 2.1, apart from the operating agreement); who else has to agree to what (Dominic, the chef partner); and why this chunk comes first in the build.

**2. Agenda**: the conversations in a sensible default order. Group decisions that belong in one conversation. Note which deliverables fall out of which conversation. The agenda is a default; Brandon may start anywhere.

**3. One brief per decision.** Cover every Decision task in `tasks.md`, and every Deliverable or Action that needs Brandon's input before it can be drafted. For each:

```
## 2.1.N <task title>

**The question:** one sentence.

**Why it matters now:** what this unblocks (task numbers in this chunk and others), and what goes wrong if it's skipped, rushed, or answered vaguely. Two to four lines.

**Openers:** three to five open questions that get Brandon talking or brain-dumping. Conversational, specific to Sŏn, not generic coaching questions. Include at least one that comes at it sideways (a story, a moment, a counterexample).

**Narrowing questions:** two to four questions that turn a dump into a choice.

**What the book says:** two or three lines, with pages.

**White-paper default:** the default assumption from tasks.md, and how solid it is: stated outright in the white paper, or inferred from it. None if none.

**How others have handled it:** two to four short points on how established hospitality companies or other well-run companies have approached this decision. Use only well-known, verifiable practice, and label the section "General practice, not Sŏn-specific." Name a company only when you're confident of the fact; otherwise describe the pattern without a name. No figures. If you aren't confident there's useful outside practice, say so in one line rather than inventing some.

**Options:** the real choices, one line each, with what each commits Sŏn to. Condensed from considerations.md; point there for depth.

**Watch for:** one to three failure modes: the ways this decision usually goes wrong.

**A finished answer:** the test Brandon's answer has to pass to count as done. Concrete.

**Needs agreement from:** Brandon alone, both seated founders, or all founders including the chef partner.
```

**4. Deliverables that follow**: for each Deliverable task, what the team can draft after the session, and exactly which decisions and inputs from Brandon it needs.

**5. Parking lot**: questions likely to come up that belong to other chunks (e.g. compensation mechanics in 5.5, metrics in 2.2), so the facilitator can name the chunk and park the question rather than chase it.
