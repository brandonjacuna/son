# 4.7 Diversity and inclusion: the work

The book treats diversity, equity, inclusion, and belonging as a manager's job in three places: hiring; performance assessment, reward, and recognition; and running teams (pp. 354 to 359). The white paper holds inclusion mechanics (the interview language, the paid practical, the question whether the building can create what a person needs) and states no position on who the team should be. This chunk asks the founders whether Sŏn states one, whether it ever collects identity data, and how the house's everyday mechanisms give everyone the same chance to be heard, chosen, and advanced. The hiring conduct statement is 3.1.7's; the pipeline report is 3.1.37's; this chunk decides what they are for.
