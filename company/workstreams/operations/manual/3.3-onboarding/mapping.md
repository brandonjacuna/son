## 3. What was dropped and why

Three kinds of material left the working manual. First, brand-dependent material: the uniform garment tables and their two conflicting versions; the arrival choreography and spatial sequence; the brand's register matrix, voice tests, and forbidden-lexicon list; the rule against glossing Korean terms and the Hangul display rules; the anti-concealment principle as the ground for the synthesized-media and phone-voice questions (the questions themselves survive on white-paper grounds); the three non-negotiables as a handbook stand-in; the brand-specific vocabulary for judgment and hospitality; the photography rule; and "canon's owner" as a module approver. The candidate- and employee-facing writing register (86akh7r2t) is dropped whole, since it exists only as a gap in the brand's voice rules. Second, program machinery: the documentation-methodology gate on eight instruments (the instruments survive); the marks and their ratification language; carryover routing, readiness-row numbering, and cross-session references; the ten-entry numbering of the person page; the instrument catalogue and the positions register; and "urgent" and "high priority" flags that encoded the old program's sequencing. Third, the private vocabulary: "felt knowledge" becomes "tasted knowledge," "the why block" becomes "the why session," "the charter" becomes the founding documents, and "the record" is replaced with the white paper or the prior decision it meant. Nothing that stands without brand support was removed: the two-wave cohort, the pre-opening training services, the readiness read by an assessor who is not the mentor, the five checkpoints for the decision-rights range, the training-infrastructure line, the onboarding read, the refusals on what onboarding may conclude, and the rejections of parts of the book's leader program all remain, restated as options.
