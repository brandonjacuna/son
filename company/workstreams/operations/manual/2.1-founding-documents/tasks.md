# 2.1 Founding documents: the work

Every decision, action, and deliverable this section of the book calls for, in the order they would naturally be done. Reasoning and options are in `considerations.md`; the book's guidance is in `book.md`. "WP p. N" is the white paper. A deliverable marked "Repeatable" is one others will later produce for themselves; building it with Brandon is also how the process gets built for everyone else, and each such deliverable has a paired kit task.

### 2.1.1 Read the operating agreement and note what it already settles
- Type: Action
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: pp. 71 to 72 (founding documents give every system a common purpose; they cannot contradict the legal instrument beneath them)
- Default assumption: None; the white paper is silent on the operating agreement
- Depends on: None
- Done when: a short note exists listing what the agreement holds on votes, deadlock, admission of a new principal, a principal's departure or incapacity, and ownership of domains, so the founding documents can defer to it by reference rather than restate or contradict it
- Replaces old items: 86akh3t62

### 2.1.2 Decide Sŏn's mission and the entity it belongs to
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: pp. 73 to 76 (state why you exist; uniquely specific, never fully achieved; every layer ladders up to it)
- Default assumption: (WP pp. 34, 36) the mission describes the company, whose asset is the connective layer, not the restaurant alone; Sŏn is the only brand the customer meets and the parent stays in the background. No default for the sentence itself; the white paper holds a thesis, not a mission
- Depends on: None
- Done when: one sentence exists that passes her two tests and the white paper's two (never assert the unproven, WP p. 36; not the restaurant alone, WP p. 34), with the entity stated and the cuisine question answered either way; and, if the company holds the mission, the restaurant's own sentence beneath it exists too (the ladder, book p. 75), or is listed as an open item with a closer; agreed by both seated founders
- Replaces old items: 86akh3tdr

### 2.1.3 Choose the long-term goals
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: pp. 76 to 77 (a short list of ambitions over years, stable, the parent of every quarterly and annual goal)
- Default assumption: (WP pp. 06, 33, 36, 38) the three compounding engines; a company built to run without its founders; one restaurant coherent across the day with dinner protected; the horizon of further concepts in category terms only, gated on proof. The proof that the layer moves a customer from the morning to late night (WP p. 35) is a candidate test of the goals, not a goal; chunk 2.2 decides whether it becomes a metric
- Depends on: 2.1.2
- Done when: a list short enough for one page exists, without figures or lagging indicators (WP p. 22), and every metric chunk 2.2 later sets can name its parent here
- Replaces old items: None

### 2.1.4 Decide whether "bandwidth, then joy, then retention, then results" is a rule or a description
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: pp. 77 to 78 (principles must be deliverable and connected to how the company actually operates)
- Default assumption: (WP pp. 12, 17) it is a rule: "This is mechanical, not aspirational." As a rule it decides trade-offs on a real night: bandwidth wins over results when they conflict
- Depends on: None
- Done when: the founders have stated which reading holds, with the operating consequences of the rule reading (reduce covers rather than thin the floor; restructure a period that consumes bandwidth before defending it on results) accepted or declined; agreed by both seated founders
- Replaces old items: 86akh3t00

### 2.1.5 Write the principles, version one
- Type: Deliverable
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: pp. 77 to 79 and 145 to 147 (relevant, believable, enduring, deliverable; drafted from moments, reviewed by the founder, circulated for comment; expressed in how the company hires, rewards, leads)
- Default assumption: (WP pp. 07 to 08, 11 to 14, 16 to 21, 25 to 26, 33, 36) version one is drawn from the white paper's stated tenets, listed in `considerations.md` section 4, because Sŏn has decisions and no moments. The white paper names "no hierarchy of importance" as an operating principle in its own words (WP p. 20). People and compensation commitments (labor as asset, pay for what matters, compensation built into price and shared with the kitchen, benefits from the start, WP pp. 16, 19 to 20) are stated here as commitments; every mechanism belongs to 5.5
- Depends on: 2.1.2, 2.1.4
- Done when: a short set exists, each item traceable to a decision in the record or one the founders make here, each marked as a principle (an observable behavior), a commitment, or an operating rule; version one marked pre-opening; the chef partner invited to add from the culinary domain once seated; the commitment list handed to 2.1.6
- Replaces old items: 86akh2qbc, 86akh3t9p, 17tn048qck3, 86akh3tg0, 86akh674t
