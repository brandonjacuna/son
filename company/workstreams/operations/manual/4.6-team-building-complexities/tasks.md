# 4.6 Team-building complexities: the work

The book's section covers distributed teams, going global, adding remote workers, underperforming teams, working with other teams, and managing through uncertainty (pp. 331 to 353), with the chapter's exercises appended (career conversations, offsite planning, leadership snippets, the unblocking process). At Sŏn the distributed team is distributed by the hour, not by geography: a shift's crew is gone before the next one arrives, and the two leads and the founders may rarely share a service once more than one period is open. The unblocking process and the crisis line already have homes (4.5.10, 4.1.30); this chunk decides what remains.

### 4.6.12 Decide the course-correction sequence when a unit misses its goals
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Before opening
- Book: pp. 345 to 346 (investigate the root cause in 1:1s with a hypothesis aimed at the work; then an open, curious team conversation: why are we behind, what is in our control, what can we do now, how much do the external reasons weigh)
- Default assumption (WP pp. 14, 17): "blame the process, fix the process"; "before intervening at the people level, identify which failure mode is actually present"; post-mortems are private and process-focused. The white paper never describes a conversation with a whole unit about a missed goal
- Depends on: 2.2.17, 2.2.21, 4.2.2, 4.2.10, 4.5.13, 2.3.19
- Done when: the trigger is a count (services or weeks a unit's committed goal is missed), the sequence is written in order (the lead's read against the failure-mode reference, check-ins with a hypothesis about the work, then a conversation with the unit or a decision that no group conversation runs), where a unit conversation happens if it does (never the brief, which carries nothing about performance under 4.5.13; a paid block, or the lead's read plus check-ins), the four questions are kept or reworded in Sŏn's terms, and the outcome lands as owned actions on the leads' review; the kitchen's version is marked for the chef partner
- Replaces old items: None
