# 5.4 The formal review process: the work

The book's point (pp. 399 to 413): continuous feedback does not replace a written, formal review; people trust a system only when they know how they are measured, that it is applied fairly, and how results connect to recognition. The white paper already fixes the review's temperament: every three to six months, about development and the path forward "rather than a verdict," never containing new information, running in both directions (WP p. 19). This chunk decides what that review is, what it reads, who holds it, what it writes, and what, if anything, it feeds.

### 5.4.4 Decide whether the review carries a performance designation, and on what scale

### 5.4.5 Decide what the review may feed: advancement, pay, a separation record, or nothing outside itself
- Type: Decision

- Default assumption (WP pp. 11, 19): advancement runs on its own mechanism ("no one's advancement waits on a manager's attention or judgment"; a competency conversation with the appropriate lead when ready); pay runs on a point system weighted by days worked (WP p. 20). The old work's option: the review feeds nothing outside itself: no advancement input (4.1.11 has its own three seats), no pay input by any route, no file used to justify a separation (5.2.4 and 5.10 build their own record), because a review that decides pay is heard as the number first and cannot also contain no news

- Replaces old items: 17tn048qep9 (the refusal-list half), 17tn048qcjg (the feedback-never-pay half)

### 5.4.12 Decide how "no surprises" is made a mechanism, and what a surprise becomes
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Hiring and training
- Book: p. 401 (if the manager has managed well, the written review holds no surprises; it documents conversations already had); pp. 405 to 407 (send the review a day ahead; when someone pushes back, acknowledge and redirect to next time)
- Default assumption (WP p. 19): "The governing rule is no surprises: continuous communication means a formal review never contains new information. - Depends on: 5.4.8, 5.4.11, 2.3.9, 5.2.7
- Done when: the rule is written as steps a holder follows, not as an aspiration; what a holder does with an observation that is not on any record is named (the next check-in, never the review); whether the closing question exists and where its answer goes (the holder's load and candor counts on the leads' review, 2.3.19); whether a strength not yet named is treated the same way as a concern; agreed by both seated founders
- Replaces old items: 17tn048qep9 (the mechanism half), 17tn048qcjg (the candor half)
