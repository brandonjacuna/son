# 5.1 Hypothesis-based coaching: the work

The book's coaching method (pp. 382 to 391): gather the data you already have, form a hypothesis about a person's strengths and gaps, and test it by saying it to them as a theory about behavior and its effect. At Sŏn this runs inside the check-in that 2.3 designed, from records the person can already read, so that the review's "no surprises" rule (WP p. 19) is true in practice and not only in the white paper.
