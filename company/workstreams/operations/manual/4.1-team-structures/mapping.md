## 3. What was dropped and why

Three kinds of material left the working manual. First, brand-dependent material: the two canon uniform-role tables and every reconciliation against them, so the seat inventory's default rests on the white paper's list alone; canon's "floor manager" wording and the canon change routed from the ruling; the customer-facing reading of the threshold and the maître d's name, which go to the naming session the white paper lists as open (WP p. 15); the sonic governance tiers; and the visual design of the web and who owns it (the structural content it carries survives in 4.1.18). Second, program machinery: the Recommended, Founder-gated, Chef-gated, and Team-filled marks and the marks register; readiness-row numbers and blocking flags; carryover routings, session and paragraph cross-references, the documentation-system gate on instruments, instrument numbering, "Class A" labels; and the diagnosis-to-versioned-change workflow, of which only the logged-versus-versioned distinction survives in 4.1.12. Third, financial content: the pricing of salaried seats, lead entry pay, fluency pay, parity reads, and automatic pay moves, all routed to 5.5 as structure-free compensation work, with no figures carried. The work-style-by-period grid is not a task, since the old page itself refused it as a tool; its substance lives in each period's charter. Nothing that survives without brand support was removed: the one-team test by interfaces, the single-lead reporting rule, the room, door, and sequence designations, the two-webs reading of entry, the salaried test, the fluency definitions, the three-role advancement split, the kitchen spine, the change entry, and the refusal of layering all remain, restated as options.
