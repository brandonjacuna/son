# 4.1 Team structures: the work

Every decision, action, and deliverable this section of the book calls for, in the order they would naturally be done. Reasoning and options are in `considerations.md`; the book's guidance is in `book.md`. "WP p. N" is the white paper. A deliverable marked "Repeatable" is one others will later produce for themselves; building it with Brandon is also how the process gets built for everyone else, and each such deliverable has a paired kit task.

In this chunk a "seat" is a position in the structure (a row in the seat inventory), a "leadership line" is a seat that owns a domain across every open service period, a "domain" is a set of seats with one lead, a "strand" is the outward-facing or inward-facing half of a domain (WP pp. 10 to 11), and a "designation" is a per-service assignment of a live function that lasts one shift (WP p. 10). The floor manager and the Maitre d are one seat: decided 2026-09-13 and recorded in 2.2's `decisions.md`; nothing here reopens it.

### 4.1.10 Define cross-strand fluency and cross-period fluency so each can be demonstrated

### 4.1.12 Decide how a structure change is made and how it reaches people
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: pp. 265, 280 to 281 (tell people up front the structure will change; a change is sketched without names, narrated before it is announced, and messaged to each affected person, with a plan to monitor it)
- Default assumption: (WP pp. 07 to 08, 17) knowledge lives in the system, not in a person; blame the process, fix the process; the white paper has no change process. The old work's option: every structure change (a seat created or retired, a line changed, a period's seats added, a designation's rule changed, a pool or salaried mark changed) is one versioned entry with its reason stated first, in structural terms (a tension lost, a disturbance class resolving to nothing, a period's gate, a ruled charter); proposed through the leads' review, finalized by the partners; it reaches people through the affected person's own check-in, a new version of the seat's sheet, the next shift brief, and the team home (2.2.13), never an announcement. A reduction's reason is never a person; a held seat is never retired as a retirement but handled as a departure or a reduction (5.10). The book's "care" versus "correction" distinction survives: a quick fix is logged, a structural fix is versioned
- Depends on: 4.1.3, 4.1.6
- Done when: the classes of change that need an entry are listed, the reason rule is accepted or amended, the review and finalizing steps are named, the four channels are accepted or changed, and the person-affected rule of the book (one-to-one message, acknowledge the loss, say how it will be monitored) is kept, adapted, or declined; agreed by both seated founders; reads: through the channels 2.2.11 names once decided
- Replaces old items: 86akhpubc (the care versus correction distinction; the rest is retired machinery), 17tn048qg2g
