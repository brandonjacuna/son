## 3. What was dropped and why

Three kinds of material left the working manual. First, brand-dependent material: the "Playground Philosophy" as a named obligation and its six-question test card; its two-edge language and its non-negotiables-versus-philosophy ranking; canon citations used to justify individual mechanisms (scripted-service prohibitions, floor authority, "present without performing," the forbidden lexicon applied to peer recognition, canon's pre-service timing); the customer-side coupling to brand service protocols; and the findings that set canon against the white paper. Second, program machinery: the retired marks, the document-methodology gate on every instrument, readiness-row numbering, carryover routings, and old-session cross-references. Third, material the project rules keep out: the founders' personal values, interiority, and working style, including the later comment on 86akht14m declining founders' values conversations, which is dropped because it records a position about Brandon's personal values. Nothing that survives on white-paper grounds was removed: the ten conditions, the destinations principle, the staff meal question, the brief's team slot, bandwidth as the first read, and the thinner-belonging finding all remain, restated as decisions and considerations.
