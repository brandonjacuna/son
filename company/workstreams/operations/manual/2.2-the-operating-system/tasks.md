# 2.2 The operating system: the work

Every decision, action, and deliverable this section of the book calls for, in the order they would naturally be done. Reasoning and options are in `considerations.md`; the book's guidance is in `book.md`. "WP p. N" is the white paper. A deliverable marked "Repeatable" is one others will later produce for themselves; building it with Brandon is also how the process gets built for everyone else, and each such deliverable has a paired kit task.

In this chunk "the operating system" is the human system the book describes; the software is "the stack" or "the OS surface." Rhythms (weekly, quarterly, the loop window, the reset) are chunk 2.3's; this chunk decides that a mechanism exists, who is in it, and what it reads.

### 2.2.1 Decide the units that carry goals, metrics, and review, and the direction goals ladder
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: pp. 84 to 86, 102 to 103, 115 (the operating system replicates from company to division to team to individual; goals and metrics replicate down)
- Default assumption: (WP pp. 07 to 11, 33 to 34) four units that intersect rather than nest: the company; the service period, where reason to exist and operating logic are answered; the domain, where authority and accountability live; the person, where development is held. No division and no team in the book's sense. Goals are set by the unit that holds the information and ladder up to a long-term goal (2.1.3), not replicated down. The white paper never states this; it is inferred from its structure
- Depends on: 2.1.3, 2.1.14
- Done when: the founders have named the units, stated the direction (up from the unit that holds the information, or down from the center), and ruled that the book's division and team are not used, or chosen the book's cascade or a hybrid on purpose with its cost accepted (`considerations.md` section 1); agreed by both seated founders
- Replaces old items: 86akh5up7, 86ajgmm5h

### 2.2.2 Decide the decision-rights rule beneath the partner level
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: pp. 121 to 122 (ownership must be defined early or culture suffers and politics follow; the book assumes the chart supplies the structure)
- Default assumption: (WP pp. 07 to 08, 10, 13, 26, 31) the shape every instance in the white paper shares: authority sits with the closest competent owner; the range inside which that owner acts without asking is stated in advance; the triggers that escalate are named before the event, each with a destination. Three operating rules attach: a trigger names its destination; a range states what the owner may not do as well as what they may; ranges are set one level out from the position that holds them. Any financial value in a range is set in Dominic's domain and not carried in this manual
- Depends on: 2.1.13, 2.2.1
- Done when: the founders have adopted the three-part form and the three operating rules, or amended them; stated that an entry missing a part is marked incomplete rather than filled by analogy; and stated that the leads' ranges are written by the founders before the leads are interviewed; agreed by both seated founders
- Replaces old items: 86akh3uez (received from 2.1), 86akh3tu6, 86akh5u87 (the rule half)
