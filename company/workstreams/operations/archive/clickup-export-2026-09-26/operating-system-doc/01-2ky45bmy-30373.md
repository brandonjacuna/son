# Index

## What this is

The company. How Sŏn runs, decides, hires, develops people, and holds itself to account.

This document is built one session at a time by the Scaling People translation program (parent task `86ajgmh9a`, tracker `2ky45bmy-17233`). Each session adds a page. At Session 17 the pages are restructured into the company wiki.

**This is not a summary of anyone's book.** It is Sŏn's operating system, written to stand on its own. The test at assembly: a new general manager should be able to run Sŏn from this document without ever hearing the name Claire Hughes Johnson.

## What lives here and what does not

**Here.** How the company operates. Decision rights, planning, cadence, hiring, onboarding, team structure, feedback, review, compensation architecture, separation.

**Not here.** Brand canon, which governs how Sŏn speaks, looks, and positions. Canon is the source of truth for anything brand, and it lives in two local files: `sources/brand-guidelines.md` for positioning, naming, voice, and lexicon, and `sources/brand-guidelines-deck.pdf` for the design system and experiential guidelines. This document pulls from canon. It does not restate it and it does not contradict it. Brand facts are never asserted from memory.

**Not here.** Financial figures. Financials are not a context source for this program. No figure is generated, estimated, or recalled into this document. Where a decision needs one, the gap is stated and flagged rather than filled.

**Not here.** Finished instruments. This document carries recommended positions and the specifications that describe what an instrument must contain, do, and refuse to do. Building the instrument itself is downstream work, gated on the document methodology (`86ajgn2z5`).

## Status of every position

Nothing in this document is marked final. Every position carries one of four marks until Brandon ratifies it.

| Mark | Meaning |
| ---| --- |
| Recommended | The team's position, ready for founder ratification |
| Chef-gated | Deliberately left open for the chef partner to shape. Not a gap, a structural position |
| Founder-gated | Requires founder input the team cannot supply: values, interiority, working style |
| Team-filled | Populated once the team exists |

A page is expected to change. Later chapters revise what earlier chapters produced, and Session 17 restructures all of it into the wiki. Ratification is Brandon's act, not a session's.

The governing rule: never present as landed what only the group can land. Founder-gated work is developed to the ceiling one founder can reach, argued, staked, then marked for what Dominic, the chef, or the team must close.

## Standing rules

The customer is the customer, never the guest. No em dashes. No performed conviction: never "we believe," "we hope," "our goal is," "we are committed to." Declarative over aspirational. Sentence case for all headings and labels. No daypart code names; dayparts are named by time of day or service period. Brand facts defer to canon. No financial figures. Profanity is spoken-only and never appears in writing.

## Two conventions this document holds

**"Operating system" means the human system.** Strategy, accountability, and the communication cadence. Sŏn also runs a software stack that the record calls an operating system, and the two are different things. In this document the software is always "the stack" or "the OS surface," never "the OS" or "the operating system" on its own. Set by S1, section 4.

**Sŏn compounds without headcount growth.** Where a page says "as the house grows," it means as the system holds more, never as headcount rises.

## A note on canon, added by S2

Canon is cited by version. The two canon artifacts on the allowlist state different six-tier authority hierarchies: `sources/brand-guidelines.md` is Version 1.0 and `sources/brand-guidelines-deck.pdf` is Version 3.0, and the deck ranks the company non-negotiables at tier 2 where the text version does not rank them at all. Until that is reconciled by versioned decision, a page in this document cites canon by version and marks any position that turns on the difference as held.

Canon also currently claims to be "the source of truth for every decision made under this name," which is broader than the split this index describes. The relationship between canon and the operating charter is founder-gated and is not settled by this index.
