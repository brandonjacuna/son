A small category label in the uppercase eyebrow register. Recessive, never loud.

```jsx
<Badge>Dinner</Badge>
<Badge variant="accent">Late night</Badge>
<Badge variant="solid">선</Badge>
```

Variants: `outline` (default hairline) · `accent` (theme accent rule) · `solid` (inverse fill). Text is always uppercase — this is one of the few sanctioned uppercase contexts.
