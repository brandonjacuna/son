**W1. Rule the chef seat, and with it which branch of third-seat recruitment runs.**
Type: founder decision
Body: The record disagrees with itself. The white paper holds the chef as "an equal founding partner" with an equal vote on whether a service period is built; both canon versions hold the seat as "[HELD], Pending Executive Chef Hire" with two named hiring criteria, the Mahk standard and the chef's relationship to Bangjja Yugi, Baekja, Buncheong, and Cheongja (page section 10.3, finding 15.7, P57). Branch A, partner: not recruiting at all, but a partnership negotiation belonging with the operating agreement, canon's criteria as the founders' due diligence rather than a scorecard, a cooking for the founders in a kitchen the candidate chooses as the last round, and three terms settled in writing (that the culinary identity is the brand's and the dishes are the chef's; that the chef partner's vote on a service period is equal and their vote on brand canon is not; that the seat sits in a code-bearing organization). Branch B, hire: the leadership process in full, canon's criteria as field 8 of the chef's seat sheet, a cooking as the paid practical, and two record artifacts rewritten (the lunch decision as an equal decision among all three partners, and the record's account of the founders). Under either branch the kitchen pipeline is gated in a fixed chain and the culinary identity is the brand's (P58). The kitchen practical's station, plate, mise standard, and rubric wait on the seated chef (P44, chef-gated). This is first in the order because the hiring timeline places the chef first, every kitchen row, sheet, and entry waits on it, and readiness row 59 holds dinner's gate closed until the ruling is filed. The ruling is already open as founder decision `86akh2qrp`, to be filed at `86akh3tw9`; this item is the recruitment design that turns on it.
