## One-screen summary

**The shape.** One program, one data model, several views. At the center is a **trunk core**: why the house exists, how to learn here, and the safety floor. Around it sit **thirteen disciplines**, each an area of knowledge or craft that anyone may study to any depth: movement and body; ensemble and critique; reading people and rooms; hosting the visit; tempo and pacing; the room's design and senses; flavor and perception; liquid craft; sources (Korean food and drink culture, proposed); decision craft; the house as a system; teaching, coaching and assessing; leading and managing. Back of house is a fourteenth region, drafted now under D30 and redeveloped by the executive chef. Every discipline has four **depths**, defined by what a person can do and never by who needs it: Foundation, Working, Deep, Student. A **role** is a required set: the disciplines and depths a person must hold to be released in that seat, plus its gates. Job descriptions render from the required set. Cross-training is walking to a neighboring discipline; a role move is a conversation.

**What the sky shows.** Capabilities, not modules. A star is a verb someone would be proud of on shift ("carries and sets down steadily at any tempo"); many modules feed one star, and a star lights only from the gate record or a proven conversation, never from completion. The daily view is the trunk plus one lit next step. The sky opens on zoom-out. A private "comb" shows a person's own depth per discipline against the faint outline of what their role requires, which is the job description drawn as a shape.

**How many.** The inventory holds the trunk core and thirteen disciplines, with a back-of-house draft region. Counts by group (rows with an ID, after the merges the red teams asked for) are given at the head of each group in section 3 and totaled there. Roughly a third of the rows are Foundation, half are Working, and the rest are Deep and Student. The number is not a ceiling; it is what the sweeps surfaced and the red teams left standing.

**The most important additions beyond the Phase 2 list.**
- Flavor and perception as a full discipline, foundation in the trunk, open to a porter or a host all the way to teaching the house (D28's own example).
- Perception as a trained skill at the entry seat: scanning, reading the plate, reading the table's state, reading the whole room, and a video club for learning to notice.
- Ensemble craft borrowed from fields that have solved it: call and callback, structured handoffs, the pair, the pass, invisible repair.
- Decision craft as Brandon's own way of deciding, made teachable: commander's intent, judging the call not the result, knowing when a gut call deserves trust, predicting and checking, and the Critical Decision Method that grows the decision library past Brandon.
- A teaching discipline that certifies teachers, assessors, coaches, facilitators and mentors, and a leading discipline that separates leading (open to anyone) from managing (designation-gated).
- Tempo and pacing as its own discipline, which is where Brandon's music pedagogy lives: dynamics, phrasing, rest, the door as the rhythm section, the late-night turn as one caller and one moment.
- Rhythms that never finish and always produce an output: the pre-shift decision case, the after-action review, studio class, studio nights, discipline stewards, and a Student tier that ends in a contribution the house keeps.

**The handful of calls Brandon needs to make** (section 7 has each with options): the shape and the visual; whether depth is defined by capability rather than by which role needs it; the discipline set (thirteen, or D29's seven with strands, and whether sources is a discipline); how outside credentials in wine, spirits and coffee are treated under D27; the trunk's pre-contact set; whether founders and managers are critiqued live; the pay counting and the transparency sheet before the pay units ship; back of house as a draft region against the computed brief's "shell".

## 1. Sŏn's teaching style, as understood

For Brandon to confirm or correct. Each line traces to the intake or a decision.

1. Training aligns people with how Brandon decides. Skill is hired-for capability that the house develops; the target on top of skill is decision-making like his, so the business runs without him in the room (intake group 7; D14).
2. Knowledge can be learned at a screen. Understanding is proven by talking, and practicals by doing at a table. Nobody is released by clicking through (intake groups 4 and 5; D8).
3. Physical macro movements are drilled apart from decisions: isolate the pattern, run it at many tempos until it is automatic, so attention goes to the decision. The two merge in performance (intake group 3).
4. A trunk of core modules, then endless branches organized by discipline, not by role. Interest unlocks depth; a short conversation unblocks a move; cross-training is designed in, never gritted through (intake group 2; D3, D28).
5. Learning is paid. Take-home practice is optional, invited and unpaid, and gates nothing (intake group 3; D7).
6. Efficacy decides length. One objective per critical-path unit with spaced recall; long-form off-path content is unrestricted (intake group 3; D15).
7. Everyone critiques everyone, across levels, as a normal culture, with two rituals kept apart: private incident review and open everyday feedback. Constant improvement is the principle; complacency and mere participation are the failure modes (intake groups 4 and 7; D22, D14).
8. Outsiders' views are applied to Sŏn through their lens. The material that teaches a goal does not have to come from hospitality; another subject can carry the point (intake group 8).
9. Sŏn is a hyper-informed late entrant. The program should be the best in the field and never locked into traditional thinking (WP; intake group 7).
10. Safety and the why come before any customer contact. Testing happens before impact, never after (intake group 4; D11).

**How this shapes the inventory.**
- Every row names a learning job (know, see, decide, do, hold, find, teach) and a proof that is a conversation, a drill check or a practical, never a screen check.
- Every movement row is a drill by Brandon's method, filed by movement family, with the decision it later merges with named.
- Every judgment row rests on Brandon's own decisions, elicited by interview, and keeps separate practice, gate and measure banks (D14).
- Every discipline opens to anyone by interest and ends in a Student tier that produces something the house keeps.
- Every outside carrier must earn its place by supplying a practice form or a cleaner instance of the idea; a carrier that is only an analogy moves to the library.
- The front-end check runs first: gaps that are really tools, conditions, tempo, policy or motivation are routed out (section 6).
- Complacency is designed against with rhythms that never finish and always produce an output, not with completion counts.

## 2. The program structure

### 2.1 Structure directive, and what the three red teams agreed

Brandon's directive (2026-09-28, recorded as D28): branches are disciplines, not roles; a foundation of each discipline belongs in the trunk; going all the way into a discipline as a student fits no role branch; roles become required sets; job descriptions render from them; better structures and visuals than the tree are welcome.

All three red teams found the merged inventory only half-escaped role pockets, and agreed on the fixes adopted here:
- **Depth is defined by what the person can do, never by which role requires it.** "Working means some role requires it" and "Deep means a requirement for leads" rebuilt the ladder under new names.
- **Stars are capabilities, not modules.** A sky of two hundred module-stars is the bloated web the skill-tree research names as failure mode one.
- **Hosting is cut by the customer's arc, not by door, table and counter.** Those become banks inside each moment.
- **Movement is cut by movement family** (carry, pour, build, clear and reset, walk, body at the table), with role banks inside each family.
- **Liquid craft's depth is principle-based** (extraction, fermentation, distillation, texture), not the guild certificate list.
- **Leading is separated from managing.** Leading (influence without authority, one improvement at a time, holding the standard as a peer) is open to anyone; managing (authority over people and scheduling, D5 scope) stays designation-gated.
- **The default view is a lit next step,** and the sky is the zoom-out.
- **The Student tier ends in a contribution,** not a reading list.

### 2.2 Depth, defined by capability

| Depth | Meaning | What lights it |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | Knows why it matters and recognizes it when it happens. The trunk holds every discipline's Foundation. | G1 item or a proven conversation |
| **Working** | Does it alone, at tempo, on a real full night. | The gate record (G2 to G4; D8, D10) |
| **Deep** | Handles the cases nobody wrote down, adjusts others, and can coach it. | Observed performance plus a conversation with the discipline's steward |
| **Student** | Extends the discipline: tests a claim, creates, writes a spec or protocol, authors a Foundation module, presents at a studio night. Teaches it to the house. Length is unrestricted. | The steward signs a contribution the house keeps; Brandon approves anything that becomes house standard |

Roles require depths; roles never define them. A barback can be Deep in reading people; no lead is Deep in anything by title. Radial distance on the sky is depth in one discipline, never height over another person (D3).

### 2.4 Recommendation: one data model, four views

1. **The grid (D) is the source of truth.** Every module carries `discipline`, `depth` and `routes`; role manifests hold required depth per discipline plus gates; job descriptions render from the manifests. It prints as the wall map, structure only. The Organizational Systems Architect reviews the `framework/module-spec.md` change (D18).
2. **The lit path is the daily view.** On a phone: the trunk, the person's own lit stars, and one next step with its booking link (D13). This is the Duolingo and Khan lesson and the floor red team's requirement for a green hire.
3. **The sky (A) is the explore view.** Capability stars, bridges, role doors and threads, locked crossings drawn as conversations. Three guardrails: a star lights only from the gate record or a proven conversation; no counts, streaks or totals (D12); a person's position is private and unshareable (D31). Alternative if the live page is refused or delayed: one constellation per discipline as tiles (the skill-tree research's Alternative A).
4. **The comb (E) is the personal and hiring view.** A person sees their own bars against their role's outline. The job description is the outline, written in catalog language (B): the required set as the "major", cross-training sets as "minors", depth always in words.

**Why.** The sky carries the culture (open depth, self-direction), the lit path carries the operation (critical path, gates), the comb carries the person and the hiring, and the grid keeps all three honest. Brandon picks the visual (D28); the recommendation is the sky with these guardrails.

### 2.6 The flow inside the structure

**The trunk.** The trunk core (why, how to learn, safety) plus every discipline's Foundation. It runs in layers, which are placement around a first whole task, not grades:
- **T0, remote and paid:** know and see only, each proven later in conversation. Held to the units whose absence could harm someone before release, or whose meaning is needed to make sense of the rest.
- **T1, on site before any customer contact:** the safety floor, the whole night seen before any part, the drill method, calls and speaking up, a short decision frame, the room walked.
- **T2, while branching, after first release:** the depth of the house's ideas, care, the business, critique upward, the outsider's lens. Spaced to the learner's shifts (D15).
- **T3, standing rhythms that never finish and always produce an output:** pre-shift, the after-action review, the prediction check, studio class, studio nights, video club, the panel series, teaching rotation.

The pre-contact set is deliberately lean (the floor and craft red teams; section 5). Whole task first: the first on-site act is a whole-service observation and, for everyone, sitting in the chair as a customer once (SVC-044, policy-gated).

**The disciplines and their depths.** Each discipline section in 3 lists Foundation, Working, Deep and Student rows. Foundation rows are trunk; Working rows are required by some role and open to all; Deep and Student rows are open by interest to anyone whose Foundation is lit, with only a conversation at the door. A few Deep rows are also required for leads and managers, which is a fact about the role, not about the depth.

**Stars.** Each discipline names its capability stars: a few per depth, each fed by several modules. Section 3 gives each discipline's stars at the head of its group. The signature star is the integrated performance at tempo; the drills and decisions that feed it draw in from both sides, so the picture teaches Brandon's split.

**Gates.** Every role's home nodes run the gate spine: G1 spoken check, G2 small-menu practical (drills banked, the integrated practical never banked, safety elements non-compensatory), G3 angel shift with the mentor, G4 the eyes-off window, then release, then rechecks on a rhythm (D8 to D11). Safety elements: SAF-001, SAF-004, BEV-014, SAF-009. Preconditions for any gate shift: CUL-002, CUL-006 for the learner and CUL-004 on the receiving floor (D9). Test-out by conversation or demonstration is allowed on any non-safety unit (section 5).

**Bridges and crossings.** Bridge stars open when both neighbors' Foundations are lit: palate calibration (FLV and LIQ), pacing (HOSP and TIME), the room tastes too (ROOM and FLV), one grammar for two bars (barista and bartender inside LIQ), the service bar handoff (ENS between service and beverage routes), fermentation (SRC and LIQ). A crossing for learning needs no key. A crossing for a role move is the unblock conversation, auto-booked on a talent block, ending in one of four recorded outcomes (D13, LEA-006). Named crossings: runner to back waiter to front server (in-route); host to runner or barback; front server to host and lead host and, over time, Maître d' (WP p.19); front server to beverage (learning through FLV and LIQ electives, or a move through barback); barback to bartender; barista and bartender (in-discipline); server to kitchen (the BOH region, chef-gated).

**Stewards.** Each discipline has a named steward: the house's deepest person in it, whatever their role. The steward keeps the discipline's modules current, runs its studio class, and signs Student contributions. Where the house has both an ideas person and a hands person in a discipline, pair them (the Bauhaus form master and craft master, VP). Pay is `founder.teaching_pay` (D6). This replaces the "discipline apprenticeship" mechanic with an owner.

### 3.2 MOVE: movement and body

Cut by movement family, not by role. Every drill follows the movement library: isolate the movement; run a tempo ladder (slow, fast, slow to fast); vary conditions (surface, load, light, a teammate crossing); mark choreography before running it full out (Warburton 2013, VP per the walk's read of the primary); check automaticity with a dual task; get feedback from a clip against an exemplar or a peer check, never a self-rating; name the decision module the drill later merges with and the rehearsal where they combine. A design rule added by the traditional-thinking red team: drill serial parts (pour, carry, set-down) in isolation, but train concurrent parts ("scan while carrying", "glance while greeting") together from early on (Wightman and Lintern 1985, LO). Movement quality gets a vocabulary (Laban's weight, time, space, flow, LO) so "set down quietly" becomes describable. Take-home kits are optional, unpaid, and gate nothing (D7). Paid drill time is short: bursts inside pre-shift and the coming-on warm-up (intake group 5).

Deep: "moves in ensemble through a full room"; "uses their body so it lasts a late shift". Student: "designs a drill and extends the house movement vocabulary".
