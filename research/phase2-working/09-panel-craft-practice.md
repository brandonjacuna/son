## Seat 2: Practice and Simulation Designer

Status: profile unvalidated (stages 6 and 7 both skipped). It consumes the Hospitality Craft Educator's content, which is also unvalidated. This read stands with that caveat.

### Read in its own lane

- **Right: movement drills are separated from decisions and recombined at G2.** Choosing the practice form by the kind of skill is this seat's core rule. Drilled rehearsal suits procedures with one right way, and G2 is the first functionally aligned combination.
- **Right: SVC-007 and BEV-009 use expert comparison, and 4.7a turns panel disagreement into discussion cases.** This matches decide-then-compare with minority views (Klein and Borders; verified-primary, developer-authored).
- **Right: take-home kits and mock settings are low-fidelity options.** High fidelity rarely beats low by enough to justify its cost (Hamstra; Norman).
- **Right: practicals are peer-led.** Trained peers match actors on skill.
- **Wrong: practice items and measurement items are not kept separate.** SVC-007 carries a "panel-keyed SJT check", 4.7a tracks SJT agreement over time, SAF-001 "feeds G1", and G1 uses "set cases". Exposure and coaching inflate scores without changing judgment (Lievens; partially verified). Any item used in practice is retired from gates and from the 4.7 measurement.
- **Wrong: G3's "reflection time after the shift" and G4's team feedback are named but not designed.**
  - No prebrief sets the container.
  - No debrief strategy is chosen: facilitate a judgment gap, teach a knowledge gap, self-assess only where the learner can see the gap.
  - No review of what went right.
- **Wrong: 4.5(b) relies on "self-rating" for take-home drills.** Tray tension and eyes on the tray are gaps a learner cannot see in themselves. Self-assessment fails silently there.
- **Wrong: most modules name no practice form.** Without one, the default quiz stack creeps back in.

### Concrete challenges
