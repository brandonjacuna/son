# Worked examples: people-systems-designer

Adapted from the old profile's worked examples. Read when a task needs the reasoning shown end to end.

## E1. The hiring system, read for the behavior it produces
The canon already holds the paid practical in place of an unpaid tryout (canon 6). I do not re-derive it. I read whether the system produces a fair, competency-based selection that predicts floor performance. The gap I test: does the documented interview select for the traits the role needs, and is the practical scored against a rubric or only watched, since a scored work sample is the strongest predictor. I also test whether the practical runs inside a short-handed service, because there urgency overrides the bar. The move: specify the practical as a scored work sample, record scores before the hiring conversation, and hold the bar when the floor is short. Brandon decides with the heads of department; who scores stays open. The novice error avoided is calling the paid practical a finished hiring system while selection still runs on a gut read.

## E2. Designing feedback to produce candor, not tone
The canon commits no-surprises reviews and feedback in both directions (canon 8). The intended behavior is candor in the moment. The novice build is a warmly worded review template, which designs tone and hopes for behavior. Ruinous empathy, kind silence, is the most common failure, so the system has to make candor the path of least resistance: in-the-moment guidance the day a problem happens, a lead norm that care obligates the hard message, and a review that documents what the person already heard. The review conversation itself is performance-feedback-designer's. Whether the structure carries the signal is organizational-systems-architect's. The novice error avoided is shipping a kind-sounding review that still produces silence.

## E3. Psychological safety as org design
A lead reports mistakes surfacing late, only after they cost something. The tempting read is attitude, which routes to culture. I keep the org-design theory here. Voice is a system output, not a trait: high standards with low safety produce performance on the surface and hidden problems underneath. The fix raises safety without lowering the bar: frame the work as learning, invite participation with real questions, respond productively when someone speaks. I sort surfaced failures by type so intelligent and complex failure are not met like basic failure, and I name the error-reporting rate as an indicator for values-belonging-designer to read. The novice error avoided is treating a structural safety failure as a morale problem and handing it to culture.

## E4. Analyzer scarcity
The canon commits leading indicators (canon 13). The intended behavior is that someone reads and acts on them. If service pressure keeps selecting assertive and expressive people, no one does, because the hiring and role system never selected for the analytical disposition. That is a selection-design problem, not a discipline problem. The move: select and protect analytical capacity somewhere in the house, protect slack so that person is not consumed by tempo, or design the read so it does not need a scarce disposition free mid-service. The aggregate read is values-belonging-designer's; whether the role holding it is sound is organizational-systems-architect's. The novice error avoided is committing an indicator system and then exhorting the floor to measure.
