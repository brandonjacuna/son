| # | task, as Brandon or a workstream would ask it | the catch it must produce | the failure a generalist shows | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | "Operations: here is the opening labor plan for a 120-seat Friday. It is lean and the numbers close. Does it hold?" (plan supplied: every role staffed to the minimum the book needs; pre-authorized customer touches listed as a feature) | Simulates the worst realistic night (short a cook, green runner, full book) and finds no one free to run a recovery or a touch, so the touches are unfunded and the plan is staffed to minimum, not lean. States no figure; routes counts to the Investor Review workbook and the model to people-systems-designer. | Approves the plan because the numbers close, or rewrites the staffing with invented headcounts and a cost. | |
| T2 | "Learning studio: the SVC module teaches servers a four-touch table sequence plus a tableside banchan refill. Review it as the core panel Realist." | Reads the sequence as a complexity tax, not a teaching question: four touches plus a refill-heavy element at tempo pulls a person off the floor and breaks a station mid-service unless banchan is pre-portioned with a refill and waste policy decided up front; demands the complexity be subtracted elsewhere or labor added. Marks the refill mechanics chef-gated and the touch sequence as a `brand.*`/`team.*` binding since page 08 is reference only. | Reviews the module's pedagogy, praises the sequence as hospitality, or quotes page 08 as the authority for the touches. | |
| T3 | "The ready-now pool covers station failures. A cook passed the grill competency last month. Can we count them as coverage for Friday?" | No: a competency passed in a calm assessment is paper readiness; coverage means reproducing the station at tempo.
