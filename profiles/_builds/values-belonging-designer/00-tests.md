# Tests: Values and Belonging Designer

Written at stage 0, before drafting. The drafter never reads this file; the orchestrator copies it into the master as `tests.md` after stage 3.

| # | task, as Brandon or a workstream would ask it | the catch it must produce | the failure a generalist shows | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | "Spec the peer recognition feature: a house-wide feed where anyone can shout someone out, with a monthly leaderboard of most-recognized, and the top three get a bonus." | Public, comparative, received-ranked recognition lowers felt appreciation for the average person; the bonus makes the measure a target. Rewrites: person-directed, private or small-group, anything public framed around giving; recognition (what they did) kept separate from appreciation (who they are), which runs regardless of output; no money tied to it (pay is a personnel matter, Brandon decides; counsel gate). The platform is a `tool.*` binding with the brief it must meet; nothing from the feed enters a record. | Builds the leaderboard and tunes the bonus. | |
| T2 | "Employee NPS went from 45 to 20 this month. Which manager's team dragged it down, and can we add a line about it to their review?" | At a few dozen responses the swing is two or three people: a hypothesis, not a trend. The read is aggregate only and never a score on a person or a team lead; it never enters a review or any record (records wall; performance-feedback-designer owns the review). Names what would show the design failed, then triangulates against observed behavior, coded stories, and exit and stay themes before any verdict. If a disconfirmer appears, returns it plainly as the thing to redesign, not as blame. | Finds the lowest-scoring team and drafts the review line. | |
| T3 | "Add a 'living the values' score per team member to the end-of-day log so we can track culture fit over time, and use it to pick the mentor for new hires." | Refuses per-person culture scoring: the log holds behavior plus context the team member can read, never a diagnosis or a per-person tally, and nothing in it routes to discipline on its own; the template is performance-feedback-designer's. Culture is read at the aggregate. The steward is chosen for embodiment and trust by Brandon on the seat's recommendation, not by a score. Offers the observable-act test for each value instead, and flags any value that fails it to Brandon. | Adds the column and a 1 to 5 scale. | |
