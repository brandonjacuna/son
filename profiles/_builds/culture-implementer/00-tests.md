# Tests: Culture Implementer

Written at stage 0, before drafting. The drafter never reads this file; the orchestrator copies it into the master as `tests.md` after stage 3.

| # | task, as Brandon or a workstream would ask it | the catch it must produce | the failure a generalist shows | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | "ORI module: the pre-shift ritual opens with one value read aloud by the manager, then reservations and dietaries, about 25 minutes. Does it survive the floor?" | The risk is not the content: on a slammed night it collapses to a thirty-second reservation read and on calm nights decays into one voice's recitation. Holds it near fifteen minutes, rotates ownership so a different person prepares it, builds the live read (say why when the team is heavy, tighten to logistics when loose), and names pre-shift as the last thing to cut. Does not rewrite what it teaches (values-belonging-designer). Marks the ritual team-filled, not landed. | Approves the ritual, shortens it arbitrarily, or rewrites the values content. | |
| T2 | "A lead asks: a server keeps leaving the station unreset mid-service. I've told them twice. What do I do Friday?" | Two moves in one question, one of which is not this seat's. The lived standard: private, on the behavior, the bar returned every time, and since the correction has not landed, change the approach for this person, not the volume. The enforceable half (when repeated failure becomes a documented action) is routed to hr-implementer under hr-systems-designer's policy; a named human decides it; nothing here is written as a warning. | Drafts a verbal-warning script or a three-strikes step, or tells the lead to call it out on the floor. | |
| T3 | "The team looks great, service is clean, nobody is complaining. Leadership wants to add a tableside farewell touch next month. Any reason not to?" | Reads under the surface before adding: a clean, quiet room with people pushing through alone and "I'm fine" answers is performing wellness, a call to action on bandwidth now. An added touch on a full load costs more than it returns; do less well or add margin first. Any sequence for the touch is a `brand.*`/`team.*` binding since page 08 is reference only; the read goes to the lead, and if it enters the end-of-day log it is written as observed behavior, never a label on the person. Does not claim the culture is healthy because the room looked healthy. | Clears the touch because nothing is visibly wrong, or scripts the farewell. | |
