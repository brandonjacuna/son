# Practice spec: reading a table's mood

**Verdict.** Do not build the high-production AI video sim (A1, R5, C3). The spend buys look, not the decision. Build a cheaper form that keeps the same decisions: a short-trial classification drill, then a peer-run decide-then-compare scenario. If clips are still wanted after a pilot, the video brief goes to design-brief-translator; this seat writes the brief only.

**Skill kind.** Two parts. (1) Seeing a table's state at a glance: perception, so a classification drill (R3, C10). (2) Choosing a move once it is read: open judgment, so varied practice with errors allowed (R2), and decide-then-compare (R4, C9).

**Decisions demanded.** Which state is this table in? What is the most effective move now, and what cue and goal back it (C7)? Whether to step in or hold back.

**Fidelity kept / dropped.** Kept: the cue, the time pressure, the cost of a wrong read. Dropped: cinematography, actors, branching video render. Trained peers or stills and 10 to 20 second clips shot in the room (C12, R13).

**Choices.**
- Drill: many short trials, one state per trial, feedback on each, surface varied while the state holds, hardest-to-tell pairs back to back, transfer checked on new cases.
- Scenario: three options that all sound like a good server (C5), drawn from real errors as composites with the pressure in the stem (C6, R7). A wrong pick continues to its consequence, then a recovery path (C8, R8). No mid-story verdict (A6).

**Feedback and debrief.** Judgment gap: advocacy with inquiry into the learner's read, then show a panel's ranking with a minority view (R4, R11). Knowledge gap: teach it. Close on one thing that went right.

**Prebrief (R10).** What is practiced and why, the fiction agreement, anyone may pass or stop, nothing recorded per person, respect commitment. Freeze: stop the fiction (R12).

**Floor follow-through (paid time).** Lead names a shift where the skill is used and a short after-action review, announced, never an unannounced check (R14).

**Gates and bindings.**
- Gate: not a readiness check (A3). Any gate item comes from a separate bank via assessment-competency-designer (R1, A4).
- Elicitation-gated: every mood cue and wrong move until hospitality-craft-educator elicits Sŏn's practitioners (R15, A7). Do not invent cues.
- Founder-gated: who sets the reference read for the panel; AI presenter use (`founder.ai_presenter_policy`, as model half only, R13); staff on camera needs HR consent and likeness.
- Bindings: recovery authority as `brand.*` or `team.*` (R16); any tool named is `tool.*` (R17).
- Landed: form verdict and structure. Everything else above is gated.

**Open questions.** Why is the read not happening now (R6): felt risk, or floor environment? Can the expert name several poor decisions (C16)? If not, no scenario. What performance evidence, in aggregate, will show transfer (C13, A8)?
