# Practice spec: allergen at the table

**Verdict.** Short decide-and-continue scenes run with trained peers, plus a closed-procedure drill for the capture steps. No quiz on which dish carries which allergen (C4): the list goes to a job aid (instructional-designer). Cheapest form keeping the decisions: spoken scenes, no video, no actors.

**Skill kind.** Mixed. Capture and carry-through is a closed procedure, drilled to a standard. Noticing an aside is open judgment: varied scenes where errors can happen (R2).

**Decisions demanded.** (1) Stop on an aside ("my daughter can't do nuts, but anyway") instead of keeping pace. (2) Confirm what the allergy is and how severe. (3) Carry it through: capture, hand-off to kitchen, confirm at delivery. (4) Check rather than guess about a dish.

**Barrier (R6).** Hypothesis: felt risk (sounding intrusive, slowing the table). Elicitation-gated (R15): hospitality-craft-educator confirms it and supplies real errors first.

**Fidelity.** Keep: aside mid-order, table pace, pull to keep moving. Drop: set, props, video (R5).

**Choices.** Wrong options come only from elicited real errors, as composites with no names, with the pressure in the stem (C6, R7). Three options per item. Equal length and tone; the wrong move sounds like a good server (C5). Prompt: "most effective move?" (C7).

**Feedback.** A wrong move continues to its consequence, then the learner takes a recovery path forward (C8, R8). No mid-story verdict (A6). Because this is a safety item, the correct action is stated before close (R11).

**Prebrief (R10).** What and why; fiction agreement; anyone may stop or pass; nothing recorded per person; commitment to respect.

**Facilitator guide (R18, Output).** A lead does not play the customer to a direct report. End the fiction, ask what they saw, advocacy with inquiry, close on one thing that went right. Judgment gap: inquire into the learner's frame. Knowledge gap: teach it. Frozen learner: stop the fiction; they choose whether to debrief (R12; tbri).

**Floor follow-through (R14), paid time.** Lead reviews one real allergen table per shift with the server, announced in advance, never an unannounced check. Job aid at the pass. A near-term allergy table to handle.

**Gates and bindings.** These scenes are never gate items (R1, C1, A3, A4). If readiness is wanted, assessment-competency-designer builds a separate bank. no per-person records; aggregate evidence only (C13).
- Allergen capture path in the ordering tool: `tool.*` binding.
- Food and ingredient facts: chef-gated.
- Recovery and escalation authority: `brand.*` or `team.*` binding until canon sets it; page 08 reference only (R16).
- Scene voice review: materials-author-editor.

**R19 trace.** Cue: elicitation-gated. Decision, feedback, debrief, kept out of gates: landed. Choices: design landed, content gated. Food facts: chef-gated. Tool path: binding.
