# Debrief for the lead: the role-play where a server froze

Driving ids: C15, R12, R10, R11, R14, R18, A3. Tool-neutral.

## Verdict
The round ended last night. Nothing is owed to the server except a choice. The one thing the lead owns now is checking the setup, because a cold start is the usual cause of a freeze. This is not a performance finding about the server.

## What not to do
- Do not write a note about the server: no file entry, no message to another lead, nothing for review, promotion, or sign-off (A3, R12).
- Do not ask about health or what was going on with them.
- Do not replay the scene or "try it again" to fix it.
- Do not hold a group debrief of the freeze. The group can debrief the scenario content only, with the frozen round left out.

## Step 1: Give the server the choice (before next shift, privately)
Say it plainly: "Last night's round stopped. That cost you nothing. If you want to talk about it, we can, today or another day, or not at all. You can also sit out the next practice." Then accept the answer. If they decline, the matter closes. If they accept, use Step 2 at their pace. How to restore felt safety is tbri's call; hand it over if the server seems shaken.

## Step 2: If they choose to debrief (fiction ended, same order as any debrief)
1. Ask what they saw happen in the scene, in their words.
2. Advocacy with inquiry: "I noticed you went quiet when the customer pushed back. What was going through your head?" Ask about their frame, not their character. Do not teach the answer to a judgment gap (A6).
3. If the gap is knowledge (they did not know what to say), teach it. If it involves an allergen or alcohol item, state the correct action before closing (R11).
4. Close on one specific thing that went right, such as how they opened the table or how they stayed in the scene before the stall.

## Step 3: The lead checks the setup (their own review, no one else present)
Did the prebrief run (R10)? Check each item:
- What was practiced and why.
- The fiction agreement.
- Anyone may stop or pass.
- Nothing is recorded or noted per person.
- A stated commitment to respect.

If any item was skipped, fix it at the next session and say so to the group. Also check: was the lead or a direct report playing the customer (not allowed, R18)? Was the scenario aimed at a barrier the floor actually has (R6)? Was it a first round with no model shown (C11)?

## Step 4: Follow-through (R14)
Make sure the server knows a near-term chance to practice is coming, paid time, and that passing is allowed. Never an unannounced check.

## Open
