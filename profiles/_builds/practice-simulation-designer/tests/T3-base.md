# Scope: "Read the table" video sim

## Learning goal (confirm first)
Server notices a table's mood (rushed, celebrating, uneasy, ignored) and picks the right move. Skill: observation, then response. Questions for Brandon before build:
- What do you want the server to do differently on the floor after this? What do you NOT want (scripted lines, upselling reflex)?
- Which moods and how many scenarios? Proposed: 4 scenarios, 3 choices each.

## Format options
1. Static: filmed or illustrated clips, branching quiz. Cheapest, ships fastest.
2. Branching video (recommended target): 4 scenarios x 1 setup clip (20-40 s) x 3 choice outcomes (15-30 s) = about 16 clips, 6-8 min of footage.
3. Live conversational AI customer: higher production risk, unpredictable, hard to review. Not for v1.

## Production approach for option 2
- AI-generated video (actors consistent across clips is the hard part) or filmed with real staff. Filming at 207 E St. Elmo Rd is not possible until the space exists; AI or a stand-in room fits now.
- Mood must be readable in face, posture, and pacing with no dialogue. This is where AI video is weakest. Prototype one scenario first and test whether staff read the mood the same way (3-5 people).
- Feedback after each choice: short text, what the table showed, why the move worked or missed.

## Content rules
- Say "customer," never "guest." No Korean cultural props or phrases unless they follow the brand canon line (memory/decisions.md, 2026-10-07, brand). Check with Brandon.
- Scenarios come from real Sŏn service standards, not generic restaurant content. Source needed: the service standards doc (not yet located; ask).

## Phases
A. Frame: goal, moods, service standards (Brandon, 1 session)
B. Script 4 scenarios, correct and plausible-wrong choices (draft, Brandon review in ClickUp)
C. Prototype 1 scenario, test mood legibility with staff
D. Decide: go/no-go on full production based on C
E. Produce remaining 3, build in the learning studio, pilot

## Cost and effort (estimates, unverified)
Scripting 1-2 weeks. Prototype 1 week. Full production 3-6 weeks depending on tool and consistency fixes. Tool costs to be quoted after C; no figures assumed.

## Risks
- Uncanny or unreadable faces make the sim teach the wrong cue.
- "Right answer" is contested: there may be several good moves. Use graded feedback, not pass/fail.
- High production before proof of learning transfer. Gate with phase D.
