# Recovery practice: hire actors? (practice spec)

**Verdict (C12, R13, R5).** No, not by default. Run recovery practice with trained peers playing the customer, working from a short brief on the customer's frame. Hire actors only if the named target is confidence in front of an unfamiliar, upset stranger. Today the target is the recovery decision, and peers demand the same decisions at no cost.

**Skill kind (R2).** Open judgment, not a closed procedure. Varied scenes where errors can happen, with instruction on handling them.

**Decisions demanded.** (1) Read what kind of failure this is. (2) Act within the recovery the house authorizes. (3) Recognize when escalation is due. A peer customer forces all three. Actor skill adds look and feel, not a decision.

**Fidelity kept / dropped.** Kept: the customer's frame and pressure, the table's pace, a consequence that continues after a weak move (R8). Dropped: professional range, costume, rehearsed lines.

**Choices and scenes.** Wrong moves come from real errors elicited from Sŏn practitioners, as composites with no names, each with its pressure in the stem (C6, R7). Scripts set the customer's side only; the server's words stay their own (A2). If a move is wrong, play out the consequence, then make the learner recover forward (R8).

**Prebrief (R10).** What is practiced and why; anyone may stop or pass, and a pass costs nothing; nothing recorded or noted per person; a stated commitment to respect.

**Feedback and debrief (R11, R18).** End the fiction, ask what they saw, use advocacy with inquiry into why they read the failure as they did, close on one thing that went right. The facilitator guide carries debrief strategies and when each fits (R18).  Freeze: stop the fiction, end their round, learner chooses whether to debrief (R12).

**Floor follow-through (R14).** Lead runs a short after-action review within the shift week, plus a near-term chance to use the skill, as paid time. Follow-up is announced support, never an unannounced check.

**Not a gate (R1, A3, A4).** Practice scenes stay out of any readiness check and are never recorded per person. If someone wants a check, hand to assessment-competency-designer with a separate bank. Evidence is aggregate performance, not satisfaction (C13).

**Gates and bindings.**
- Recovery authority: `brand.*` or `team.*` binding, unset in canon. Brand Guidelines p.08 is reference only (R16). Founder-gated.
- Floor cues and wrong moves: elicitation-gated until hospitality-craft-educator runs (R15, A7).
- Voice review of scripts: materials-author-editor. Feedback landing: tbri.
- Live practice is not recorded by default; anyone on camera waits on HR consent.
