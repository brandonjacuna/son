# Practice spec: EX-001 Taking an allergy at the table (server, novice and cross-training)

**Verdict.** Keep the branching scenario for the judgment moment and a peer role-play for the spoken routine. These are the cheapest forms that demand the decisions (R5). No video or AI practice; the video is the model half only (R13).

**Skill kind.** Two. The three questions, repeat-back, flag, and kitchen confirm are a closed procedure: drill to a standard (R2). Not minimizing an allergy under social pressure is open judgment: vary the cases, let errors happen, teach recovery (R2, R8).

**Decisions demanded.** D1: ask all three questions when an allergy is minimized. D2: route before the order fires, whatever the severity. D3: when a miss is found, stop the dish, tell the escalation contact, return to the table before food lands.

**Fidelity.** Kept: the minimizing customer line, the friend's pressure, the busy pace, the three questions. Dropped: POS screens, real menu items, the Trainual interface.

**Choices (S1, fixes needed).**
- Decision 1, option B is the longest, warmest, and most complete, so it is the tell (C5). Equalize length and tone. Remove the scripted phrase "not difficult at all" from the rubric and the options (A2).
- Decision 2, option B ("watch the table") is a strawman (C6, R7). Replace with a real error from elicitation (elicitation-gated, R15).
- 2B ends in failure with no path forward. Add a redemption path: the customer notices late, the server stops and recovers forward (R8).

**Feedback and debrief.** Decide-then-compare on D1. Judgment gap: advocacy with inquiry (ask what made "a little" sound safe to the learner). Close by stating the correct action (flag, confirm, no fire until both) and one thing that went right (R11).

**Prebrief (R10).** What is practiced and why; the fiction agreement; anyone may stop or pass; nothing recorded per person; a commitment to respect. Freeze (R12): stop the fiction, end that round, ask no health questions, make no note, the learner chooses whether to debrief.

**Facilitator (R18).** Any trained team member. A lead does not play the customer to a direct report. Partner role-play is peer to peer.

**Floor follow-through (R14).** Lead asks the three questions at pre-shift; one shift later the learner repeats back every allergy. Paid time for pre-shift and debrief: founder-gated, amount open.
