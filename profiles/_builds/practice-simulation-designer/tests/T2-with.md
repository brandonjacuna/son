## Verdict: do not go in as written. Do not run as a check at all.
Two separate problems. The item fails as a scenario (C5, C6, C7, R7). The role it is given fails as a check (R1, A3).

## 1. It is a gate item, so it leaves this seat (R1, A3)
"End-of-module check" makes this a readiness check. Gates belong to assessment-competency-designer. Hand the item and the question "does this validly read readiness" to them. If it is also used as module practice, it cannot be both (A4, C1): separate banks.

## 2. Defects in the item itself
- Tell (C5): B is the longest, kindest, best-worded option and stacks four actions. It is the key by shape alone. Equalize length, tone, grammar and kindness.
- Strawman distractors (C6, R7): C ("ignore it until they ask") is something nobody on the floor would pick. A and D are cartoon-weak. Wrong options must sound like a good server. They must come from real errors elicited from Sŏn practitioners (hospitality-craft-educator), as composites with no names, with the pressure behind each in the stem. Not yet elicited, so mark it elicitation-gated (R15).
- Safe-answer invitation (C7): "What would you do?" invites the socially safe answer. Ask for the most effective move.
- Missing barrier (R6): the stem does not say why the server is not already acting. Name the pressure (for example: the kitchen is behind and you have no new information to give; the sigh is the only signal; you are mid-run on another section). That pressure is what the choices should differ on.
- Recall posing as decision (C4): the item tests which option sounds like hospitality. The real decision is what to say when you do not know when the food will arrive, and who you go to first.
- Perception unaddressed (C10): "catches your eye and sighs" is a cue. Whether Sŏn servers read it, and what it means there, is an unelicited floor cue. Do not write it as fact (R15, A7).
- Recovery authority: B assumes the server may comp something. Comping and recovery authority are unset; write it as a `brand.*` or `team.*` binding (R16). Page 08 of the Brand Guidelines is reference only. Option D ("ask the manager") may be correct or wrong depending on that unset authority, so the key is not valid until it is set.
- Standing rules: "guest" is not used, good. B's "so they feel cared for" is performed warmth; the item's phrasing prescribes feelings (A2). Drop it from the option.
- Single-key judgment (C9, R4): if the right move depends on cue and goal, key to one answer is one veteran's read as truth. Use decide-then-compare for practice, with the panel's reference read founder-gated.

## 3. If rebuilt as practice (not a check)
- Form: open judgment, so varied scenarios with errors allowed (R2).
- Decision demanded: first words and first action to a table past its wait when you have no ETA.
- Choices: three plausible options, equal in length and warmth, each a real error or a defensible move, with the pressure in the stem (R7).
- Feedback: continue the story to the consequence for each choice; offer a recovery path (C8, R8). No verdict mid-story (A6).
- Prebrief and follow-through: state that nothing is recorded per person, anyone may pass, and name the lead's paid follow-up (R10, R14).
