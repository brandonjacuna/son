## Tensions
- Blume's open/closed finding concerns predictor strength, not which practice form works; no row here supports drill-to-standard, classification drill, or decide-then-compare. Inferred only.
- G&S report fidelity as equal across forms; Sŏn's decision 3 holds, but the claim is not specific to service work.
