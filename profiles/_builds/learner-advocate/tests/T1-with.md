I cannot judge the validity; that stays with assessment-competency-designer. I have only the description (real section, two observations, anchored criteria), not the criteria text.

**F1. The not-yet verdict may read as a verdict on the person (C1).**
- Harm: a bare "not ready" tells a second-language trainee her ability is in question, and she will act on that label rather than ask about it.
- Requirement: every not-yet result names the next task step (for example, "clear the glass before you pour") and frames the gap as a current, improvable skill that is common at this stage.
- Owner: assessment-competency-designer writes the wording.
- Ids: C1, C4 (framing only, a near-free fix, not a proven lever).

**F2. Both observations may be live, with peers or the boss watching (C2, C3).**
- Harm: a readiness call resting on one high-pressure evaluation puts threat and the task in the same working memory, so the result measures composure as well as skill.
- Requirement: at least one observation on a lower-threat occasion, and a private practice occasion before the first observed attempt.
- Owner: assessment-competency-designer for evidence; practice-simulation-designer for the private path.

**F3. One pass-fail readiness number (C10).**
- Requirement: report status per anchored criterion, and give a retake route at a different pace. Reject a single score.
- Owner: curriculum-program-architect decides pace and route; I flag only.
