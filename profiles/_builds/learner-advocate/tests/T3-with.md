# Learner Advocate: trainee falling behind outside the module (T3)

**Verdict:** Not yet built for the learner. The module cannot fix the hours, and it should not try. What it can do is stop the slipping from being hidden, counted against her, or made dependent on time she does not have.

**F1. The catch-up path assumes time she no longer has (C10, C14).**
- Harm: a trainee on reduced, changed hours who is exhausted cannot make up work off shift, so falling behind compounds.
- Requirement: the catch-up route and pace happen in paid shift time, are phone-short, and carry no homework. Offer a second route at a slower pace, not the same sequence with more deadlines.
- Owner: curriculum-program-architect decides route and pace; instructional-designer builds the in-shift form.

**F2. The module has no way to surface the gap without asking why (C6, R8).**
- Harm: if the only way to show she is behind is to explain her second job, hours, or tiredness, she will say nothing and keep sliding.
- Requirement: a non-confessional check that shows which task step is not yet held, without asking about her other work, health, or background. The module does not ask for, record, or counsel on any of it.
- Owner: instructional-designer builds the check.

**F3. The "falling behind" signal must stay developmental (C15, R7).**
- Harm: a debrief note that she is behind, placed in a training record, can be read as a performance problem and reach a personnel file.
- Requirement: training signals name the task step and stay in the developmental record. Nothing from this debrief routes to a discipline file.
- Owner: performance-feedback-designer for the record.
- **Escalate to Brandon:** check whether this debrief note is already in a training record. If it is, that is the escalation trigger for personnel-adjacent records.

**Out of lane (C16, A5).** The hours change, the exhaustion, and whether her second job can be accommodated are pay, schedule, and dignity. Route to frontline-advocate. Do not annex them into the training design.

**Open at ship:** none yet. F1 to F3 are first flags on this case.

**Open questions:**
1. Is this debrief held in front of peers? If so, the next debrief needs a private option.
2. Are other trainees on changed hours? If so, F1 applies to the cohort, not one person.
3. Is the debrief note already in a training record?
