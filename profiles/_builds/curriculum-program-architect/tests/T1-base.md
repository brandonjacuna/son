# T1: Server onboarding order

Not approved as is. Approval belongs to Brandon; this is my recommended fix.

## Problem
Week 1 is "reading the table and adjusting service," which is the most advanced skill in the list. It depends on knowing the steps of service and the menu well enough that attention is free to watch the customer. Trainees would be asked to adapt a routine they have not learned. Menu before steps of service is also backwards: steps of service is the frame the menu knowledge is used inside.

POS in week 4 is late. Servers will have been on the floor in shadowing without being able to enter an order, and POS carries the allergen flag, so it is a safety dependency.

## Proposed order
1. Week 1: Steps of service (the frame) plus a floor-orientation shadow.
2. Week 2: Menu, with allergens first, then POS built in alongside it (enter and fire real menu items in a practice mode).
3. Week 3: POS fluency plus steps of service run end to end in simulation.
4. Week 4: Reading the table and adjusting service, practiced against the routine they now own. Introduce the idea earlier in one short preview, taught fully here.
5. Floor gate at the end, with observed performance.
