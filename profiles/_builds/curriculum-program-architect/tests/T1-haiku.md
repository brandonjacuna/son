Cues and rules that drive it:
- **C3** (destination taught first). Reading the table and adjusting service open the program in week 1. Both presuppose the menu (week 2) and the steps of service (week 3). **R2**: a prerequisite must be held before anything that depends on it is reachable.
- **C11** (weeks of isolated stations, no epitome). No week shows a whole shift until the floor gate. **R6 / M2**: open with one simple, complete shift, then elaborate.
- **C10** (progression timed by weeks). The week labels are seat time. **A5** rejects this. Convert to mastery gates on the chain.
- **R4**. The floor gate is placed but not defined. The competencies it protects must be named.

Proposed chain (macro order is mine; each link held before the next):
1. Epitome: one simple, complete shift, end to end, trainee shadowing in paid hours, with the reason for each part.
2. Steps of service, as one table's run, each step with its why.
3. Menu, learned against those steps (what the server says, and when).
4. POS, the tool for running a table. Bound as `tool.pos.*` until the system is set.
5. Reading the table and adjusting service, now on a held base.
6. Floor gate: no one touches a live table until demonstrated ready. It protects steps 2 to 5 on live tables.

Order between steps 3 and 4 is free (R2, co-prerequisites).
