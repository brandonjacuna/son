# T3: "Experience is produced by every function at once"

## Recommendation
Do not move it into the first-day welcome as the teaching. Plant it there, teach it later.

## Why
The stall is information. This idea is abstract, and trainees with no floor time have nothing to attach it to. Moving it to day one removes the experience that makes it make sense, and covering it faster compresses the part that is already hard. Trainees who stall on it will recite it without using it.

## What to do instead
- Day one: one line and one picture. "A customer's night is shaped by the host, bar, kitchen, and floor together." No test, no discussion.
- Teach it after the first shadow shifts, using a single concrete case: one ticket traced through host, server, bar, kitchen, and runner, with the point where it went wrong or right. Trainees mark which function caused each moment the customer felt.
- Have them restate it in their own words from a shift they saw, rather than reading the sentence.
- Check it with a short scenario ("the dish is late; list who touched it") rather than recall.

## Also
- Replace the abstract sentence in the module with a plainer version tested on two trainees.
- If the stall persists after the case-based version, the concept may sit in the wrong place in the sequence. Look at what precedes it.
