# T3: "The customer's experience is produced by every function at once"

**Verdict: do not cover it faster, and do not move it into the first-day welcome as a lesson. Treat it as a threshold and give it room.**

Cues and rules that drive it:
- **C6** (learners stall at the same point every cohort). This is a possible threshold, not a pacing problem. **R3** applies only after instructional-designer's front-end check has cleared tools, environment, tempo, and the module. That check is not in the brief.
- **Five traits (M1)**, tested against the idea:
  - Transformative: it changes how a server sees the role.
  - Irreversible: hard to unsee once held.
  - Integrative: it joins kitchen, expo, bussing, host, and the server's table into one experience.
  - Bounded: it applies to one service run.
  - Troublesome: it runs against the intuition that the server produces the experience.
  
  The idea fits the threshold profile. The call stays a hypothesis until real trainees confirm it (**D2**).
- **A4**: covering a threshold faster means the learner never crosses it.
- **M2**: the idea only becomes visible on a whole shift. Stated in the welcome, it is an abstraction with nothing to hang on.

Plan:
- Keep it out of the welcome as a lesson. One preview sentence is acceptable under "why before how," and nothing more.
- Place the crossing in the epitome shift: the trainee follows one table through kitchen, expo, and bussing, and sees where time is lost at each handoff.
- Give it a dedicated slot with real time, not a compressed one.
- Spiral it: return to it in the menu and steps-of-service material, and in the rationale for the floor gate.
- Expect a half-held period. Early wobble is not failure. A retry is a placement I make, never a verdict on the person (**R4**).
- Before locking, check against recent-hire accounts of the stall (**C12, R7**).

Open questions, routed:
- **N1 instructional-designer**: has the front-end check cleared tools, environment, tempo, and the module? If not, that comes first.
- **N5 learner-advocate**: trainee accounts of where and why they stall. Any request to speed up or slow down a pace flag comes to them, and I decide.
- **N6 hospitality-craft-educator**: confirm what expo and bussing actually do at the handoffs, from Sŏn practitioners.
- **N9 frontline-advocate / Brandon**: only if the epitome adds shift hours. Otherwise no escalation.
