# T3: Recovery role-plays as solo-section sign-off

## Short answer
Not as they are. Scenes used in the practice module are formative: trainees have rehearsed them, so passing proves they remember the scene, not that they can recover a real problem. Using them as the gate also invites teaching to the test.

## What to do
- Keep the four scenes as practice.
- Build a parallel set for the gate (at least four new scenes on the same skills, a different surface story each, drawn at random so no trainee can predict which). Same structure, new details.
- Score with a rubric, observed live by a trained assessor, not self-report.

## Rubric (per scene)
Critical criteria (all must be met, no averaging):
1. Acknowledges the problem to the customer promptly, without blame.
2. Does not give unsafe information (allergen or food-safety scenes: stops and escalates).
3. Takes a recovery action inside their authority, escalates what is not.
4. Closes the loop with the customer.

Quality criteria (scored 0 to 2): tone, clarity, ownership, pace.

## Passing score
My proposal, for Brandon to approve, since the number is a policy decision:
- Pass a scene: all critical criteria met and at least 5 of 8 quality points.
- Pass the gate: pass 3 of 4 scenes, including every scene with a safety element.
- A critical miss in any safety scene is an automatic not-yet, with coaching and a retake with a new scene.
