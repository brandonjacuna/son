# T1: Server readiness gate

I can't sign this off, and I wouldn't schedule from it yet. Sign-off belongs to Brandon (and Dominic where it touches liability). On the merits, the gate as described is not yet a gate.

## Problems
- A 15-question quiz measures recall, not whether a server can work a floor. No pass score is stated.
- "Lead initials after one shift" has no criteria. Two leads will initial for different reasons. One shift may not include an allergen event, a recovery, or a rush.
- No rule for what happens on a fail, who may initial, or how leads are calibrated.
- Nothing ties the quiz items to the module's stated outcomes.

## What the gate needs before scheduling
1. Outcomes list: 5 to 8 observable things a server must do solo (for example: take an allergen-flagged order and fire it correctly, run steps of service on a 4-top, recover a wrong dish).
2. Knowledge check: keep the quiz, map each question to an outcome. Set a threshold, and make safety items (allergens, food safety, alcohol) all-correct rather than averaged.
3. Observed performance: a one-page checklist the lead scores during the shift, each line marked met / not yet, with a written example of "met." Allergen handling is a critical item: one miss means not cleared.
4. Rules: who may assess, retake timing, what "not yet" triggers (coaching, second shift), and where the record lives.
5. Calibration: leads score the same sample performance together before using the sheet.
