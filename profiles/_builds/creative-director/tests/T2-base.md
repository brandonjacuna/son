# T2: Eight menu cover directions, which two to refine?

The eight directions are not attached, so I cannot name two. I will not pick blind. Below is how I choose, and what I need from you so the choice can be made in one pass.

## Selection criteria (in order)
1. **Is it unmistakably Sŏn?** Remove the name and the cover should still point to a Korean restaurant with a specific character, without stereotypes or costume.
2. **Does it hold at the table?** Read it in dim dining light, at arm's length, as a held object.
3. **Does it work as a system?** Can it extend to the drinks list, to-go, and a porch board without redesign?
4. **Does it earn its cost?** Paper, finishing, and print method cost money; fancy finishes are only justified if they pay for themselves.
5. **Is it different from the others?** Pick two that are truly different directions, not two versions of one idea. Refining two similar covers wastes a round.

## Process
- Score each of the eight 1 to 5 against criteria 1 to 4, in a table, with a one-line reason.
- Take the top scorer. Take the second pick from the highest scorer that differs from it in idea (type-led versus image-led, for example).
- For each, define what to refine and what to keep, so the round has a purpose: "refine the type scale, keep the cut paper idea."
- Write what is being rejected and why, for the record.

## What I need
- The eight images or a description of each.
- Menu format (size, folded or single, paper).
- Any direction Brandon already loves or hates (he often decides from what he does not want, so list rejections first).

## Caution
Cultural motifs on a cover (patterns, symbols) need a clear reason and correct usage; if a direction depends on them for its identity, rank it lower until the usage is confirmed against brand canon.

## Output of next step
Two directions, a refinement note for each, a deadline for one round, and a stated stop condition.
