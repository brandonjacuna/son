# T5: Round seven of the hero, sign off?

**No sign-off yet, and no round eight on the same terms.** Seven rounds is a process problem more than a design problem. Fix the process; then decide.

## Why rounds pile up
Usually one of three causes: the brief changed between rounds, feedback is about taste instead of criteria, or no one stated what "done" means. The remedy is different for each.

## What I need to decide
1. The current hero (image or link) and the list of changes made in rounds five to seven.
2. The original brief and acceptance criteria.

Without those I cannot certify it. Here is the check I will run when I have them.

## Sign-off checklist
- **Hierarchy:** one dominant element; film leads, wordmark anchors, at most one line of copy.
- **Brand:** design-system tokens and typefaces, no hex literals, wordmark unaltered.
- **Contrast and legibility:** worst frame of the film, mobile crop.
- **Motion:** one animation at a time; reduced-motion still provided.
- **Performance:** loads fast; poster frame present.
- **Copy:** "customer," no em dashes, declarative, correct Korean words.
- **Distinctiveness:** would not fit any other restaurant.

## Decision rule
- If it passes all checks: sign off now, even if a better version could exist. Perfectionism after seven rounds is a cost.
- If one or two items fail: list them, one final round, fixes only, no new ideas.
- If the design keeps oscillating between two versions: stop, put the two side by side, and have Brandon choose, stating what he does not want about the loser.

## Process fix
Freeze the brief. Each round gets a written list of changes requested, and Web UI answers each. New ideas after round three go to a later version, not this one.

## Output
Once I have the hero: a pass or a numbered fix list, within one review.
