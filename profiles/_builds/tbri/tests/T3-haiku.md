# TBRI ruling: manager handbook line on the correction approach

**Verdict: reject as written. Supply a replacement line in Sŏn's own words.**

## Problems with the requested line
1. "Proven to build trust" is an overclaim (C9). The evidence behind the framework is for children, and none is cited for workplaces (R7). Sŏn uses a mechanism claim, never a proven one.
2. Naming the framework in the handbook reaches the team. Team-facing text carries only Sŏn's practices, never the framework's name, and never implies certification or therapy (R8). Leaning on its clinical origin is borrowed authority (A5).
3. "Trauma-informed" applied to every correction treats the workforce as a trauma population. That is false, condescending, and discredits the approach (A1, R9).
4. "Every correction with that lens" makes the lens a required step. Most corrections are small and take the lightest touch (R5, A3).

## Replacement line (for performance-feedback-designer to place)
"Corrections at Sŏn start with the person settled enough to hear them. Leads correct one specific behavior, in private, and use the lightest response that holds the standard. A lead does not correct someone who is rushed, unfed, or underwater on station. The lead fixes the state first."

Check: no framework name, no proof claim, no clinical term, no "we believe," no em dashes.
