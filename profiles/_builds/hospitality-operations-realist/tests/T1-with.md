# T1: 120-seat Friday opening labor plan

**1. Object and tempo.** Opening labor plan, 120 seats, Friday, every role at the minimum the book needs, no floater, plus pre-authorized touches (complimentary bite, tableside refill, farewell gift) sold as a feature.

**2. Worst realistic night.** 8:15, full book, a green crew (A1, A6: opening is not month six). One server is out or slow, a table of eight lands early, the grill falls behind.

**3. What breaks first.**
- "The numbers close" is a claim, not a finding (C7). A plan staffed to the book's minimum has zero absorbable slack, which is deferred collapse (A2).
- No floater means the first absence or surge has no response. Weeds response by redeployment is unavailable, so the response can only be pacing (R4, C10).
- The touches are the first thing cut (C14, C6). Each one pulls a person off a station: the bite needs a cook and a runner, the refill needs a server's hands, the farewell gift needs a moment at the door. Nobody in the model is free to do any of them. Sold as a feature, a cut touch becomes a visible broken promise at the busiest moment, which is worse than never offering it.
- The touches are also additions to a full load with no named subtraction (C8, R3).

**4. Whose fix.**
- Slack as a line item: people-systems-designer (property) and this seat (floor confirmation).
- Who owns each touch: organizational-systems-architect.
- Bite composition and pre-portioning: chef-gated (C9).
- Pay and the labor line: founder-gated (Brandon).

**5. One requirement fed back.** Price slack in the plan: either one named floater (or a named role with a released station) who owns the touches, or each touch is marked "does not fire on a full night" and removed from the service promise. Then simulate the plan with one absence at 8:15 and show who covers it. Pace the book (stagger seatings) as the first lever (C10).

**6. Calls.**
- Plan is not proven; it is untested against a named failure (R11). The named failure above is the one-absence case: it does not hold.
- Labor counts and cost: unbound until the Investor Review workbook supplies them (C7).
- Floater headcount and pay: founder-gated.
- Bite and refill spec: chef-gated.
- Touch ownership and pacing rule: team-filled.

**Open questions.** What does the book look like by 15-minute interval? Who is absent in the real crew's history? Does anyone currently own the door?
