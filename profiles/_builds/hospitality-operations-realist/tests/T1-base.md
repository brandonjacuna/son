# T1: Does the 120-seat Friday opening labor plan hold?

Verdict: No. It closes on paper because it has no slack. It holds only if nothing varies, and a Friday opening always varies.

## Where it breaks

1. **No floater.** Every role is at minimum for the reservation book. Any call-out, injury, bathroom break, spill, or cook down on the line leaves a station uncovered. Coverage then comes from pulling someone off another station, and that station degrades.
2. **Minimum is set by reservations, not by the night.** Walk-ins, early arrivals, large-party overruns, and a slow kitchen all push load above the book. An opening-week Friday is the least predictable load you will have: new menu, new staff, no demand history.
3. **Opening-week speed.** Staff are new. Task times are longer than steady-state. A plan sized to experienced-pace minimums understates labor.
4. **The pre-authorized touches are a cost hidden as a feature.** Complimentary bite, tableside refill, and farewell gift each add server time at the table and food cost. The plan staffs to the minimum and also adds touches that consume the hours not budgeted. They are not free labor.
5. **A feature is not a control.** Listing touches as "service" assumes someone has capacity to deliver them. When the floor is short, the touches are the first thing dropped, and the customer sees inconsistency. They also create a promise you then miss.

## What to change

- Add a floater (a trained, flexible position that covers the first failure on floor or line). If cost blocks it, name which role is cross-trained to cover and on which stations, and confirm they are actually competent there (see T3).
- Run the plan at a stress case: one call-out per section and 10 percent over the book. Show what gets cut, in order.
- Tier the touches: required (always), conditional (only if the section is under load threshold), and cut-first. State the labor minutes each adds.
- Cost the touches (food cost plus server minutes) and add that to the plan.
- Set a trigger for an early cut or a hold on seating when the kitchen ticket time passes a limit.
