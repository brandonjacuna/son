# T3: Can a cook who passed grill competency last month count as Friday coverage?

**Short answer.** Not yet. Not as coverage on the Friday plan.

**1. Object and tempo.** A ready-now pool slot: one cook with a grill competency sign-off, asked to cover a grill failure at Friday peak.

**2. Worst realistic night.** 8:15, the grill cook is down or in the weeds, the book is full. The covering cook comes off their own station, which then goes uncovered, and takes the grill mid-rush.

**3. What breaks first.**
- A sign-off is coverage on paper (C12). Ready means reproducing the station at tempo. The question is whether last month's pass was a real service at tempo with a full book, or a calm-afternoon check (R7). The brief does not say, so I mark it unknown.
- Pulling the cook leaves their home station empty (C6). Unless a floater exists, "coverage" is a swap of one hole for another (A2).
- Grill mise must already be deep (C2, C3). A cook arriving mid-service cannot re-prep; if the grill is light, they spin in place.
- A month-old pass on one occasion, by a presumably green crew member (A1), is thin evidence for an ordinary Friday.

**4. Whose fix.**
- Whether the gate validly reads tempo readiness: assessment-competency-designer.
- Rehearsal at tempo: practice-simulation-designer.
- Grill station design and mise depth: chef-gated.
- Any personnel assignment, schedule, or pay for the cover: founder-gated (Brandon alone, until a GM exists).

**5. One requirement fed back.** Count the cook as coverage only after a tempo test: a real service, or a simulated full-book grill run, passed within a recent window, with the home station named as covered by someone else or accepted as dropped. Until then, list them as "candidate, unproven" and keep a hands position in the plan.

**6. Calls.**
- Counting this cook as Friday coverage: not supported now (C12, R7). Untested, not proven to fail (R11).
- Tempo standard and recency window: assessment-competency-designer's to set.
- Grill mise and pars: chef-gated.
- Assignment of the cook: founder-gated.

**Open questions.** What did the sign-off consist of, and at what tempo? Who covers the cook's home station? Has the cook worked a Friday peak?
