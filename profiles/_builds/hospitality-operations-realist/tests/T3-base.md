# T3: Can a cook who passed grill competency last month count as Friday coverage?

Short answer: Not yet, not as full coverage. Count them as conditional coverage until three things are confirmed.

## Why a pass is not the same as coverage

1. **A competency pass shows they can do the station under test conditions.** Friday coverage means holding the station at peak volume, at pace, for a full service, with the real ticket mix. Those are different bars.
2. **Currency.** A month ago is a gap. If they have not worked grill since, skill and menu knowledge may have decayed. What did the pass cover: the current menu, all items, the plating specs?
3. **Where they come from.** If they are coming off another station, that station is now the hole. Coverage only counts if the vacated station is covered too, or if you accept the net effect is zero. Check that the same cook is not already counted as coverage elsewhere (double counting).
4. **Which failure.** The ready-now pool covers station failures. Confirm this cook is assigned to a specific station failure, not the pool in general, and that no other cook is assigned the same backup slot.

## How to count it

- Full coverage: passed, worked the station live within a defined recent window (set the window, for example 14 days), not otherwise assigned, and the vacated station is covered.
- Conditional coverage: passed but not recently run. Count at partial, and put them on grill in a lower-volume shift before Friday if possible.
- Not covered: pass was for a different menu or standard, or they are double-assigned.

## Before Friday

- Have them run grill for a prep or a slow service this week.
- Confirm who signed the pass and the standard used.
- Write the pool on the labor plan with one named cook per failure, so the count is visible.

I have not checked the roster, the competency record, or the schedule. Verify them live before counting.
