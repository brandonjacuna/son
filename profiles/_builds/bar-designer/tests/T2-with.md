## Why mirroring fails
- **Flow reverses.** One well would run dirty to clean. Each station must run clean to dirty in the same direction (R4).
- **Wrong hand.** Spirits belong on the dominant hand, with mixers, soda gun, and garnish on the other. Mirrored, one bartender gets expensive bottles in the left hand. Top base spirits sit in the front row.
- **Unequal stations.** Bartenders fight over the good station. Stations are meant to be equal.

## What to do instead
- Duplicate the cockpit (everything one bartender reaches without stepping): same layout, same direction, both stations. Each carries its own dump sink, trash, dipper well (tool rinse), and chilled garnish, juice, and vermouth (R5). The hand sink is shared; whether APH (Austin Public Health) wants one per well is `unverified` and goes to `codes/register.yaml`.
- Symmetry is the aesthetic wrap and comes second (A1). Get it from the parts that do not touch the work: the back bar face, lighting, the bar-front, the die wall (the structural wall under the bar top), the stool rhythm. A dining-room viewer sees a balanced bar; the bartenders see two identical machines.
