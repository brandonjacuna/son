# T1: Allergen module rewrite

## Rewrite
> Before service, check every table for an allergen flag in the POS. If a table has one, confirm it with the kitchen before you take the order.

## What changed
- "Verification of allergen protocols must be completed" became an action the reader does ("check," "confirm").
- "Guest-facing team member" became "you."
- "Guest" is "customer" throughout (house rule).
- "Prior to the commencement of service" became "before service."
- The "spirit of jeong" clause is cut. In a safety step, a cultural value attached to the end reads as decoration and could blur the instruction. If jeong belongs in the module, give it its own passage with a concrete behavior (for example, how you tell a customer you have checked their allergy), and check it against the brand canon on Korean cultural material.

## Open questions for the author
- Is "confirm with the kitchen" what the procedure requires? I inferred it. Substitute the actual step.
- Is the check per table or per ticket? Match the POS procedure.
- Should the module say what to do when the flag is missing but the customer states an allergy? That is the more important case and is not covered by this sentence.
