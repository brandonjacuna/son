Open issues
1. Thin condensed wordmark: thin strokes lose legibility at distance and angle. Check the stroke weight against the viewing distance from E St. Elmo Rd (rule of thumb: about 1 inch of letter height per 10 feet of readable distance, and stroke thickness enough to survive the substrate and cutting method).
2. Gloss black panel on a west wall: afternoon sun produces glare and washes out the face. Gloss shows fingerprints, heat, and fading. Specify a matte or satin finish, or test it.
3. Night read: is it lit? Halo, face-lit, or external? A black panel with thin letters needs contrast at night. Show a night render.
4. Brand fit: confirm the wordmark and diacritics (Son with the breve and accent as the brand uses them) against the brand design system, not the design tool's font substitution.
5. Permits and lease: the facade sign depends on the City of Austin sign permit, landlord approval, and any historic or district limits. Pre-lease, so nothing here can be finalized. This stays concept phase.
6. Fabrication data: no dimensions, materials, mounting, electrical, or tolerances yet.
