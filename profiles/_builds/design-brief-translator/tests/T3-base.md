# T3: Opening announcement, three formats

## Brief
**Outcome:** One announcement told once, delivered as a web page, an Instagram set, and a printed card for neighbors on St. Elmo. The same message and look across all three, each shaped for its medium.

## Core message (shared)
Sŏn, a Korean restaurant, is opening at 207 E St. Elmo Rd, Austin. The announcement is for the neighborhood first. It states what Sŏn is, where, and what the reader can do next.
- Opening date: NOT CONFIRMED. Do not invent one. Use whatever date Brandon has approved; if none, the copy says what is known and no more.
- Any preview night, hours, or reservation link: confirm before use.

## Audience split
- Neighbors (card): local, physical, may be wary of a new business. Tone is plain, respectful, specific about location and what to expect. Consider a line about noise, parking, or a contact for concerns only if the owners want it.
- Instagram: followers and local food audience; image-led.
- Web page: the permanent source of facts; links from the other two.

## Per medium
- **Web page:** hero image, 80 words of copy, address with map link, one action (join list or reserve once live). Accessible, fast, mobile first.
- **Instagram set:** 5 to 7 slides. 1 announcement, 2 the room or food, 3 the place (St. Elmo), 4 what to expect, 5 date and address, with a final slide pointing to the link. Text kept inside safe margins; alt text on every image.
- **Printed card:** A6 or 4x6, one side message, one side address and contact. Large readable type, one image at most. Print specs (stock, bleed, quantity, delivery method) to confirm.

## Rules
Say "customer," not "guest." No em dashes. Declarative copy, no "we hope" or "our goal." Korean words spelled consistently.

## Open questions
1. Approved date and any preview event?
2. Which imagery exists? No stock Korean table stand-ins; use real photos or none.
3. Who signs the card (Brandon, both founders)?
4. Print budget and distribution plan?

## Sequence
Web page first (source of truth), then Instagram, then card, so every link and date matches.
