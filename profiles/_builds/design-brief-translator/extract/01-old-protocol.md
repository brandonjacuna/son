## Tensions
- The seven layers extract more than a design-system maker needs: color, type, and materiality parameters collide with the closed system. The frame keeps ratio, density, accent coverage, and hierarchy mechanism; this source does not say which layers to drop.
- Old success criteria are judged by a sharp reviewer's eye (example 1); no test is given a coding maker can run.

## Not usable
- Layers 5 and 6 as full parameter sets for a Sŏn brief: era and materiality citations risk the Korean canon line (reference only) and are survey material without a decision here.
- Critique severity labels (function-breaking, specificity, content-integrity, brand): taxonomy, no decision attached.
