## Corrective action
Type: policy (with a discretion reservation). Owner: Brandon. Version: 0.1. Review date: set at adoption, 12 months out.

**Purpose.** Corrective action tells a team member where their conduct or performance falls short of a stated standard and what must change. It is applied the same way to the same facts.

**At-will.** Employment at Sŏn is at will. Either side may end it at any time, with or without cause or notice. Nothing in this section changes that, and nothing here is a promise of continued employment or of any process.

**Usual steps.** Corrective action usually runs verbal, written, final written, then termination. These are a guide, not a required sequence. Sŏn may start at any step, repeat a step, or skip steps, including going directly to termination, depending on the seriousness of the conduct, the facts, and the team member's record.

**Fairness basis.**
- Every action rests on a specific, dated incident or standard, never on a protected characteristic, a complaint, a leave, or a report made in good faith.
- The same facts produce the same recommendation to Brandon, who decides.
- The team member gets the reason in writing and may respond in writing or in person. The response is kept with the record.
- Developmental feedback and review conversations are not corrective action and enter no discipline file on their own. Only a dated incident the team member saw and could answer goes in the file.

**No surprises.** Sŏn's practice is to tell a team member about a concern before it becomes a termination. This is a practice, not a promise, and it does not limit the at-will statement or the skip-step reservation above.

**Questions or concerns.** A team member who believes an action was unfair may use any complaint route in the handbook, including one that does not run through their supervisor. Retaliation for raising a concern is prohibited.

**Acknowledgment.** Receipt only, in plain language, through the handbook acknowledgment tool. An unsigned acknowledgment is not grounds for discipline.
