# How You Are Paid: Menu-Price Compensation and Points

## What this is
Sŏn builds compensation into its menu prices. Customers do not add a tip to cover your pay. The portion of revenue set aside for team compensation is distributed to eligible teammates through a points system.

## This is not a tip
- It is not a gratuity. No customer chooses it, and it does not depend on any one customer's decision.
- It is Sŏn's compensation to you, paid through payroll as wages. Taxes are withheld like other wages, and it appears on your pay statement.
- Sŏn controls how it is distributed. Customers do not.

## How points work
- Points are assigned by role, hours worked, and [other agreed factors]. The point values for each role are published to the team. [Insert table once approved.]
- Your share each pay period = your points divided by total points, multiplied by the compensation pool for that period.
- Pool size, total points, and your points are shown on your pay statement or in a report available to you each period.

- Points are adjusted only through the written process in this handbook, never by a manager's discretion on one person.

## Guarantees
- Every hour you work is paid at least the legal minimum wage, whatever the pool produces. [CONFIRM base rate structure.]
- Overtime is calculated on all pay for the week, including points pay.

## Notes for the drafter (remove before publishing)
- Legal classification is the key risk. A mandatory charge or a price-embedded amount is usually wages and not a tip, which is why the wording above avoids "tip" and "gratuity." Employment and tax counsel must confirm before publishing.
- Customer-facing menu and receipt language must match this section. Do not tell customers it is "service" or "gratuity" if the handbook says it is not.
