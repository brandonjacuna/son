# Corrective Action

Sŏn uses corrective action to help teammates fix problems and to keep a fair, consistent workplace.

## Steps
1. **Verbal warning.** Your manager meets with you, names the issue, states the expected standard, and documents the conversation.
2. **Written warning.** A written notice describes the issue, the earlier steps, the expected change, and a review date. You may add a written response. You sign to confirm you received it, not that you agree.
3. **Final warning.** A written notice states that further issues may end your employment.
4. **Termination.** Employment ends.

## How it works
- Steps are documented and kept in your file. Each manager applies the same standard to the same conduct.
- Sŏn may start at any step, or skip steps, based on the seriousness of the issue. Serious conduct (for example theft, violence, harassment, working impaired, falsifying records, or a food safety violation that endangers customers) can lead to immediate termination.
- Warnings do not need to be of the same kind. A warning for lateness counts toward the next step for a different issue if the pattern is the same behavior standard.
- Warnings stay active for 12 months unless the notice says otherwise. [DECIDE: confirm period]
- You can raise a concern about a warning with the General Manager or a founder. Retaliation for raising a concern in good faith is prohibited.

## At-will employment
Employment at Sŏn is at-will, as Texas law provides. You or Sŏn may end the employment at any time, with or without cause or notice. This section is a guide, not a contract. It does not promise that any step will be followed before termination, and only the CEO can change at-will status in a signed writing.
