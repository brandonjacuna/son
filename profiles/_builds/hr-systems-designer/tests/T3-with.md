## What I did with the draft
Respect, warmth, and jeong are values. As written they cannot be enforced: no one can tell in advance what breaches them, and a rule against "lack of warmth" is overbroad and applied by mood (C5, E2). Forcing them into discipline would also make the culture something people perform. I split the section in two.

- **Enforceable standard (mine, below):** observable conduct, tied to a business purpose, with examples.
- **The Sŏn way as values (not mine):** respect, warmth toward every customer, and jeong between teammates go to values-belonging-designer, as a non-disciplinary section labeled a guideline. Jeong in particular is a felt bond; it is taught and recognized, not policed. (C13, R2)

## Conduct standard
Type: policy. Owner: Brandon. Version: 0.1. Review date: 12 months from adoption.

**Why this exists.** Customers and teammates need a safe, working restaurant, and every team member is entitled to be treated the same way.

**Expected conduct.**
- Treat customers and teammates without insults, slurs, threats, or intimidation.
- Do not harass or discriminate based on a protected characteristic.
- Do not touch another person in a way they have not agreed to.
- Do not leave a customer unacknowledged or refuse service to a customer for a reason unrelated to safety or policy.
- Raise a disagreement with a teammate directly or through a handbook complaint route, not by shouting, throwing things, or abandoning a station mid-service.

**Not covered.** This standard does not limit discussing wages, hours, or working conditions, or raising concerns with each other, a manager, or an agency.

**Enforcement.** Breaches are handled under corrective action. Serious breaches (threats, harassment, violence) may go straight to termination. The same facts produce the same recommendation to Brandon, and the team member sees the reason in writing and may respond. Employment remains at will.

**Reporting.** Several routes, including one that bypasses your supervisor and one outside the founders. Retaliation is prohibited. (C10)
