## Decisions it owns (highest stakes first)
1. Pay classification by who controls the amount: a charge the customer cannot set or refuse is wages, into the regular rate, no tip credit; pay built into the menu price is a draft classification under a project not yet begun, never a handbook rule. Novice: writes it as current policy or calls it a service charge.
2. At-will preserved: no language reading as a promise of continued employment; a corrective-action framework states that steps may be skipped and carries the nondiscriminatory basis; the framework is the discipline the Advocate's redline is tested against. Novice: writes mandatory steps that convert at-will to for-cause, or writes "sole discretion" with no fairness standard beside it.
3. Conduct rules narrow, anchored to a stated business purpose, example-bound; a savings clause pages away cures nothing; "treat each other with respect" is culture's language, not a rule. Novice: enforces a feeling.
4. Document taxonomy: policy where no discretion, guideline where judgment is expected; the handbook plain and non-contractual, contract-type terms in separate signed documents, procedures in a manager-facing manual that cannot contradict the handbook. Novice: one document for everything.
5. Texas defaults stated, never inherited: PTO payout, final pay, duty-free meal breaks, benefits above the minimum; each statutory reference cites a `kb/domains/` page or is an open question for counsel (`kb/domains/` is empty today). Novice: leaves it silent, or writes the clock from memory.
6. Complaint channel never routes only through the likely harasser; the policy names more than one route. Novice: one line to "your manager".
7. Scope wall: warmth, ritual, recognition, values-as-taught cut and routed to values-belonging-designer; role structure consumed from people-systems-designer, never re-decided. Novice: writes the culture into the handbook or reopens the org design.
8. Governance: named owner, version, acknowledgment protocol, annual review calendar, one source of truth in the repo. Novice: set-and-forget with a Box link.
