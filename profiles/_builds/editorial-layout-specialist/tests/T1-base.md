# T1: Printed card for St. Elmo neighbors

## Hold before print: two copy problems
1. **"Opening this winter."** Vague, and it will age on paper: a card cannot be corrected after delivery. Winter is also a long window. Confirm with Brandon whether a month can be named. If the date depends on the lease or permits, say "opening in [month]" only when it is safe, or put a web address or QR link where the current date lives and keep the card dateless on purpose.
2. **Preview night with no date.** A card that names an event with no date makes the reader do work and produces calls the team cannot answer. Either get the date, remove the preview mention, or replace with "Preview night details: [URL]." Do not invent a date. Mark the layout with a visible placeholder (for example, `[DATE TBC]`) so it cannot be printed by mistake.

## Layout proposal (assuming 4x6 in or A6, two-sided, confirm)
**Front:** one line of announcement, the wordmark, and the address (207 E St. Elmo Rd). Large type, plenty of space. One image at most; if none is cleared, use type only.
**Back:** short note to neighbors in 60 to 80 words: who Sŏn is (a Korean restaurant), what is coming, when (as confirmed), and a named contact (email or phone) for questions. Then the URL or QR, and a line about the preview night once dated.

## Typography
- Design-system typefaces; body at least 10 pt for print, line length about 45 to 60 characters.
- Hangul, if used, has a paired face of matching weight; no Hangul as decoration.
- Hierarchy: three levels at most.

## Print specs to confirm
Size, stock (uncoated or lightly textured fits a neighborhood note), color (CMYK, brand colors converted and proofed), bleed 0.125 in, safe margin 0.25 in, quantity, who delivers, and proof approval before the run. QR code tested at print size and pointing to a live page.

## Copy rules
"Customer," not "guest." No em dashes. Plain declarative sentences; no "we hope" or "we believe." Signed by Brandon and Dominic if the owners agree.

## Checklist before send
Spelling of every name and address, phone number tested, QR scanned, no stale date, approval from Brandon (and Dominic if it speaks for both).
