# T2: Welcome packet for the first hires, document review

The draft is not attached, so this is the review framework and the checks I run. Send the file and I return line-level notes.

## This document touches employees: review is strict
A welcome packet is read as the company's promise. Anything on pay, schedule, tips, benefits, policies, or at-will language has legal weight in Texas. Any statement of that kind needs Brandon's or counsel's confirmation, not mine.

## Checks

**Content**
- Does the first page say who the reader is, what happens on day one, who to ask, and where to be (address, time, parking, entrance, what to wear and bring)?
- Pay, tip handling, schedule, and probation: stated only if confirmed; otherwise a clear "details in your offer letter."
- No promises not backed by a signed document ("job security," "growth guaranteed").
- Required items present: I-9 and W-4 instructions, handbook acknowledgment, safety and food handler certification requirements.
- Consistency of names: Brandon John Acuña-Cardona as CEO in operational materials; legal documents use Brandon John Acuña. Dominic Thomas, CFO.

**Voice**
- "Customer," never "guest." No em dashes. Declarative and warm; no performed conviction ("we believe," "our goal is").
- Explain Korean terms once; spell them consistently.

**Layout**
- Clear hierarchy: title, sections, subheads, at most three levels.
- Body 10 to 11 pt print or 16 px screen, line length 50 to 75 characters, enough leading.
- Page breaks do not strand headings; tables fit the page; no widows or orphans.
- Contrast and accessibility for PDF: tagged headings, alt text, selectable text.
- Consistent numbers, dates, and times format.

**Structure**
- One idea per page where possible; a one-page "first week" schedule; a contact page.
- Version, date, and owner in the footer so a new hire knows which version they have.

**Scope**
Sŏn only. Nothing from other ventures or former partners.

## Output format
A numbered list of findings (blocking, fix, polish), each with the location, the problem, and the exact replacement text.

## Process
It is for the first hires, but it needs founder review; the review copy lives in ClickUp, the approved final goes to Trainual.
