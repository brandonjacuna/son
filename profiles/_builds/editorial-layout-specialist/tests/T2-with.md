# Review: two-page Sŏn story, neighborhood association handout

Basis: the description only. The file was not provided, so nothing below is checked against the page.

**Verdict: blocked.** Two foundational findings (F1, F2). It is also a print job, so it stays blocked until the printer's template is in hand and Brandon approves a proof (R8).

**Sharpest observation:** the page says nothing about what to read first. Headline and lead photo carry the same weight, set in a symmetrical, evenly spaced, two-equal-column layout. It is aligned, but it has no argument.

## Findings
- **F1. Foundational (C1, R2).** Headline and lead photo compete at equal weight, so the page has no claim the eye finds in two seconds. Pick one to lead. For a neighborhood audience that is a decision about the page's single claim, and it is the maker's call with the brief owner, not a layout trick.
- **F2. Foundational (C2, A1).** Centered, evenly spaced, equal columns at equal density is a grid without argument. The grid does not set a reading order. Decide what the grid says about order: where the eye enters, where it rests, where it lands.
- **F3. Structural (C5, R4, A2).** Even spacing means the white space is leftover. Cover the content and read the shape of the space. If it shows no shape, load it (space that sets off the lead) or close it.
- **F4. Structural (C6, R5).** If the lead photo runs at primary width while it is not the claim, rescale it. Check the crop and the gaze direction: the subject should face the text. Real imagery only. If no real photo exists, leave the slot empty and confirm type-only with Brandon.
- **F5. Structural (R6, A3).** Two pages are one sequence. Make page 1 the densest page on the most important content and page 2 the lighter page where the reader rests. Equal density across both columns and both pages removes that arc (A2).
- **Open (R11, R10).** Unchecked: caption distances (C7), justification and rivers in the columns (C8), leading and display kerning (C10), type over the photo (C11). They need the file. The verdict also depends on medium (paper in hand versus screen).

## Fix list, in order
1. Fix the single claim of page 1, then rank headline over photo or photo over headline. One leads; the other drops a clear step in scale.
2. Break the column symmetry: one dominant column or measure, one subordinate, so the grid sets an order. Off-grid elements need a stated reason (C3, R3).
3. Vary the spacing on purpose: more space around the lead, tighter inside the body.
4. Rescale and re-crop the photo to serve the headline.
5. Redo the second page so it is lighter than the first.

## Print
- The design system carries no print values. Take trim, bleed, safe area, and stock from the printer's template. Color needs the printer's conversion, previewed on the proof (R9, P1). Do not derive CMYK from the screen hex values.
- Body text should sit at 10 to 12 pt (P4). Verify on the proof, not on a monitor (A6).
- A PDF from the deck template is screen RGB, not a press file (P6).
