## Layout rules
- Headline (sentence, max two lines), one visual, one supporting line.
- Body not smaller than 18 pt; numbers large and with units.
- Consistent grid, margins, and alignment; slide numbers; footer with date and "Confidential."
- Contrast and alt text; exported PDF checked.
- Hangul only where meaningful, correct and with a paired face.

## Copy rules
"Customer," not "guest." No em dashes. Declarative; no performed conviction. Forward-looking statements are marked as such (projection, assumption) so they are not read as fact. Whether disclaimers are required is a legal question for counsel, not me.
