# T4: St. Elmo space shot at 5pm, "use them"

The photos are not attached, so this is the decision frame. Light at 5pm can be excellent, but "use them" depends on several things that are not about light.

## Questions that decide whether they can be used
1. **What state is the space in?** If it is unfinished (bare, under construction, no fit-out), photos of it show something that is not what customers will see. Presenting the empty shell as the restaurant is misleading. It can be used honestly as "the space as it is today" for a progress story, clearly captioned and dated.
2. **Access and permission.** Is the lease signed, or do we have the landlord's permission to photograph and publish images of the property? If the lease is not signed, the project is still at concept stage, and a public photo of the address announces a location before the deal is final. Check with Brandon before publishing.
3. **Who shot them, and who owns them?** If a founder shot them, fine. If a contractor or friend did, get a written license.
4. **People and neighbors.** No identifiable people, neighbors' windows, license plates, or other businesses' signage unless cleared.
5. **Quality.** Phone or camera? Resolution for the intended use, straight verticals, exposure, and consistent color.

## Where they could work
- Behind-the-scenes or progress story on social, captioned with date: "St. Elmo, today."
- The neighbor announcement, if the framing is honest about the stage.
- Internal reference for the photographer's shoot brief (best use right now).

## Where they should not
- The opening campaign hero or the website hero until the space is complete and shot on purpose.
- Anything implying the room is finished.

## Process
Select the best 5 to 8, color-correct lightly (no changes that alter the architecture), strip location metadata, caption honestly, and keep a rights note. Brandon approves publication.

## Reply
Send the photos and state the lease/permission status, and I return a select, crops, and captions.
