## Tensions
- Findlay's figures are self-reported by the vendor of the method; 2000 and 2012 panels differ in size and products, so the comparison is not controlled. The stronger base is the 2007 wine study cited on the poster (not read).
- Byeon: 4 Korean and 4 Japanese cheongju and sake samples; 80 under-30 and 86 over-30 consumers, 10 sommeliers, 11 trained panelists. Small sample set, one category.

## Not usable
- 93 volatile compound analysis and PLS regression: instrument science, no floor decision.
- Makgeolli polarised-mapping paper (the PMC link): 62 untrained consumers, no training; product description only.
- No tea or controlled crew-training study for sake found.
