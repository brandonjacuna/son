## Tensions
- Guest implies a count (6 to 12) is enough; Hennink separates the count for names from the count for understanding. Use Guest for the seed list, Hennink for the stopping test.
- Both are interview-study results on large samples (60 and 25). Neither tests 4 to 8 people, or practitioners who are also trainees.
