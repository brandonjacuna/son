## Tensions
- Evidence is public-bar counter service, filmed, about 200 customers. It does not show fine-dining anticipation; the seat states transfer to a seated room as reasoned extension and Brandon's elicitation as primary ground.
- Cues here are what customers do to get served. A fine-dining server anticipates before any display, so these are floor-level cues, not the ceiling.
