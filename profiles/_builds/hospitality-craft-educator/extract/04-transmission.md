## Tensions
- 05 and 06 agree that access plus trainer behavior drive learning, but 06 treats LPP as not a teaching technique (p. 20); a designed module must create access, not simulate it.
- 05 is a French grande-ecole setting and 06 a Brazilian pub; neither is fine dining in the US.
