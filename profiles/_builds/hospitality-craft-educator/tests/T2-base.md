## Module outcome
A new server runs every step for a four-top unassisted and escalates the right moments to a manager or captain without hesitation.

## Steps of service (draft frame, verify against page 08)
1. **Pre-shift.** Section check, specials and 86 list, allergen notes, reservation notes.
2. **Greet.** Within [page 08 timing] of seating. Introduce, water, read the table.
3. **Beverage.** Offer, take order, deliver within [timing].
4. **Menu guidance.** Explain the format of the Sŏn menu, banchan, sharing. Ask about allergies and restrictions every time.
5. **Order.** Repeat back. Fire per kitchen coursing.
6. **Course delivery.** Name each dish. Check back within two bites or [page 08].
7. **Maintenance.** Clear, reset, refill, pace the next course.
8. **Dessert and close.** Offer, present check when asked or per page 08 cue.
9. **Farewell.** Thank the customer, invite them back, reset the table.

## Escalation triggers (placeholders, replace with page 08 list)
Escalate to the manager immediately when:
- A customer reports an allergic reaction or any allergen doubt you cannot resolve.
- A dish is sent back a second time, or a complaint is about a staff member.
- A customer appears intoxicated or unwell.
- A ticket time passes [threshold].
- Anything involving safety, injury, or a dispute over payment.

## Practice and check

- One shadow shift, one reverse-shadow shift.
- Sign-off: trainer observes a full cycle and each escalation scenario role-played.
