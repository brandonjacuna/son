# Warmth module

**Verdict: blocked, and reframed.** Required greeting lines on the relational side and "smile more" are rejected (C5, A2). Warmth cannot be promised by a module (C4). What is trainable is where attention goes and how the moment is appraised; the rest is a selection and environment question.

## Why
- Mandated smiles, feelings, or phrases produce surface acting: worse well-being for staff, no better service, and customers read it as going through the motions (C5, A2).
- "Make the staff warmer" is first a question about who is hired and what the floor is like (C4). That routes to people-systems-designer.
- "Cold" is not yet a cue. No incident sits behind it (R1). What did a cold moment look like at one real table?
