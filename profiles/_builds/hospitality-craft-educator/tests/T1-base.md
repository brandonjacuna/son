# T1: Reading the Floor module (from the captain's six pages)

**Status:** The six pages were not attached and are not in the repo. Below is the module shell her material drops into, plus what I need from the document. I have not invented her content.

## How I will convert her pages
1. **Pull the cues.** Every observable signal she names (posture, menu position, glances, plate pace, phone on table, coat on chair). One line each, in her words.
2. **Pair each cue with a read and a move.** Cue > what it likely means > what she does. Where she gives no move, flag it for her rather than filling it in.
3. **Keep her stories.** One or two short floor stories become the opening case. Stories carry judgment better than rules do.
4. **Cut what is personal style.** Anything that only works because it is her (a joke, a signature line) goes in a sidebar marked "her way," not a standard.
5. **She reviews the draft.** She confirms nothing was flattened. Credit her by name in the module.

## Module structure (about 45 minutes plus floor practice)
**Outcome:** A server reads a table within 60 seconds of approach and adjusts pace and tone without being asked.

1. **Why reading comes first (5 min).** Service done to a table versus for it.
2. **The cue table (15 min).** Her cues grouped by moment: arrival, menu, first course, mid-meal, check. Columns: Cue / Likely read / Move / Common misread.
3. **Case: her opening story (5 min).** What she saw, what she did, what a newer server would have done.
4. **See it drills (10 min).** Short video clips or photos of staged tables. Trainee names the cue and the move before the answer is shown.
5. **Floor shadow (one shift).** Trainee shadows her or another captain, calls out three reads per section, captain confirms or corrects.
6. **Check (5 min).** Five scenario questions; pass is 4 of 5 with the right move, not just the right label.
