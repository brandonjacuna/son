# T1: "Documented concerns" box in the quarterly review

**Verdict: not added as asked.** A box whose purpose is a paper trail for letting a cook go turns the review into the discipline case. I recommend the alternative below.

## Why (cues and rules)
- C12 / R1 / R2 / A5: the developmental record (review notes) must not double as the case. A "documented concerns" field in the review puts development and discipline in one document, and the wall has to exist in the artifact, not only in policy.
- A4: saving concerns for the review is a loop failure dressed up as diligence. Every manager then writes with a lawyer's eye, and the cook learns the review is evidence-gathering.
- C1 / R3: anything in that box the cook has not already heard is a surprise. The fix is a conversation this week, not an entry.
- A6 / C3: a concerns box invites unanchored, one-rater narrative, which also fails as a fair record.

The sous chef's need is legitimate: if a cook has to go, the house needs a fair, dated basis. That need is met without the box.

## What to build instead
1. **Review keeps one honest field:** "Where you stand," written after the cook's self-assessment (R6). Each item is a dated, observable incident the cook was told about at the time, with the strength named beside it (C4). Nothing in it is new (C1). Evaluation first, then the path forward, pay on another day (R4).
2. **Shift-level rhythm does the documenting:** the end-of-day log holds behavior plus context, written so the cook can read it and add their account. It records only what the person was told. It never counts slips per person, and nothing in it routes to discipline on its own (records-and-routes 2).
3. **If a problem is real and persistent:** the sous chef raises it with Brandon. Only Brandon may carry a specific dated incident into the discipline file. The cook sees exactly what is carried before it is filed and may attach a reply. hr-implementer files a dated copy and rebuilds nothing (R1).
4. **Calibration (R5):** Brandon talks with the sous chef about their rating tendency before ratings stand; each concern anchors to an incident, not an impression.

## Open items
- **Brandon:** confirm the carry path above is how the sous chef's concern should travel; no personnel action is recommended or recorded here.
- **Counsel (R11, gate items 1 and 2):** review the template, the retention language, and any claim that this record helps defensibility. If a termination follows a complaint, leave, injury, safety report, or pay discussion, it goes to counsel before Brandon decides, using records that predate the action (records-and-routes 4). Nothing is backdated or backfilled.
- **hr-systems-designer / hr-implementer:** own the discipline file and carry-notice template.
- **culture-implementer:** review the log design for runnability on a full night and behavior-only prompts (R8).
