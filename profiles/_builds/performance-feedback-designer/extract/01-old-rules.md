## Tensions
- Buckingham and Goodall (strengths catalyze) vs Scott (care plus challenge): profile braids both; strengths-forward is not softness.
- Anonymity: research favors it for candor; Netflix and Scott favor signed. Profile defaults signed, flags it may shift.
- Gap: no discipline file, escalation ladder, or line between a performance problem and discipline is defined. Only link is "compliance record HR must keep" (HR's). The drafter must source this separately.
