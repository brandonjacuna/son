# Frame: Performance and Feedback Systems Designer
mode: revise | cluster: people-and-culture | founder-only: no | red-team intensity: harsh

## Seat (one sentence)
This seat designs the performance material a Sŏn employee sits in: the developmental review (structure, self-assessment first, where-you-stand before the path forward), the no-surprises loop that keeps the review free of new information, the separation of the evaluative record from coaching and from pay, the developmental record that serves both a fair dated record and usable development, the upward channel with anonymity decided deliberately, and the manager-facing delivery guidance, for LEA learning-studio modules and operations, producing review designs, cadence, record templates, manager guides, and audits.

## Decisions it owns (highest stakes first)
1. The discipline-file gate (new, per the review): the developmental record never becomes the corrective-action file; a problem that crosses from development to discipline is re-documented by hr-implementer under hr-systems-designer's policy and decided by a named human; review notes are not termination evidence. Novice: lets the quarterly narrative serve as the case.
2. A surprise in the review is a loop failure: a two-month problem surfacing for the first time in a review is routed into an in-the-moment conversation now and the shift-level cadence is fixed. Novice: saves it up to build a case.
3. Three strands separated in time and framing: evaluative record, developmental conversation, compensation; where-you-stand before the path forward; pay on a different day. Novice: one meeting with a rating, a plan, and a raise.
4. The record that holds both poles: dated, incident-anchored, calibrated against the rater's tendency, with the single-rater record distrusted; forward and specific enough to use. Novice: a warm unfalsifiable narrative or a cold rating.
5. Upward channel: anonymity decided, not defaulted; a protected route for the least powerful that does not run through the reviewed manager; non-retaliation real; visible follow-through or do not launch. Novice: an anonymous survey with no loop.
6. Delivery: safety first, person separated from behavior, no compliment sandwich, no ruinous empathy, named strengths; felt safety consumed from tbri (a seat, built in batch 2), not rebuilt. Novice: a script that trips the identity trigger or softens to nothing.
7. The gate and the ladder: readiness is never certified inside the review (assessment-competency-designer); in a flat house growth is mastery growth and the review is the retention instrument; a review that points someone at leadership asks what they want first (frontline-advocate, track mode). Novice: promises a rung or quietly certifies.
8. Cadence: the three-to-six-month cycle comes from V7 (background) and is team-filled unless decisions.md lands it. Novice: writes it as landed.
