# Veteran server cross-training onto garde manger

**Verdict:** Instruction is the lever. Build one module branched inside one person (R1, E1). Skip what a gate or observed performance shows held. Give full support where the learner is new. This is not the new-hire module.

**Opening (R11):** Reason first. A server who knows cold-station timing and allergen flags reads tables better and calls plates correctly. One sentence, carried into the plan step.

**Stage branch and fade (C2, R2):** Skip service pace, standards, and ticket flow only where a gate or observed performance shows them held (that read is assessment-competency-designer's). Knife work and station schema get full support: worked example, critical feature marked for two attempts, then one prompt, then nothing. The job aid comes off after two clean unaided runs under normal conditions, tuned against the floor.

**Load and medium (R3, C3):** Teach knife cuts in isolation, then integrate. Narrate the demo. No captioned video. Video, if used, is input only (C4).

**Practice wrap (R7, R8, C10, C11):** Plan (what, in what order, where the risk is, said aloud). Do (real components, real station, learner chooses the order). Review (reconstruct the sequence and the decision made, C14). Triggered by cross-training, not applied to routine work.

**Standard versus whole task (R6):** Cut specs get a crisp behavioral objective and spaced retrieval. Plating and station flow get whole-task practice with a modeled example and coaching, never atomized steps.

**Retrieval and spacing (R4, C5):** Reps spread across shifts, retrieval a shift later, spaced return a week on. Added paid shifts go to frontline-advocate. Retrieval items never enter a gate bank; share the item list with assessment-competency-designer.

**Behavioral measure (C15):** On the station during service, a read of one best and one worst case. No confidence rating.

**Bindings and owners (R13):** Knife spec, station layout, mise, and schema are `chef.*` (chef-gated). Allergen matrix is a binding owned by the chef. Ticket steps are `tool.pos.*`. Expert judgment spoken aloud comes from hospitality-craft-educator; I invent no floor cues. Practice conditions go to practice-simulation-designer.
