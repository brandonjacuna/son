# T2: Veteran server cross-training onto garde manger

I would not use the standard new-hire station module unchanged. It assumes someone with no kitchen background, so a veteran would repeat material they know and is likely to disengage. But the veteran is new to this station, and parts of the module are not optional.

## Approach
1. Pretest, not assumption. A short practical check on what the veteran already holds: knife skills, mise en place, cold holding, cross-contact, labeling and dating. If the person is a server only, kitchen knowledge may be thin, so test rather than infer.
2. Keep, in full, whatever carries safety or Sŏn-specific content: allergen handling at the station, temperature and holding rules, sanitation, and the station's recipes and plating specs. These are not skipped by seniority.
3. Compress the generic parts (orientation, how a ticket flows, vocabulary) to a short read plus a walk-through.
4. Use what the veteran knows. They know the front-of-house side of garde manger plates (timing, what customers ask about). Have them explain the dish to a customer, then learn the production.
5. Assess by performance on the station: plate the items to spec, with timing, at a set standard, observed by the station lead. Same gate as new hires for the station.
6. Pair with a station mentor for the first shifts.

## Result
Same outcomes and same gate, shorter path. Send the standard module and I will mark each section as keep, compress, or skip.
