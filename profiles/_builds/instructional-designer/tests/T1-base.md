# T1: Ticket mis-fires after the POS module

I would not rebuild yet. "Mis-firing" is a symptom with several different causes, and each needs a different fix. A rebuild that guesses wrong repeats the problem in a nicer package.

## Diagnose first (a day or two)
- Pull examples: what kind of mis-fire? Wrong course timing, missing modifier, allergen flag skipped, wrong table or seat, fired too early or late.
- Where does it happen: during rushes only, with certain menu items, with certain trainers or shifts?
- Was the skill ever demonstrated in the module, or only explained? Do servers pass the module and still fail on the floor?
- Is the POS layout itself causing errors (a design fix for the system, not training)?
- Do they know what the kitchen does with the ticket, i.e. the consequence?

## Likely rebuild if it is a skills gap
1. Start from the real errors. Build the module around the five most common mis-fires.
2. Practice, not narration: a practice POS mode where trainees enter realistic tickets with modifiers, allergen flags, and seat numbers, with immediate feedback.
3. Show consequence: a short look at what arrives at the kitchen from a correct and an incorrect ticket.
4. Build a one-page job aid at the terminal (check before you fire: table, seat, course, modifier, allergen).
5. Spaced retrieval: a short ticket drill in the first three shifts and again after two weeks.
6. Assess by observation: a lead checks the first 10 real tickets against a short list, and the error rate is tracked.

## Success measure
Mis-fire rate per 100 tickets before and after, for new servers. Set the target with the lead.
