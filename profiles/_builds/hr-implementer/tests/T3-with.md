# T3 SAF module linked row: vendor timekeeping SOP, 30-minute auto-deduct over six hours

Verdict: does not run as written. I cannot confirm it meets the requirement (R11, C3, A1).

## Why
- The SOP auto-deducts 30 minutes per shift over six hours with no step to verify the break was taken and duty-free. A worked-through break is unpaid worked time. Work suffered or permitted is work time (kb item 17, last_verified 2026-10-09). In a restaurant on a busy night, breaks are the first thing skipped.
- Overtime is at 1.5 times the regular rate over 40 hours in a workweek (kb item 17), so unpaid deducted time can also become unpaid overtime. This feeds the look-back: 2 years, 3 if willful (kb item 9).
- The "30 minutes over six hours" figure is the vendor's. The kb does not list a Texas or federal meal-break requirement, so I cannot confirm that threshold. Open question for counsel; I will not supply a number from memory (R1).
- I also have not been given the text of the SAF requirement the row links to. If it states a different standard, the answer changes. Please send it.

## Controls needed for the row to pass
1. Deduct only on confirmation: the employee records the break start and end, or confirms or corrects the deduction on the punch screen. No confirmation, no deduction, and the time is paid.
2. Reason required on every edit to time; the employee sees edits to their own time; audit one supervisor editing often (C1).
3. Pre-shift arrival, setup, and onboarding trips are captured from arrival (C2).
4. Time records retained, not reconstructed; retention periods per kb item 10, Texas period not verified.
5. `tool.*` binding for timekeeping: scheduling vendor is a candidate only; no system confirmed.
