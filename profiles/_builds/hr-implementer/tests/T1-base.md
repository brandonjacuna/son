# T1: Separation checklist, lead terminated yesterday (2026-10-08)

Assumptions: involuntary termination, Austin, Texas. I have not seen the file, the incident record, or whether the lead is a minor, on leave, or has a pending complaint. Items marked CHECK need that answer. Verify legal deadlines with employment counsel or the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) before relying on them.

## Today (day 1 to 2)
1. Confirm the incident record is complete: dated, factual, witness statements, prior warnings. Missing pieces get written now, labeled with today's date.
2. CHECK risk flags: recent complaint, injury or workers' comp claim, leave request, accommodation request, protected-class issue, wage question. If any exists, stop and get counsel before further steps.
3. Cut access: POS, scheduling, payroll, email, door codes, keys, shared logins. Change any shared passwords the lead knew.
4. Collect property: keys, uniform, badge, cards, devices.
5. Tell the team in one neutral line: who covers the lead's duties. No reasons, no commentary.

## By day 3
6. Pay: Texas Payday Law requires final wages within 6 calendar days of discharge. Target: pay by 2026-10-14 at the latest, sooner is better. Include all hours, tip share owed, and any approved PTO if policy says it is paid out. Do not deduct for unreturned items without written authorization.
7. Run the final hours and tip reconciliation against the timekeeping record, including breaks.
8. Send the separation letter: last day, final pay date, benefits status, how to reach HR, return of property.

## This week
9. Benefits: end date for health coverage. If a group plan exists, notify the plan administrator or carrier so continuation (COBRA or Texas state continuation) notices go out. CHECK plan size.
10. Unemployment: expect a TWC claim notice. Calendar the response deadline (printed on the notice, short) and respond factually from the incident record.
11. Update payroll, org chart, schedule, and system records. Remove from vendor accounts.
12. File the termination record in the personnel file (separate from I-9 and medical files). Retain I-9 per rules.
13. Handle reference requests: route to one person, confirm dates and title only.
14. Cover the gap: reassign lead duties, check scheduling coverage and any lead-level approvals or certifications.
15. Debrief the two managers involved: what happened, what to fix in the process. Do not discuss with the team.
