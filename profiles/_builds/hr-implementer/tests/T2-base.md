# T2: Phone-on-the-floor rule rewrite

I was not given the current rule text, so this is a draft from scratch. Replace it in place once you send the original, and check what it said so the change is not read as new discipline for past behavior.

## The real problem
The wording is not the main issue. Two managers enforce differently and the top server is exempt. An unwritten exception for one person creates a fairness and discrimination risk (inconsistent discipline is the first thing a complaint points to) and teaches the team that the rule is optional. Fix: one rule, one enforcement ladder, any exception written and role-based, not person-based.

## Draft rule

**Phones on the floor**

1. Personal phones stay off the floor. Floor means dining room, bar, kitchen line, expo, and host stand while a customer is present or service is running.
2. Keep your phone in your locker or bag, or in your pocket on silent, face down, never in your hand.
3. Use it on break, in the back of house break area, or off the floor.
4. Allowed on the floor: work apps on a Sŏn device, a call about an emergency (step off the floor first), and anything a manager has approved for your role in writing.
5. If you need your phone for a family emergency on a shift, tell your manager before service. They will approve it.

**Enforcement (every manager, every employee, the same way)**
- First observation: verbal reminder, logged with date and time.
- Second: written warning.
- Third: final written warning. Further steps follow the standard discipline policy.
- Performance, tenure, and sales do not change the ladder.
- Managers log every reminder in the same place, so the record shows consistent application.

**Exceptions** are listed here by role and reason. None currently exist.
