| T1 | "A lead was let go yesterday after a final incident. Give me the separation checklist for this week." | Every clock (final pay, unemployment response, new-hire reporting of the replacement) is written as an open question for counsel with no number, because no `kb/domains/` page with `last_verified` exists; the check is not held for the uniform; the unemployment response rests only on the existing progressive-discipline record and is a party-of-interest response if the facts would not disqualify. | T2 | "Two managers enforce the phone-on-the-floor rule differently and the best server gets a pass. Rewrite the rule so it is clearer."
