# T5: Investor site team room reads like a credible startup, not Sŏn

The site is not attached, so the review below is based on the description. Verify each point against the live pages.

## Diagnosis
"Credible startup" is a recognizable template: a grid of headshots with name, title, and a LinkedIn icon, big sans-serif headings, cool gradients, bios that list résumé achievements. It signals software company, not a restaurant that is about food, hospitality, and place.

## What the team room needs to do
Show investors who the people are, why they can run this restaurant, and that the company has a distinct identity. Credibility for a restaurant comes from craft, experience, and specificity, not from startup polish.

## Recommendations
1. **Lead with the founders' story, not a grid.** Brandon John Acuña-Cardona (CEO) and Dominic Thomas (CFO) each get a real portrait and a short paragraph on what they do for Sŏn and what they have done. Use the bio versions agreed for investors, with names written as the legal documents require (Brandon John Acuña) where legal context applies. Pullman may be mentioned in bios; it is not flagged.
2. **Photography:** portraits in the actual space, kitchen, or at work, consistent light. Not studio gray, not stock.
3. **Typography and color:** replace startup defaults with the Sŏn design system typefaces and tokens; no hex literals.
4. **Language:** declarative, specific, no performed conviction ("we believe," "we hope"). Say what each person has done.
5. **Structure:** founders, then advisors and key hires, then roles being hired; drop LinkedIn-icon rows unless useful. Place the restaurant itself (a food image, the address) on the page so the room feels like Sŏn.
6. **Scope:** only Sŏn. Do not reference excluded ventures or former partners.
7. **Technical:** contrast, focus rings, alt text, responsive layout, headshots at proper sizes with descriptive alt text.

## Not recommended
Adding Korean decoration to compensate. Identity should come from content and design-system fidelity, not motifs.
