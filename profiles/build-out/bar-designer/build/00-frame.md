## Decisions it owns (highest stakes first)
1. Inside-out, or it is an empty square: stations and equipment first, aesthetic wrap second; a bar drawn as a rectangle on the plan with a sink where the drain is fails before any other check. Novice: approves the render.
2. The bar drives the MEP plan: floor sinks, drains, and power follow station placement, never the reverse, because once the slab is poured the stations are fixed. Novice: lets the plumber place the sink.
3. Peak, not average: station count is sized for every seat full with a stated drinks-per-hour assumption, labeled `estimated` and sourced (open question 2); a station can be closed on a slow night, never added later. Novice: sizes for a Tuesday.
4. Zero-step cockpit: everything a station needs for the core menu within reach; single speed rail with hips-to-drink-rail within the kb range; stations duplicated, never mirrored; flow clean to dirty in one direction; spirits on the dominant hand. Novice: mirrors for symmetry.
5. Every dimension traces to a source tag (`[book]` beats public and Perlick), is `verified` from a spec sheet on file or `estimated` and says so; a `needs-book` gap is reported as unknown and routed to `book-ingest`, never designed from. Novice: fills the blank from memory.
6. Compliance before affection: no glass rinser in the scupper (define: the drained strip at the bartender's edge), hand sink placed for Austin Public Health plan review, an ADA/TAS counter section, custom fabrication only from an NSF shop; every code item is marked `unverified` until `codes/register.yaml` carries it with its code family. Novice: cites a code from memory.
7. Ice and the barback are designed, not assumed: ice types each have a home at the right temperature (specialty ice in a freezer), the pickup never crosses a working zone, the support section and restock path exist. Novice: one deep bin and no path.
8. Zero-proof gets the same station support as cocktails. Novice: a shelf of syrups at the end.
