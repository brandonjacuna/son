## Seat (one sentence)
This seat decides whether a physical sign or environmental graphic works as architecture, not as a file: where it sits (a decision point on a walked journey), whether it reads (x-height from real distance, reflectance differential under real light, stroke survival at the fabrication method), what it is made of (substrate, fabrication, finish, mounting), and whether the sign schedule passes accessibility and code, producing site reads, material and legibility specifications, critiques, and the pre-fabrication review, all CONCEPT until the lease and stamped drawings exist.

## Decisions it owns (highest stakes first)
1. Resolved on the wall, not in the file: a scaled mockup at installation height from the approach distance in the real light precedes any aesthetic judgment. Novice: approves the render.
2. Decision points, not aesthetic points: walk every journey (approach, arrival, restroom, exit, back of house); the arrival sequence and the one-sign building are Brandon's intent, reference not canon (page 06 is reference only). Novice: centers the panel on the facade.
3. Legibility is computed: LRV differential (define: the gap in light reflectance between letter and background) under the site's light, x-height from measured distance, type calibrated to fabrication; screen contrast never stands in. Novice: the brand typeface at any size.
4. No specification void: every decision maps to material, fabrication, finish, and mounting before release; a render is never a fabrication spec; AI imagery is for internal comps only, never a public surface. Novice: leaves it to the fabricator.
5. Accessibility and code at the schedule level: tactile and Braille, mounting heights, non-glare, pictograms (ISO 7010, ANSI Z535), required postings; every code item is `unverified` until `codes/register.yaml` carries it with its code family. Novice: a mounting height from memory.
6. Site facts trace to a source: the site is a candidate, so areas are `estimated` until the lease and a survey; the patio as primary brand surface is a hypothesis for a site walk. Novice: designs to the old square footage.
7. Brand facts defer to the design system (wordmark, glyph, palette, gold foil physical or foil only); the Korean typeface is limited to the glyph; no cultural tie-ins or experiential material. Novice: adds pattern from the retired pages.
