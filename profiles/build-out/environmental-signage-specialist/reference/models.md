# Models and pre-fabrication review: Environmental Signage Specialist

Read when running a pre-fabrication review or when a cue needs its reasoning. Figures here are `unverified` unless a primary source is filed in `codes/register.yaml` or `kb/`.

## Mental models
- M1. Materiality is a design decision, not a finish note. Substrate, finish under light, adhesion to a textured wall, and how dimensional letters attach decide whether the sign survives and reads.
- M2. Environmental work is site-specific or wrong. Approach light, background luminance, seasonal sun, the competing visual field, and the real wall material live at the site, not in the file. At a candidate site they are required inputs, not assumptions.
- M3. Decision-point blindness: work made on a screen places signs where they look right on the elevation and ignores flow and sightlines. The walked journey is the test.
- M4. Legibility is calculated, not felt. Distance sets x-height; light and finish set the reflectance differential; the fabrication method sets the minimum stroke and counter. Rule-of-thumb ratios carried from the old profile (about 1 inch of x-height per 30 to 40 feet; a 70 percent LRV differential for primary wayfinding; 40/60 copy to whitespace) have no primary source here and stay `unverified`.

- M5. Viewing angle: a high-mounted sign read from eye level distorts; test from the real eye point, and letter compensation may be needed (moved from cue C7).
- M6. Color approved on screen shifts in the physical world; require drawdown samples (color swatches printed or painted on the real substrate) under installation lighting (moved from cue C10).

## Pre-fabrication review, by domain
- P1. Scale and legibility: x-height from measured distance, stroke and counter survival for the method, hierarchy per panel, eye-level distortion for high mounts.
- P2. Material and substrate: substrate, thickness, finish, adhesion to the actual wall, edge wear in high-traffic zones, color by drawdown under installation light.
- P3. Lighting and context: sun and shadow through the day and season, background luminance at the approach angle, uniformity of any illuminated sign, viewing-angle distortion, shadow direction on dimensional letters.
- P4. Wayfinding logic: decision-point coverage, progressive disclosure walked end to end, confirmation signs, naming consistency, information load per panel, one arrow convention, integration with egress.
- P5. Accessibility and code: schedule-level audit of tactile characters, Braille, mounting heights, non-glare, pictograms, required postings; each item points to a `codes/register.yaml` entry or is `unverified`.
- P6. Gap to fabrication-ready: outlined type files, font licensing, color file format, alignment with the current drawings, message schedule version. Check the message schedule version against the current drawings; a schedule older than the drawings has drifted (moved from cue C14).
