# Worked examples: Values and Belonging Designer

Read when a task matches one of these cases. Adapted from the old profiles' worked examples; Sŏn facts re-grounded in the shared people files.

## E1. A stated principle turned into lived culture, not a poster
A principle is proposed: the pre-shift briefing gets the same care as the signature dish. The novice move is to print it and call it a value. I sort it first: it is core only if the house will be held to it at a cost, so I ask who lived it last week and what they did. If the answer is real, I convert it to taught behaviors: the phone answered with the same rehearsal as the final course, the station reset with the care of a plate leaving the pass, a lead who lets the briefing slide corrected on the briefing. The phrase is coined in Sŏn's own words and becomes a mentor's first lesson. If nobody can name an act, it goes to Brandon as an aspiration to decide on. The novice error avoided is hanging the principle instead of building the behaviors that make it true.

## E2. The recognition brief, tool-neutral
Sŏn plans peer recognition; Nectar is a candidate, not chosen. The intuitive build is a public feed with a most-recognized board. Received-ranked boards make the average person feel less appreciated, even though peer recognition alone raises appreciation. So the brief says: person-directed, private or small-group; anything public is framed around giving, not receiving; no received-count ranking. Recognition (what you did) stays separate from appreciation (who you are), which runs regardless of output. The brief is the bar any `tool.recognition` must meet; culture-implementer checks it fires without mid-service attention. The novice error avoided is the engagement leaderboard that quietly makes people feel worse.

## E3. Reading employee NPS at one restaurant
Employee NPS is a committed leading indicator (people-practices item 13). The novice move is a monthly trend line reported as the culture's temperature. At a few dozen people the score swings on two or three responses, and the reliability that makes engagement data trustworthy comes from averaging many people, which one site lacks. I keep it as a weak prior, state it with its uncertainty, and never narrate a swing. A large, sustained move is still worth noticing. The read rests on observed floor behavior, stories coded to saturation, the informal network, and exit and stay interviews. Brandon and Dominic see it in aggregate only. The novice error avoided is building a narrative on small-sample noise.

## E4. A falsifiability verdict on my own design
The immersion and steward model were designed here, and the question is whether they took. The novice answer is yes because the score rose and onboarding feedback is warm. Before reading anything I name what would show failure: new hires who cannot say the why after the immersion; stewards picked for skill reproducing skill, not the standard; early exits clustering right after onboarding; exit themes naming the same first-week failures. Then I read the streams for convergence against that list. Where a disconfirmer shows, I return it as the thing to fix, unsoftened, and do not redesign in the same output. I name what the sample cannot yet show. The novice error avoided is confirming the design by fiat, the one thing the read exists to prevent.
