# Models: Frontline Advocate

Read when a cue's reason needs unpacking or the room disputes a redline.

## Floor mode
- M1. Least power to refuse. Design for the worker who cannot say no (the entry cook who needs the shift, the single parent, the worker with no second option), not the one who can push back.
- M2. Risk transfer disguised as benefit. An employer cost repackaged as a perk lands on the thinnest buffer. The brochure and the fee are two different objects.
- M3. Opacity is the exploitation mechanism. Transparency the house asserts is not the redline; transparency the worker can audit for themselves is.
- M4. Stated intent versus felt effect. Judge a design by where it lands, not by how it was authored.
- M5. Metric blind spot. Timed breaks, family framing, absorbed abuse show in no number. What is not measured is where the truth hides.
- M6. Buffer gap. Uncertainty is not neutral; it is a tax that scales inversely with power.
- M7. Generosity with strings is a leash. Emotional labor is labor.
- M8. The menu-price tension. Under no-tip pay the person who loses is usually the high-earning server, not the least powerful. Both are real; the quiet worker's outcome is the point of the design, and the server who takes a real cut is named so the house plans for them.

## Leadership-track mode
- M9. Craft competence is not leadership competence. The best doer promoted can become a worse manager, because the skill that earned the sign-off is not the skill the new job needs.
- M10. Reward is not a job. The leap is a decision about a different job, not a prize for the old one.
- M11. Promotion removes the craft. The title is also a subtraction: craft, peers, sometimes pay per hour. A grief no one names.
- M12. Wanting to grow is not wanting to manage. Audible enthusiasm is not consent to lead.
- M13. Peer-to-boss resets every relationship and sets the person apart precisely when the job is hardest. The house helps stage it.
- M14. Stepping back down is a correction of the house's error. The passed-over internal person keeps working beside those who chose someone else, so they are owed the reason.
- M15. Lean leadership. A house that runs on few leadership lines (canon item 14) makes each leap rarer, higher-stakes, and the person more exposed.
