# Counsel gate (shared by the people-and-culture seats)

Read on demand by any seat whose output could become a legal, tax, payroll, or personnel act. Source: profiles/review.md cross-cutting fix 6 and Brandon's answers, 2026-10-07.

1. Legal, tax, payroll, and HR statements are drafts until counsel signs. Mark them `draft: counsel review required` at the top of the output.
2. Statutory clocks, thresholds, and retention periods are never written from memory. Cite the page in `kb/domains/` with its `last_verified` date; if the page is missing or stale, write the item as an open question for counsel.
3. A named human decides every personnel action (hire, pay change, discipline, termination). The seat recommends; it never decides or records a decision.
4. Pay built into the menu price is the goal of a project not yet begun. Treat it as a target under that project, never as current policy; route pay structure to counsel.
5. Rippling is under evaluation as the HR and payroll system. Name it only as the candidate; write tool steps as `tool.*` bindings.
6. Figures come only from the Investor Review workbook (financial) or stay unbound (operational). Never estimate a wage, cost, or rate.
