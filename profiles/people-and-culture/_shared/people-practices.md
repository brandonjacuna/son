# People practices

Shared people canon for the people-and-culture and scaling-people seats. Confirmed against the white paper (Box 2466517057642) on 2026-10-09; Brandon approved confirmed items as canon. Items not confirmed are targets, not policy.

Locations are white paper page numbers (Part in parentheses). Source numbering follows `profiles/_builds/hr-implementer/00-plumbing-gaps.md`.

## Canon (confirmed)

- 6. A paid practical in a real environment is the second interview for roles with a physical skill component. It replaces the unpaid tryout shift (p.18, Part II).
- 7. Onboarding starts before the first shift: information, peer mentor, locker, uniform and shoes ready (p.18, Part II).
- 8. Reviews every three to six months, focused on development. No surprises. Feedback runs both directions (p.19, Part II).
- 9. Full uniforms and shoes company-paid. Secure lockers for every team member. A real employee area with a comfortable break space (p.19 to 20, Part II).
- 11. Labor is an asset to invest in, not a cost to minimize (p.16, Part II).
- 13. Leading indicators: employee NPS, cultural labor score, customer recognition rate. A dedicated feedback channel syncs to the execution system and the loop is closed (p.22, Part II).
- 14. Cross-training builds toward internal succession. Leadership runs on roughly eight lines against fourteen at the comparison room (p.19 and p.22, Part II).

## Targets (partly or absent)

- 4. Flat house, no titled ladder. Partly. The white paper confirms a web with no top or bottom, progression by connections not altitude, and advancement tied to demonstrated mastery (p.9, p.11, p.19). It also defines titled roles (Operations Lead, Maitre d, Lead Host, Station Leads, Head of Beverage; p.10 to 11) and lists role naming as an open item (p.15).
- 10. Blame the process. Partly. Confirmed: management absorbs blame from staff, post-mortems are private and process-focused (p.17). Silent on private individual corrections focused on the behavior.
- 12. Cross-training with paid added mastery and chosen rotation. Partly. Cross-training is career development (p.19), and cross-strand fluency is to be recognized in compensation (p.11), but the compensation architecture is an open item (p.15). Silent on chosen rotation.
- 15. Second unit as a live future risk; culture runs without the founder. Partly. The company is built to run without the founders and hands a second unit a system (p.6, p.16). A second unit is framed as an optional horizon gated on proof, not as a live risk (p.38).
- 5. Team pay built into the menu price (point system by days worked, whole team): the white paper states it in principle (p.20, open decision p.22), and Brandon (2026-10-07) set it as the goal of a project not yet begun. Target only; counsel gate item 4 applies.
