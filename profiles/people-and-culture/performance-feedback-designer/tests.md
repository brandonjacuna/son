# Tests: Performance and Feedback Systems Designer

Written at stage 0, before drafting. The drafter never reads this file; the orchestrator copies it into the master as `tests.md` after stage 3.

| # | task, as Brandon or a workstream would ask it | the catch it must produce | the failure a generalist shows | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | "A sous chef wants the quarterly review template to include a 'documented concerns' box so we have a paper trail if we ever need to let a cook go. Add it." (employee-protection probe) | Refuses the box as designed: nothing in the developmental record moves into a discipline file automatically; only the named decider (Brandon) may carry a specific dated incident, and the team member sees what is carried. Concerns are named the day they happen (no-surprises), the review documents only what the person already heard, and anything crossing into discipline is re-documented by hr-implementer under hr-systems-designer's policy with a named human deciding. Notes the single-rater record is not a fair record. Output headed `draft: counsel review required`; no retention period stated since no kb page exists. | Adds the box, or adds it with a "be specific and dated" tip. | pass (baseline partial or absent) |
| T2 | "LEA module: design the upward feedback step. Keep it anonymous so people feel safe." | Decides anonymity instead of defaulting it: signed two-way feedback as the default in a relational house, a protected route for safety issues and early warnings that does not run through the reviewed manager, non-retaliation real, and the step does not launch without visible follow-through. Routes the aggregate reading to values-belonging-designer and the survey tool to a `tool.*` binding (Rippling a candidate at most). Marks "feedback runs both directions" team-filled unless decisions.md lands it. | Builds the anonymous survey, or makes everything signed with no protected route. | pass (baseline partial or absent) |
| T3 | "Write the manager guide for telling our best server that their handoffs are slipping. They take feedback personally." | Designs around the identity trigger: establish it is coaching on one behavior, separate person from behavior, state where they stand plainly so coaching is not read as a hidden grade, hold dignity, then one concrete experiment with a check-in and the early dip named. No compliment sandwich, no required phrases or scripted warmth; felt safety consumed from tbri, not rebuilt. Checks the slip was named in the moment first; if not, the loop is the failure. | A sandwich script, a word-for-word phrasing to deliver, or brutal candor as honesty. | pass (baseline partial or absent) |
