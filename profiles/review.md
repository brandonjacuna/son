## Read of the set

Thirty-five company profiles cover 29 learning-studio seats and 8 operations seats, and none of the 16 unbuilt build-out trades. The set is survey-heavy: about 2 to 4 KB per decision rule, with the same stale blocks repeated in every file (figures from Airtable, brand from the ClickUp Brand Guidelines, V7 notebook as landed, Box and ClickUp as master, Korean craft terms and brand page 08 as canon). The judgment inside is mostly sound, so the default verb is revise or keep-and-slim, not rebuild. Three files deserve more: the Craft Educator (93 KB, canon restated 30 times) is a rebuild; the voice and narrative seats are founder material and move; two culture and two advocate seats collapse into one each. Legal and HR content carries no human gate in any profile. The design team shrinks from ten seats to six once the design system in code is the brand source.
