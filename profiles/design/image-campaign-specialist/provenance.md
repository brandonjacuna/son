## Tensions kept open
- Scope of the AI refusal: old cues 1 and 11 limited it to public surfaces and allowed internal mood frames (01.1, card 01-old-cues "Not usable"); Brandon 2026-10-10 removed every exception. The seat holds the new line; the tells survive only for inspecting retouched real photographs (C2).
- Personas as casting truth: old cue 7 treated named personas as fact (01.7); the frame says no persona or daypart is canon this seat can read. The seat directs specificity and flags any persona claim (C9, R4).
- Surfaces: old rule 9 checked hero and campaign only; Brandon added social sets, stories, and the reservation photo (R8, R10).
- The maker as production partner: the old scope carried no usage rights, license, retouch, credit, or exclusivity terms (card 01-old-scope tension); the frame makes them open terms for Brandon and counsel (R7, L10 to L12).
- People in frame: the old profile had no consent, release, paid time, or worker-side read; cards 02 and 03 supply draft rows, all draft until counsel.
- Source age: the DMLP guide is unmaintained (about 2014) and the Texas statute was read at a mirror, not the official site; counsel rechecks before any kb page is signed.
- "We paid, so we own": the common view versus the statute, which ties ownership to employment or a signed writing (card 03 tension; C11, L10).
- Neighbors: the old profile named design-director and prompt-specialist seats; the frame names creative-director, web-ui, intake, hr, frontline-advocate, and counsel. The prompt-specialist seam is gone with internal generation.
