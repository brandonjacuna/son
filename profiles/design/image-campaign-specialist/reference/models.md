# Models and the pre-ship review (reference)

Read for a pre-ship review, a severity rating, or an illustration concept.

## Mental models
- M1. The brief is the work. An image with no brief behind it is an accident that happened to be in focus. Review starts from what was briefed, not from what was captured.
- M2. Specificity is not a flourish. A specific face, light, place, and hour are how an image carries memory rather than documentation; the average carries nothing.
- M3. Casting is human art-direction judgment: a face that belongs to a specific community and carries real cultural markers. Getting it wrong is a brand failure, not a craft miss. The audience itself comes from canon; the seat directs only the specificity.
- M4. An illustration concept is either developed or defaulted. The test is whether the first idea was interrogated or shipped: the first five to ten conventional ideas are discarded, and the concept grows by structural analogy, not by drawing the prompt's nouns.
- M5. Approved is coherent. Exceptional is irreducible: remove any element and something unrecoverable is lost, and the result makes the brief look inevitable.

## Pre-ship review: five domains (hero, campaign, social)
- P1. Composition: the squint test, the thumbnail silhouette, entry, path, and exit for the eye.
- P2. Color and tone: temperature, the histogram (clipped skin highlights disqualify), a curve suited to the medium.
- P3. Type integration: a designed type zone, depth cuing, contrast at the weakest point of the background, legible in grayscale.
- P4. Context: reviewed inside the actual live layout, at every aspect ratio, inside safe zones, with the mobile focal point checked on a device, sRGB for web.
- P5. Approved or exceptional (M5).

## Severity map
- S1. Foundational: the art-direction gap, the floating present, literal illustration.
- S2. Structural: average faces, softbox light, default hero framing, casting misread, type sitting on the image, style drift across a series.
- S3. Surface: anything a retouch or re-crop fixes without a new brief.
