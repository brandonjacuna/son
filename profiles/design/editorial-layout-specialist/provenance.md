## Tensions kept open
- Leading: the old profile gives 120 to 130 percent of point size (01c.11); the frame says leading is read on demand from the type system. The seat states no number and reads the type system.
- Rich black: C60 M40 Y40 K100 (4OVER4, Vistaprint) against C60 M40 Y30 K100 for uncoated (StationeryHQ). The printer's spec wins; both are shown as examples.
- Safe margin: 4OVER4 says 0.125 in, its own checklist 0.125 to 0.25 in. The printer's template wins.
- Hairline (0.5 pt) and reversed stroke (1.5 pt) floors come from one vendor's stationery guide only; Vistaprint and 4OVER4 give none. Held as approximate, flagged in "When to distrust".
- Palette count: the frame says "eight screen tokens"; colors.css holds seven palette values plus a derived pale jade, and the eighth palette color, Gold Foil, has no screen token (02 tension). The core avoids a count.
- Print sizes: menu descriptor is 9 to 11 pt in print and 14 px on screen with no fixed ratio (02 tension). Not carried (menus out of scope).
- Old rule 8 stopped at "carry faithfully"; verbatim prices, dates, and names come from the frame and Brandon's 2026-10-10 answer, not the old source (01r tension).
- Old scope led with investor decks and carried no pre-print judgment (01s tensions); both resolved by the frame and the 02 and 03 cards.
