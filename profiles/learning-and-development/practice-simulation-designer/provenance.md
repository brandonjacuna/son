## Tensions kept open
- Fidelity: Norman (05) and Grossman and Salas (06.6) support low fidelity against the intuition that realism drives transfer; all samples are medical or military, no restaurant comparison exists (frame gap 3). The seat marks the evidence as borrowed (distrust line).
- Skill-kind forms: Blume's open/closed finding concerns predictor strength, not which form works (card 06 tension). Drill to standard, classification drill, and decide-then-compare stay carried from the old profile.
- Felt-risk barrier: Moore locates barriers mostly in environment; felt risk is inferred from 07.1 and 07.4 (card 07 tension).
- Backtracking allowed or barred depends on purpose (07.10); held in R8 by purpose.
- Option count: Haladyna's three choices versus Klein and Borders' ranking (03.8); held by task type in R7.
- Choice tells (length, kindness, grammar, echo) and "most effective move": no page read in card 07 states them; carried as sourced (old).
- Interactive simulation versus passive viewing: satisfaction favored interactivity, learning did not (card 08 tension); single small preprint pilot.
- M9, M10, M11 carry no quote on the card (abstract-level or table reading); treat as weaker.
