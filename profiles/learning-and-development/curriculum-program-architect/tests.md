# Tests: Curriculum and Program Architect

Written at stage 0, before drafting. The drafter never reads this file; the orchestrator copies it into the master as `tests.md` after stage 3.

| # | task, as Brandon or a workstream would ask it | the catch it must produce | the failure a generalist shows | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | "Here's the draft onboarding order for servers: week 1 reading the table and adjusting service, week 2 menu, week 3 steps of service, week 4 POS, then the floor gate. Approve it or fix it." | Reading the table presupposes the standard, the menu, pacing, and what a normal table looks like: it moves behind them. Opens instead with one simple, complete run of a shift (epitome), then elaborates. Places the floor gate on the chain and names what it gates, and stops there: the rubric is assessment-competency-designer's. Names the POS path as a `tool.*` binding, not a sequenced fact. Asks for a recent-hire read before locking. | Reorders by taste, keeps the exciting opener, and drafts gate criteria while at it. | pass, 2026-10-09 |
| T2 | "The cost-control module and the ordering module are both approved. Add them to the catalog." | Checks the map, not the modules: a learner can finish both without seeing that ordering decisions are cost-control decisions. Requires the explicit connection or co-location before catalog entry. Any ordering figures or vendor steps stay bindings; back-of-house ordering content is chef-gated. | Adds both rows to the catalog. | pass, 2026-10-09 |
| T3 | "Trainees keep stalling at 'the customer's experience is produced by every function at once, not by the server.' Can we cover it quicker, maybe move it into the first-day welcome?" | Tests it against the five threshold traits (transformative, irreversible, integrative, troublesome, bounded) and reads it as a likely threshold, held as a hypothesis until real trainees confirm. The answer is more room and a spiral return, not speed; the first-day welcome is the wrong slot. Hands how the module teaches it to instructional-designer. | Treats it as pacing, compresses it, moves it to day one. | pass, 2026-10-09 |
