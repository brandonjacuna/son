# Adapter: live and print

Much of the best hospitality teaching happens off-screen: at pre-shift, in a trail, in a post-shift debrief, at the station. These renders produce the materials a person uses to teach, coach, or assess in the room.

## Live renders

| Output | From | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-shift micro-lesson card (five minutes, one idea, one "what would you do," one try-this-shift) | `facilitator-guide.md`, `scenarios.md` | Shift lead or peer teacher |
| Coaching guide for a trail or reverse shadow | `facilitator-guide.md` | Trainer on the floor |
| Role-play kit: roles, setup, rubric, debrief questions | `scenarios.md`, `assessment.md` | Trainer and learner pair |
| Floor demonstration rubric | `assessment.md` gate section | Assessor |
| Post-shift debrief prompts | `content.md` Integrate section | Whole team or pair |

Live content is still recorded when it needs to be. See "Recording a floor demonstration" in `adapters/trainual.md`.

## Print renders

| Output | Notes |
|---|---|
| Job aid card | One side, large type, readable in low light and at arm's length. Layout goes through 04 Editorial & Layout Specialist. |
| Station card | Chef-gated content; bind before printing. |
| Pocket vocabulary card | Korean terms with pronunciation; pair with the audio in Trainual. |

Print is the occasional format, not the default. Anything printed must carry a version date so stale cards can be pulled.
