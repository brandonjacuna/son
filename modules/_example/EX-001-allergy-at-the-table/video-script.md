# Video script

Real footage models the behavior. AI video (if approved, see {{bind:founder.ai_presenter_policy}}) only sets up the situation. Visual direction is a brief for the Design Translating Team, not a prompt.

| Scene | Purpose | Narration or dialogue | On-screen text | Visual direction | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Activate | "A customer who tells you about an allergy is trusting the whole house with their safety." | none | Dining room before service, calm, no people in focus | none |
| 2 | Model | A team member takes an allergy from a customer who minimizes it, asks the three questions, repeats it back | none (narration carries it) | Real footage, two-top, eye level, hands and faces visible | none |
| 3 | Name the moves | Narration names each move over replay of scene 2 | The three questions, once | Slow replay with the moment highlighted | none |
| 4 | Hand off | "Now you try it." | none | Hold on the table | Button: open the scenario |
