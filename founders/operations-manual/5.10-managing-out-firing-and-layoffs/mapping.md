## 3. What was dropped and why

No old item owned here was dropped whole. What left the working manual is framing, brand material, and machinery. First, the verdicts: every "ratify" and "rule" bundle, and every "refused" or "absorbed" row, is now a decision with the old answer as one option and its reasoning (the three kinds of departure, both partners with staying as the default, no negotiated severance, the paid hold, the citation-only reason, the "not available" state, the exit conversation that never feeds a decision). Second, brand material: the line "no exploitation, no toxicity, no disposable people," which the earlier work cited as a value but which comes from the brand material rather than the white paper; the correspondence register and the Playground framework as authorities for how a departure is spoken; the six-question playground test; and findings 13.5 and 13.12. Third, program machinery: session and position citations, the four-part entry test, readiness rows 183 to 200, instrument numbering and the document-methodology gate, decision-rights entry numbering (10.1 to 10.13), the marks and the marks register, and Session 16's groupings of founder modules and questions (their substance is 6.3's). Coined terms such as "field 15," "the stack's closed package," and "the tension-loss row" are replaced with plain words where the idea survives: the departure read's record, the package 5.8.14 hands over, and what a departure costs the team.
