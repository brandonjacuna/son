## 3. What was dropped and why

Three kinds of material left the working manual. First, brand-dependent material: the canon citation for the training philosophy in move 3, and the role-naming seam between the white paper's "Maitre d" and the brand material's "floor manager," which only existed because two documents disagreed; with the brand material out of scope the white paper's name stands and only the one-word "manager" question survives. Second, program machinery: the recommended and founder-gated marks; "ratify" framing; the session cross-references (the internal voice routed to "Session 2," the moves inherited by later sessions); Session 16's re-reading of the six principles and their marks. Third, private vocabulary: "the record," "canon," "the marks and registers"; "code-bearing" and "load-bearing" appear only where the white paper's meaning is stated in plain words beside them. Nothing that survives without brand support was removed.
