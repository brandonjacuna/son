# 1.3 Distinguish between management and leadership: the work

Every decision, action, and deliverable this section of the book calls for, in the order they would naturally be done. Reasoning and options are in `considerations.md`; the book's guidance is in `book.md`. "WP p. N" is the white paper.

### 1.3.1 Decide how Sŏn uses the management and leadership distinction
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: pp. 56 to 60 (leadership sets direction and drives change and solves adaptive problems; management executes, creates stability, solves technical problems; both necessary)
- Default assumption: (WP pp. 09 to 10, 13, 21 to 22) the management layer is cut on a different axis, what and how, into two parallel co-leads who each carry both of her modes; stability is produced by the system, not by a lead; change enters through the process loop. The old synthesis argued that her distinction is used as a triage rule on problems (fix or judgment), that direction lives at the center as a written thing, and that no second axis is laid over the leads; that is option (a) in `considerations.md` section 1
- Depends on: None
- Done when: the founders have chosen from the options in `considerations.md` section 1; if the triage rule is adopted, the question a lead asks of a recurring problem is written in one sentence and handed to 2.2 (decision rights) and 3.3 (lead onboarding); the leads' role descriptions in 4.1 are told whether they carry her vocabulary; agreed by both seated founders
- Replaces old items: 86akh2qfk

### 1.3.2 Decide whether "manager" is a word the house uses, and for what
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: pp. 57 to 58 (her whole vocabulary is manager and leader); p. 18 (management structures work)
- Default assumption: None stated. The white paper uses "management" as a function (WP pp. 09, 17), "manager" mostly for roles it replaces (WP pp. 10, 22), and calls the Maitre d "the Hospitality Manager in full deployment" (WP p. 10). Role naming as a whole is "a separate session" (WP p. 15) and belongs to 4.1
- Depends on: 1.3.1
- Done when: the founders have stated whether the word is used for the two leads, for the function only, or not at all; the choice is written where the founding document (2.1.17), the candidate sheet (WP p. 18, chunk 3.1), and the working-with-me kit (1.1.11) can follow it; the full naming convention stays with 4.1; agreed by both seated founders
- Replaces old items: None (Session 16's "manager gone from the vocabulary" was recorded in the comment on 86akh2qfk and is now a choice)

### 1.3.3 Decide whether leadership without a lead role is named and recognized at Sŏn
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: p. 58 (retain high performers as leaders in non-manager roles); p. 60 (experienced people need mostly a leader)
- Default assumption: (WP pp. 10 to 11, 18 to 19) strands, not levels; designations mark accountability in the moment and are not elevations; cross-strand fluency is the development marker and is recognized in pay; the mentor is a proven cultural steward; anyone qualified can teach and is paid for it; internal succession is the goal. The white paper recognizes the behaviors and does not call them leadership
- Depends on: 1.3.1
- Done when: the founders have chosen from the options in `considerations.md` section 3; if leadership without a role is named, the behaviors it means are listed in plain words and 4.1 (designations and the progression structure) and 5.5 (recognition in pay) are told to design its form; the candidate sheet's advancement path (WP p. 18) is told whether the word appears on it; agreed by both seated founders, with the chef partner's view on the kitchen once seated
- Replaces old items: None
