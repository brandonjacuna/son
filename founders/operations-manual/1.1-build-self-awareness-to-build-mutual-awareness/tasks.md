# 1.1 Build self-awareness to build mutual awareness: the work

Every decision, action, and deliverable this section of the book calls for, in the order they would naturally be done. Reasoning and options are in `considerations.md`; the book's guidance is in `book.md`. "WP p. N" is the white paper. This is a Chapter 1 chunk: the answers here belong to each founder personally. These tasks prepare the conversation and the instruments; nothing in this manual states a founder's values, work style, strengths, or how they work. The founders' rules for disagreeing and for naming what is open are 1.2; the partners' meeting rhythm is 2.3; how the partners decide is 2.1.

### 1.1.1 Decide how the founders make their self-awareness mutual
- Type: Decision
- Phase: Before the first hire
- Book: pp. 36 to 40 (the four principles are all about trust; self-awareness makes mutual awareness possible; once you can name your values and tendencies you can talk about them with your team, and they can do the same)
- Default assumption: None stated. The white paper holds that the house is built so it does not depend on either founder's memory (WP pp. 05 to 06) and that knowledge held in people rather than the system is the failure it names first (WP p. 13); it says nothing about how the founders come to know each other. The old synthesis argued for a written working-with-me document on one shared structure, exchanged among the partners; that is option (a) in `considerations.md` section 5
- Depends on: None
- Done when: the founders have chosen the form of the exchange from the options in `considerations.md` section 5 (a written document each founder writes alone and exchanges; a facilitated conversation with no document; a shared assessment plus a conversation; a document followed by a conversation), stated who takes part (the two seated founders now; the chef partner per 1.1.9), and stated in one sentence what the exchange is for, so the leads later understand why they are asked to do the same; agreed by both seated founders
- Replaces old items: None (the S1 page's "what it is at Sŏn" argument, now an option)

### 1.1.3 Decide which self-assessment exercises the founders use, and whether a formal assessment is bought
