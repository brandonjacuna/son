# Assessment

## Low-stakes checks (learning, not the gate)

Native Trainual test. Randomize order; show 4 of 6 from the pool on each attempt.

1. **Single-select.** A customer says "I'm a little allergic to nuts, but it's no big deal." What do you do?
   - Treat it the same as any allergy: confirm, repeat back, flag, and confirm with the kitchen. (correct)
   - Note it on the ticket but skip the kitchen confirmation since it is mild.
   - Suggest dishes without visible nuts.
   - Ask whether they are sure it is an allergy and not a preference, then decide.
2. **Written response (minimum 20 words).** List the three confirming questions in your own words.
3. **Multiple-select.** Which of these must happen before the order fires? Flag on the ticket (correct). Kitchen confirmation (correct). Lead approval (incorrect). Customer signs off (incorrect).
4. **True or false.** If the customer says severity is low, you can route it as a preference. (False)
5. **Single-select with image.** Photo of a ticket ({{bind:tool.pos.ticket_example_image}}). What is missing? Correct: the kitchen confirmation mark.
6. **Video response.** Record yourself responding to a customer who says, "It's fine if there's just a bit." Thirty seconds or less.

## Role-play rubric (practice)

| Criterion | Met when |
|---|---|
| Hears it | Treats any mention as an allergy |
| Asks all three | What exactly, allergy or preference, traces and shared equipment |
| Repeats back | Restates the restriction in the customer's terms |
| No severity judgment | Routes a minimized allergy the same as any other |
| Warmth | Makes it easy for the customer to be honest ("not difficult at all") |

## Gate: observed demonstration

This module feeds the table-readiness gate. Gate design and validity belong to the Assessment & Competency Designer. Proposed form: during a live or simulated service, an assessor plays or observes two allergy moments (one clear, one minimized) and scores the rubric above; all five criteria met on both.
