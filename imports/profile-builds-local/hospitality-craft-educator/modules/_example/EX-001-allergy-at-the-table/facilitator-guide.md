# Facilitator guide: pre-shift micro-lesson (five minutes)

1. **One line of why (30 seconds).** "The customer can't see the kitchen. We are the only link."
2. **The three questions (1 minute).** Ask the team to say them together. Correct any misses.
3. **What would you do (2 minutes).** Read the setup from Scenario S1. Take two answers. Give the expert rationale.
4. **Try this shift (30 seconds).** "Repeat back every allergy you hear tonight, even the small-sounding ones."
5. **Close (30 seconds).** Name who to tell if one gets missed: {{bind:people.allergy_escalation_contact}}.
