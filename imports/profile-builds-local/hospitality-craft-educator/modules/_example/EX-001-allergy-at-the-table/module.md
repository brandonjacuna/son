# Design rationale

**This is an example module.** It exists to show the package format, bindings, and modality mix. It is not reviewed Sŏn content. Allergen handling is chef-gated and assessment-gated in a real build.

Why this is a good example: the durable content (how to listen, what to ask, why severity is never the server's call, how to recover) is fully writable now. Everything that depends on the operation (how the POS flags it, how the kitchen confirms, which menu items carry which allergens, who the escalation contact is) is a binding.

Modalities: a branching scenario rehearses the judgment moment (the customer who says "it's fine if it's just a little"); a role-play with a rubric builds the spoken performance; a spaced retrieval test keeps the three questions and the routing warm; a job aid supports the point of need; the gate is an observed demonstration.
