## 2. Work style demand differs by daypart: one labor pool, four profiles (`86ajgnhh3`)
Status: to do | Priority: high | Linked tasks: 86ajgmhm4, 86ajgmhnt, 86ajgmhxx

**Routed to: S5 Recruiting (****`86ajgmhm4`****), S6 Hiring (****`86ajgmhnt`****), S8 Team structures (****`86ajgmhxx`****)**

Her work style grid (introvert/extrovert against task/people oriented, producing Analyzer, Director, Collaborator, Promoter) is a self-awareness tool. Used as a team-composition tool in a restaurant it produces a specific, predictable distortion: service pressure selects hard for Director and Promoter. A floor staffed by pattern-matching to the room ends up with almost no Analyzers, at which point nothing gets measured and no one notices.

The four dayparts make this sharper. They do not demand the same profile.

*   **Good Energy, morning.** Repetition, precision, prep, low interaction density, high consequence for small errors. Analyzer and Collaborator work.
*   **Dosi, lunch.** Throughput under a clock. Director work.
*   **Dinner.** The full range. The only daypart that genuinely wants all four.
*   **Luxx, late night.** Energy, presence, the room. Promoter work.

A single house running four dayparts is not staffing one work-style profile. It is staffing four, from one labor pool, with substantial overlap between them.

Interacts with `86ajgmmc3` (four dayparts is not four teams).

Surfaced S1.

## 3. Levels and ladders: does a single house need them at all (`86ajgnj4e`)
Status: to do | Priority: high | Linked tasks: 86ajgmhxx, 86ajgmjey

**Routed to: S8 Team structures (****`86ajgmhxx`****), S13 Formal review + compensation (****`86ajgmjey`****)**

Her checklist assumes job ladders and levels, clearly communicated. Her whole review and compensation apparatus hangs off them.

Sŏn has none, and the open question is not when to build them. It is **whether a single 120-seat house should have them at all.**

The case against: levels in a company that does not grow are a promise the company cannot keep. They manufacture the expectation of a ladder and then supply four rungs.

The case for: without levels there is no legible answer to "what is my path," and a strong candidate will ask on day one.

Downstream of `86ajgnh8n` (Sŏn does not scale to 7,000). If lateral daypart movement is the real structure, then levels may be the wrong shape entirely and something else has to carry what a ladder carries elsewhere.

Do not default to building them because the book assumes them.
