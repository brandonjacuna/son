

A salaried seat holds two things: a salary as its base pay, and points in the pool. The team recommends that a salaried seat is in the pool and not outside it, and states the alternative. In: the seat's points on every day the house is open in the pay period, by rule and not by shift, because a seat that owns a domain across every open period has worked every day the domain ran; the seat's weight is a seat property like any other; and the seat's upside on a full night is therefore real, which "no hierarchy of importance" (WP p.20) and "the people who make the food share in the nights they help fill" require of the seat that runs the pass as much as of the seat that runs the line. A lead in the pool holds no conflict, because a lead moves no one's points and holds no discretion over the pool; the only thing a lead can do to the pool is fill the room. The alternative, salaried seats outside the pool on salary alone: it is simpler to administer, it removes the appearance that a lead is paid from the same share as the people whose reviews they hold, and it makes a full night worth nothing to the two seats that hold it. The team recommends in; the founders decide, with Dominic's domain.

### 10.2 What a lead enters at, named without altitude

Session 8 read a lead's entry as two webs: the lead holds a center seat in the house's web whose connections are the seat's by construction, and enters their own inverted web at its center like every person, at the lead seat's entry horizon (S8 P31; founder-gated at P32). In the architecture, what a lead enters at is a name with no rank in it: the seat's salary at the seat's entry horizon and the seat's point weight, both seat properties on the lead's sheet, the same for whoever holds the seat. A lead's own unlocks (the other lead's domain read through the leads' review, the periods held as they open, cross-domain fluency like anyone's, S8 10.3) add markers and connections as they add anyone's, and nothing else moves a lead's pay. "Further along for a lead is the same measure as for a runner" (S8 10.3), and in pay it is the same mechanism. There is no seat above a lead's to enter and no rank to name; the entry point is the sheet's first horizon, priced. The founders ratify (`86akh9tbz`).

### 10.3 Cross-strand and cross-period fluency, recognized in pay

The record commits cross-strand fluency to be "recognized in compensation as meaningful" (WP p.11), Session 8 defined it so it can be demonstrated (S8 P35), and this page prices it as a mechanism: a marker, on while the connections are current inside the window and off when the window lapses, adding a parameter's value to the person's points on every day it is on. It is recognized as a marker and never as a dimension, a rank, or a seat (S8 P35; S6 6.2). Its value is founder-gated (P26).

Cross-period fluency: the record does not name it and Session 5 and Session 8 argued it into the structure as the second marker (S5 P62; S8 P36), leaving whether pay recognizes it to this page (`86akh7qxy`). The team recommends yes, on the same mechanism, for one reason the day's definition makes plain: a person who holds two periods works one day on a day they hold both, so without a marker the second register earns nothing, and the record's own thesis is that the second register is what the industry's bolted-on daypart could not supply (WP p.2, p.33). The alternative, no marker, would leave cross-period fluency recognized only in connections and the succession pool's order (S8 P36), and the team states it and does not recommend it.

### 10.4 Advancement and unlocks against pay

Each unlock on the person's own seat (field 6's horizons) moves the person's weight to the next horizon on the date the domain lead records the form the assessor scored (S8 P37). The lead may not override the form, so pay progression never waits on a lead's judgment; the review has no part in it; there is no raise cycle, because the weight moves on the record's date and the daily calculation carries it the next day. A competency held in another domain or on another seat (a cross-domain unlock, S8 P39) moves nothing until the person holds that seat, and adds a connection and, where Session 8's definition is met, a marker; the record's ready-now pool (WP p.11) is where that person stands, and the team's reading of "a direct line between learning and earning" is that the line runs through the seat's own horizons and the markers, not through every competency validated anywhere (finding 19.9). That reading is the founders' to ratify with the advancement wording (`86akhcz22`).

### 10.5 Designations, never paid as status

A designation carries no pay line (S10 P33; `86akht376`). The room designation holds the room for a service at the holder's own seat weight, the door designation the threshold, the sequence designation the floor's side of the pass, the interpreter designation the check-in's language, and none is a point, a marker, or a day. The team names the tension the Realist sees and holds the rule: holding the room is real load, and the rotation floor (S10 P33) spreads it as development rather than paying it as status, because a designation day-marker would make the designation a reward, would make the rotation a competition, and would build a permanent room holder by another name (S9 P36). A designation held every service is a reset question about the seat's hours (S9 P32), and if the reset finds the load is a seat's, the answer is a seat and never a pay line on a designation.

**P28 (Recommended).** A salaried seat holds a salary as base pay and points in the pool on every day the house is open in the pay period, by rule; the alternative, salary alone outside the pool, is stated and not recommended. A lead enters at the lead seat's entry horizon, priced as the seat's salary and weight with no rank in the name, and moves as anyone moves.

**P29 (Recommended).** Cross-strand fluency is recognized in pay as a marker, on while current inside the window and never a dimension or rank; cross-period fluency is recognized the same way, because one day counts once and the second register would otherwise earn nothing. Each unlock on the person's own seat moves the weight on the form's recorded date, never waiting on a lead; a cross-domain competency moves nothing until the seat is held.

**P30 (Recommended).** A designation carries no pay line, marker, or day; the rotation floor carries its load as development, and a load that is a seat's is answered by a seat.

**P31 (Founder-gated).** The salaried list as ratified and its pool rule (`86akh9tbw`), what a lead enters at as named (`86akh9tbz`, S8 P32), and the reading that the learning-to-earning line runs through the seat's own horizons and the markers (`86akhcz22`), each extended and not filed twice.

## 11. The routed pay lines: mentoring, authorship, completion, training, translation, and the two values set with Dominic's domain

### 11.1 Mentoring

The mentor is paid as shift for every hour beside the person (S7 P42), and mentoring is "teaching under the record's own word" to be "recognized as teaching in whatever form Session 13 gives teaching" (S7 P35). The form: a teaching marker on the mentor's points on every day a plan they mentor is open, from the mentee's first paid hour to the plan's close as a state, and never per the mentee's outcome, because a mentor paid for a plan held becomes a judge of the person they answer questions for, and the mentor "gives no verdict on the person to anyone" (S7 P42). A mentor may hold one open plan at a time (S7 P41), so the marker is never stacked. Its value is founder-gated (P26).

### 11.2 Module authorship

"Any qualified team member can create training modules and get paid for teaching everyone else" (WP p.19). Session 7's constraint holds: paid on approval against the module standard (S7 I11) and never on a module written, so that volume is not the pressure to fill (S7 P35). The form: a flat payment per module approved, and again per versioned revision approved, paid with base pay on the next pay date and never as a point or a marker, because authorship is an act with a date and not a state held across days. Whether the author also earns per completion by others: the team recommends no, because it would pay authors to push modules onto people and would rank authors by reach, which is the pressure Ma warns against (Deck 3.0, p.10) and a per-person count by another route (S9 P32). The record's "the more modules completed, the more earned" is read in 11.3.

### 11.3 Completion

The record's sentence names the learner: "The more modules completed, the more earned, a direct connection between personal growth and financial reward" (WP p.19), and Session 7 fixed that completion is paid "for completion recorded by the platform, not for a module opened" (S7 P35). The team's reading is that the record's "more earned" for completion is carried by the architecture without a separate payment: every module hour is on scheduled shift and paid as shift (S7 P10), and every completed set leads to an unlock that moves the person's weight (10.4), which is the "direct connection" the sentence promises. A separate amount per module completed is not recommended, because it pays for completing rather than for holding, because the readiness read and not the completion is what validates (S7 P23), and because a per-module amount is a volume incentive the standard exists to refuse. This is a reading of a record sentence against its neighbors and the founders ratify or refuse it; the alternative, a flat amount per completion recorded by the platform under Session 7's constraint, is stated.

### 11.4 Training pay

Session 7 found that the transparency sheet carries "the training plan with pay dates" and the record never says what training pay is (S7 19.5). The form: a person in training is paid base for every scheduled hour from the first paid hour, and holds points in the pool at their seat's entry-horizon weight from the first paid hour, so that a trainee's day counts as a day and their share is the seat's. The team recommends no training weight below the entry weight, because a reduced weight is a probation label written in pay, which the person page refuses in words (S7 P25), because "nothing is required of a person before their first paid hour" and nothing is owed after it at a discount (S7 P15), and because "no one touches a table until they are actually ready" (WP p.19) already means the first weeks are the house's investment and not the person's. The alternative, a training weight, is stated and not recommended; the choice is founder-gated because it is a weight. The paid practical is base pay only, outside the pool, on the employment form counsel sets (`86akh7qwd`).

### 11.5 Translation and interpreting

Translation of a house-written page is paid "on the module authorship model" (S11 P18; `17tn048qc2c`): a flat payment per page version approved by the page's owner and a second holder of the language, paid with base pay, never as a point, and again per version; software-drafted translations are paid on the holder's approval work and marked with the disclosure line (S11 8.4). Interpreting is a designation held for a check-in, a review, a brief, or a why block, on the clock, and like every designation it carries no pay line beyond shift (S10 P33; S11 P19). The founders and the first leads are the pre-opening translators, which puts unpaid founder work and paid lead work on the same pages; the leads' translation is paid as this section states and the founders' is Session 16's.

### 11.6 The generosity range's value and the financial recovery threshold: the setting method

Two decision-rights values Session 3 could not carry (S3 4.2, instances 3 and 8): the server's generosity range stated in kind, a bottle, and not in value; and the recovery threshold above which a server escalates to the room's owner, with no value. The ledger fixes that both are set with Dominic's domain at this session and never appear in the program. The method, without the values: Dominic's domain sets each as a value against the financial model; each is written on the range card of the seat that holds it (S7, `86akhb2r2`) in the seat's languages, because a server told to use their judgment with no number is "unprotected, not free" (S10, `86akht376`) and a value the holder cannot read is not a range; each is versioned, and a change reaches the holder by the check-in and the sheet's version before the brief (S8 P49); each is read at the reset against the range-used count and the escalation count, with under-use as the failure the record names (WP p.26); and each is an operating figure visible to the seat's holder by necessity and to no one who does not hold the range, which sits inside the transparency line's closed half because a range is not a lagging figure (S11 P32). The values are founder-gated and Dominic's.

**P32 (Recommended).** Mentoring is paid as shift and recognized as a teaching marker on the mentor's points while a mentored plan is open, never per the mentee's outcome. Authorship is a flat payment per module or page version approved against the standard, paid with base pay, never a point, and never per completion by others. Translation is paid on the same model per approved version; interpreting is a designation on the clock with no pay line beyond shift.

**P33 (Recommended).** Training pay is base pay for every scheduled hour plus the seat's entry-horizon weight in the pool from the first paid hour, with no training weight below entry; the paid practical is base pay only, outside the pool.

**P34 (Founder-gated).** The reading that the record's "more earned" for completion is carried by shift pay and the unlock's weight move rather than by a separate amount per module, with the alternative stated; the training-weight choice; and the values of the teaching marker, the authorship and translation payments (P26, extended).

**P35 (Recommended).** The generosity range's value and the financial recovery threshold are set by Dominic's domain against the financial model, written on the holder's range card in the seat's languages, versioned, reaching the holder before the brief, and read at the reset against the range-used and escalation counts; the values are founder-gated and never appear in this program.

## 12. Review and pay: separated, and what carries her "pay for performance"

### 12.1 The decision

No review output reaches pay. Not the states, not the person's half, not the "nothing new" mark, not the upward half, not anything said in the block. No check-in line reaches pay (S4 P15). No feedback act of any kind is a pay input (`17tn048qcjg`). The point system's inputs are the four in 9.4, and every one of them is a record written by the stack from a form, a schedule, or a sheet, never by a person's judgment of a person.

### 12.2 The argument

Six reasons, each already in the record or the ledger. The record's review is "development, growth, and the path forward rather than a verdict" (WP p.19), and a review that moves pay is a verdict whatever else it says, because the person in the chair hears the number before the path. The check-in refuses pay (S4 P15) and feedback never touches pay (S12), so a pay-tied review would be the one conversation in the house where the person's work and their wage are in the same room, which is the room the program spent eleven sessions emptying. The person page refuses ratings (S7 P25), so there is nothing on it a pay mechanism could read. The no-surprises rule cannot survive a pay-tied review: a raise is news and its absence is news, and her own chapter is a manual for delivering that news ("Managing disappointment," Book p.417), which is the surprise the record forbids. "No one's advancement waits on a manager's attention or judgment" (WP p.11), and pay progression is advancement's downstream; a lead who could move pay at a review is the manager the advocate exists to remove, and the politics the record says begin where the path is unclear (WP p.11) begin again at the review. And legibility: a person can compute their pay from the seat, the horizon, the markers, and the days (13.2) only because nothing in the computation is anyone's opinion, and the day a review enters it, "am I being treated fairly" (Book p.414) stops being a question the arithmetic answers.

### 12.3 What carries her "pay for performance"

Her philosophy is "higher rewards for higher performance" delivered through a rating (Book p.413). At Sŏn the same function is carried by four mechanisms with no rating in them. The unlock: the person who learns a horizon and holds it on an assessor's read earns the next weight on the date of the record, "a direct line between learning and earning" (WP p.19), which is pay for performance with the performance defined as a competency held rather than a manager's impression of a half. The markers: the person who holds both strands or both periods, demonstrated and current, earns the marker while it is current. The pool's weighting: the person who works the days earns the days, and "the upside of a full night" (WP p.20) is earned by the people who filled it, kitchen included. And the days themselves. What is not carried, deliberately: differential reward for doing the same seat better than another person in it, because the house has no rating of "better" and refuses to build one, and because the record's account of who does the essential work is that the people at the edge carry it together and "no single element bears the load" (WP p.8). A person who holds a seat's horizon and never unlocks earns the horizon's weight and the pool's growth, and is not "successfully meets" or "partially meets"; they hold the seat. Session 14's high, middle, and low performers must not add a pay tier to this (carryover B1).

### 12.4 Her "comparisons," answered by the rule

"So-and-so is getting paid more than me even though they're doing the same job" (Book p.417). At Sŏn the answer is arithmetic and it is public: the same seat at the same horizon with the same markers on the same days earns the same share, and any difference is a horizon, a marker, or a day, each on the person's own pay page. A person cannot see another's page, because review content beyond the person and their lead is founder-gated and a pay page is the person's (S11 P33); a person can see the rule, because the seat weights are on the seat sheets and the transparency sheet's item 6 hands the mechanics to every candidate (WP p.18). Her "geographical location to tenure to when market data was refreshed" (Book p.417) has no counterpart, because none of those is an input. Whether the house publishes every seat's weight to the house, and not only to the candidate for that seat, is section 13.3's and recommended.

**P36 (Recommended).** No review output, check-in line, feedback act, or anything said in a block reaches pay, and the point system reads no person's judgment of a person; review and pay are separated by design, because the record's review is not a verdict, the check-in refuses pay, the page refuses ratings, no surprises cannot survive a raise, the advocate replaces the manager, and legibility requires that nothing in the computation is an opinion.

**P37 (Recommended).** Her "pay for performance" is carried by the unlock, the markers, the pool's weighting, and the days, with performance defined as a competency held on an assessor's read, a fluency demonstrated and current, and the nights filled; no differential reward for doing the same seat better exists, and no later session adds a pay tier. A comparison is answered by the rule, which is public, and never by another person's page.

## 13. Legibility and transparency

### 13.1 What a person can see of their own pay, and when

The pay page (I4), on the team home, on a phone, in the person's chosen language, by the next morning after every day worked: the day counted; the seat and horizon held that day and its weight; the markers on that day; the person's points for the day and how they are composed; the person's share for the day; the accrued share to the pay date and the pay date itself; the base pay hours for the day; and, when a weight moves, the date and the form it moved on. Everything on it is a record the person could open elsewhere (the sheet, the progression state, the schedule, the fluency record), assembled in one place. The page is the person's and their domain lead reads it only at the person's asking or on a correction (16.6); no one else reads it. This is the record's "Employees do not need to ask... They open the system and see it" (WP p.11) applied to pay, and it is her "instill trust in your systems" (Book p.415) built as a surface rather than said as a posture.

### 13.2 What a person can see of the pool's calculation: the arithmetic, held at one figure

To check their own share by arithmetic a person needs the day's total points and the day's pool. The total points is a house figure and not a lagging figure; the team closes it as visible on every pay page. The day's pool is a share of the day's revenue at a known proportion, and a person who can see the pool and knows the percentage from item 6 can compute the day's revenue, which is exactly the lagging figure the transparency line holds founder-gated: "whether any lagging figure appears on any surface the house reads, and which" (S11 P33; `86akh5uby`). The record pulls the other way: the mechanics are handed to every candidate "No surprises and nothing withheld" (WP p.18). This is finding 19.5, the record against the line.
