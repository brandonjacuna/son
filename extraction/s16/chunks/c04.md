## 6. Foster relationships, answered

Her chapter assumes a person inside a hierarchy: a manager above, peers beside, founders somewhere else. At Sŏn the founders' relationships are with each other, with the chef partner, with the two leads, with the team, with the customer, and with outside parties, and none of them runs through a person standing above another. Each is answered as a mechanism the house holds and, where the record leaves the relationship to the people in it, as a founder-gated prompt.

### 6.1 The partners with each other

Two mechanisms carry the relationship and neither is a meeting about the relationship. The working-with-me exchange: three documents on one spine, "a seam document between non-overlapping domains" for founders who "will rarely watch each other work" (S1 section 6). And the domain read: each partner's monthly questions on the other's page, with the load line added by this page (3.2). Her "two and two," knowing the roles two levels above and two below, has no levels to run on; its Sŏn form is two domains out: each founder's prompt A draws their domain's edges and names the three or four decisions nearest the edge that belong to another seat (S1 7.5), and the seat description's sixth field is "every object where this seat's domain meets another's" (S2 P7.1). The seams the program has since found belong on both: the OS surface (S2 P7.2), the Operations Lead's outcomes split between the operating systems and the financial architecture (S3 4.3, Class C), the hiring partner and construction (`86akh6742`, `86akh7qnb`), the generosity range's value and the recovery threshold set with Dominic's domain (S13 P35), and the pay page's legibility against the transparency line (S13 P40), which is the one place the concept founder's "nothing withheld" meets the capital founder's financial model directly.

Her "never surprise them with late news" is Principle 4 applied upward and sideways: the no-surprises rule (WP p.19) applied to the partners means a partner never learns at a partners' review what the other partner has known for a month, and the mechanism is the domain page's state, read weekly, and the live-risk line's daily state. Her "help your manager be their best" has no manager; its peer form is prompt J, what each partner needs from the other's domain and what the other can expect, "a dependency map of the founding team" (S1 7.5) that feeds the partners' review's asks list.

**How a partner's working style changes the weekly review's conduct without changing its agenda** (`86akh6813`). The partners' review's agenda is fixed and versioned (S4 P53) and no document changes it. What the working-with-me documents change, on the table at every gate review as input (S4 9.2), is the review's conduct in four places the agenda does not fix: whether a domain's page is read before the review or presented in it, which the record's "read not presented" already leans toward and a partner who thinks to talk may need stated as a rule; whether a Class B consultation runs in writing or in the room, which prompt D answers for each partner; whether a hold in Class A is written on the page or said, which prompt E answers; and what the chair's clock is, which a partner whose under-load placement drops the people axis (S1 8.2) should ask the other to hold. ### 7.2 A founder's path in a house built to run without them

### 7.3 Her career document against the working-with-me document
