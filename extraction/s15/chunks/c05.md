### 4.6 The conversation

Who says it. The partner who took the decision, and for the third kind the partner whose domain holds the retired seat's period, with the person's check-in holder present. The partner says it because a person hears a decision from the seat that made it, and a lead who announces a decision they could not have made has become the manager who fires. The check-in holder is present because theirs is the relationship the house built with the person, and because the hand-back and the exit interview are theirs. Where the person's check-in holder is the partner, as for a lead, the other partner is present. No one else, except an interpreter designation where the person's language requires one (S11 P20), who writes nothing. Not the other lead, unless the departure follows a line and that lead was the flag's second reader, in which case they are present as the line's co-writer and say nothing.

Where and when. Off the floor, at a closed hour or in a pre-service window with slack, never mid-service, never at the close, never on the floor, never in a room the roster is in, and never by message, phone, or letter first. On the clock, paid as shift, placed by the stack on the person's next scheduled shift after the partners' review and never later than the second, and before any brief carries coverage and before the state is saved on the person page. In the person's chosen language.

What is said, in order, which is the conversation card (I4). The decision as a state, in one sentence, with its citation: the package's date or the line's date or the entry's. The date of the last day. What the person keeps (4.8). Final pay as the pay page shows it, with the accrued share to the last pay date, and that every question about it has the pay page and the correction path as its destination (S13 14.4). The exit interview offered, with its rules (5.4). The hand-back, where the person holds a seat with items on a row (3.3 for a lead; field 15 for anyone). That the state will be read back and saved with the person's words beside it if they choose. All of it in canon's correspondence register: "names what it knows specifically. Confirms without reselling. No hospitality-speak" (Canon 1.0, section 09), which is the one register canon holds for a sentence addressed to a person about a fact.

What is never said. Any sentence whose subject is the person and whose predicate is a state the records do not hold: that they are a low performer, failed, did not fit, would be better elsewhere, are a bad person, or any word about their character, will, effort, attitude, or life. Any reason beyond the citation, because the reasons are on records the person has read and a reason said aloud in a partner's voice is a case file spoken. Any comparison to anyone. Her "I'm sorry, but I don't see another path" (Book p.457) is refused as a script: an apology performed at a decision the mechanism made is the register canon forbids, and a partner who is sorry says so in their own words or not at all, and this page writes no founder's words. Any negotiation of the decision, which is taken, or of pay, which has no lever. Any sentence about what the house will say about the person to anyone, beyond 5.2's rule, which the person hears as a rule and not a favor. Her senior-level "it's not about the person, it's about the gap between the role, the company's needs, and the performance" (Book p.459) is closer to the house's register and is still refused as a sentence, because the house does not narrate the gap; the records do.

Two exceptions, both hers, both kept. Where the person states that a record in the package or the line is wrong as a matter of fact (a date, a citation, a count), the conversation pauses, the correction path runs (S13 14.4), the decision is held on the partners' page as pending a record correction, and the item returns to the next partners' review; a state is saved only after. Where the person says a word that names a legal claim, retaliation, discrimination, harassment, or any word counsel names, the conversation pauses, the partner says that the conversation continues with counsel present, and counsel's questions in section 14 govern from there; nothing further is said and nothing is saved until counsel has read.

Junior and senior. Refused as a distinction. One conversation, in one form, for every seat from a runner to a lead. A lead's departure adds the hand-back and the structure change and changes nothing in the room. Her senior-level months of conversations in which the leader "sees the writing on the wall" are refused whole: a lead whose window is open knows it from the block, and a lead in no window is told nothing about leaving by anyone, because no one in the house holds a hypothesis on the outcome (S14 P33).

**P26 (Recommended).** The decision is said by the partner who took it with the person's check-in holder present and an interpreter where needed, off the floor at a closed hour or a window with slack, on the clock on the person's next scheduled shift and never later than the second, before coverage and before the state is saved, in the person's language, in canon's correspondence register: the state with its citation, the last day, what the person keeps, final pay as the pay page shows it, the exit interview offered, the hand-back, and the read-back. No sentence about the person's character, will, fit, or life; no reasons beyond the citation; no comparison; no performed apology; no negotiation. A stated record error or a legal word pauses it. One conversation for every seat.

### 4.7 Final pay, against a pay system with no lever

Final pay is the days worked and the accrued share to the last pay date (`17tn048qepa`; S13 P23, P24), which the pay page already shows every morning. The last day's pool share is computed at that day's close like any day's. No severance, no exit package, no negotiated amount, no withheld anything exists as a mechanism, because the system has no raise to withhold, no bonus to deny, and no rating to lower (S13 P36), and a house that cannot punish through pay cannot bargain through it either. Her severance matrix and exit package are refused as negotiating tools. What her chapter reaches for through them, a departure that does not leave a person without means for a stated time, is a founders' question with counsel and not a lever: "separation payment, if any" is a named, unset row on the parameters register (S13 I5), owned by the founders with counsel, and its existence as a row is all this page states; the team recommends that if the founders set one it is a rule for every departure of the second and third kinds and never a matter negotiated in any room, because "one rule for everyone by construction" is the pay system's whole defense (S13 9.4). Final pay's timing, the last pay date's law, the form of any separation payment, whether a release may be asked for anything, and whether any part of the pool share may be withheld or must be paid by a date are counsel's (14). Benefits' end date and any continuation are counsel's and the parameters register's benefits rows (S13 P25). The pay correction path stays open to a departed person for their final pay, and how long, and how they reach it after their access ends, is a configuration question with counsel.

**P27 (Recommended).** Final pay is the days worked and the accrued share to the last pay date, computed by the stack as any day's and shown on the pay page; no severance, exit package, negotiation, or withholding exists as a mechanism. "Separation payment, if any" is a named, unset row on the parameters register owned by the founders with counsel, recommended as one rule for every departure if set and never negotiated. Timing, releases, benefits' end, and the departed person's access to the correction path are counsel's and configuration.

### 4.8 Dignity: what the person keeps, and what the house never does

What the person keeps. Their pay page, readable for their final pay and any correction for a stated period after the last day, the period and the access counsel's and configuration. A copy of their person page's own words, in their language, on request, because the words were always theirs (S11 P17); whether the house may or must retain the page, and for how long, is counsel's. Their readiness forms and fluency record as facts about competencies they hold, which travel with them in the sense that any future house may ask and the person may say. Their relationships: "the belonging was always to the person, not just the place" (WP p.25), so a server's regulars are theirs to keep as relationships, and the customer record is the house's system (WP p.27); no person is asked to hand back a relationship, and no customer data leaves the system, with the line between them counsel's (finding 13.8). The uniform, the locker, and the tools: the sheet states what is issued and what is returned, the locker is cleared on the clock during the last scheduled shift and never after hours, and the house asks nothing back that it does not list on the sheet at the first interview. A reference, under 5.2's rule.

What the house never does. Walk a person out mid-service or in a customer's or the roster's sight; the conversation is off the floor and the last day is a scheduled date. Remove access, cancel a shift, or thin a schedule before the conversation. Ask a person to leave the same day as a default; her same-day departure for gross misconduct is answered by the paid hold (4.9) and by the partners' date, not by a manager's escort. Say anything about the person to the roster beyond coverage, to a customer beyond 5.2, or to anyone outside beyond the same. Contact the person after the last day for anything but pay, records, and the consented question in 5.5. Record a farewell, a message, or a word on the person's behalf. Ask for a resignation in place of the decision, because a departure the house decided is written as the house's decision and never dressed as the person's, which is Jaeyeonmi's "nothing conceals what it is" (Canon 1.0, section 01) read at the one place a house is most tempted to conceal. Or treat a departure as a fact about the person's future: "how people grow beyond the building" (WP p.23) is the measure the house takes on itself, and a departure of any kind is the first day of it.

**P28 (Recommended).** The person keeps their pay page for their final pay, a copy of their own words on request, their competencies as facts, their relationships as relationships with the record as the house's, and everything the sheet does not list as returned; the locker is cleared on the clock. The house never walks a person out in sight, removes access or shifts before the conversation, defaults to a same-day leave, says anything beyond coverage, contacts the person after for anything but pay, records, and the consented question, records a word on their behalf, or asks for a resignation in place of its decision.

### 4.9 The paid hold after a flag read

Her gross misconduct leaves the same day. At Sŏn the flag read writes its state before the person's next service (S14 P24), and the decision about staying is the partners' at a review that may be up to a week away. Between the two the person is on the roster. The reading: where the flag read has written "a non-negotiable act" and the act's object is a person in the house (toxicity or exploitation toward a colleague or a customer in the room), the person is scheduled off from the read to the partners' review, paid at base for every shift the schedule had placed, with those days counting as days worked for the pool as if worked, because the house has decided nothing and does not punish before deciding, and because a paid hold is the difference between "no disposable people" and a suspension. Where the act's object is not a person (a disposal in the third sense, a range act read as exploitation), the person works. The hold is a schedule state written by the stack from the flag record's state with no reason field, readable by the person, and its length is bounded by the next partners' review with no parameter of its own. It forecloses the person's agency for its length, which the Playground allows only "with a documented reason" (Canon 1.0, section 01), and the reason is the other people in the room, written here. The alternative, that the person works until the review, is stated and not recommended where the act's object is a person. Whether a paid hold in this form is lawful, how base pay for unworked scheduled shifts is treated, and whether the pool's "as if worked" day is a wage question are counsel's (14). Founder-gated, because it is the one place this page places a consequence before a decision.

**P29 (Founder-gated).** After a flag read writes a non-negotiable act whose object is a person in the house, the person is scheduled off to the next partners' review, paid at base for the scheduled shifts with the days counting for the pool, as a schedule state with no reason field; where the act's object is not a person, the person works. The alternative is stated and not recommended. Counsel on the hold's form and pay.

### 4.11 The four-part entry test and the six-question playground test

Entry test (S4 2.2). Cost in bandwidth: one reserved-class item at a partners' review that already meets weekly, with counsel's time, which is a spend this program does not price; one conversation block on the clock; one paid hold's shifts where a line stands; the exit interview's block inside a scheduled shift. What it buys: the one act the record leaves undescribed, described, so that the first departure the house causes runs on a record and not on a founder's nerve; and the refusal of every mechanism a house reaches for instead, the thinned schedule, the quiet non-renewal, the resignation asked for, the manager's escort. Who runs it: the stack assembles and writes states; the partners decide; counsel reads; the partner says it; the check-in holder is present and holds the exit interview; the domain lead writes field 15. Where its record lives: the partners' page's decision log in the closed form; the departure state on the person page; the counts on the leads' review record and the reset; the counsel register on the partners' page.

Playground test (S10 P3). The condition: a person knows, before anything happens, exactly what can end their employment and how, in every house language on the departure page (I8), which is agency in canon's sense. The legible rule: two records and only two can reach the partners, the order is a card, and the status quo is staying. The open-ended result: staying is a real outcome and the default. The tight edge: a departure on any refused record, a lead's decision, or a reduction with a person as its reason, which the closed inputs and the reason line refuse. The loose edge: a person whose staying is under decision for months, or a house that never decides and lets a line or a not-held state stand while the roster carries the tension, which is disposal by neglect; the next-review rule and the two-review fault are the reads that notice. The documented reason: this section, and the read that would notice the condition's absence is the reset's count of decisions against the count of packages and lines that never reached one.

**P31 (Recommended).** The protocol passes the entry test and the playground test as stated, with its costs named, its records placed, staying as the default and a real outcome, and the two-review fault as the read that notices disposal by neglect.

## 5. After a departure

### 5.1 The three-pass read, field 15, and the pool

Every departure, of any kind, is read in Session 9's three passes and this page changes none of them (S9 P30). The first pass, the week of the departure: the tension-loss row, field 15, written by the domain lead the day the departure is known (S5 P12), stating what the node held; the leads' review's one line with the mode left open; the pool read before the seat opens externally, with the reading recorded on the sheet (S5 P60; `86akh7ref`); and the adjacent seats' thermal-load and check-in lines over the following weeks. The second pass, memory failure: whether what the node held was in the system, with every "in its head" written as sequence corruption or skill invisibility, so the departure's cost is bounded in the only unit the program carries, which mechanism now has to be maintained (S9 9.1; `86akhpvtf`). The third pass, at re-fill: whether the re-established node carries the tension. What this page adds is the hand-back block for a departing lead (3.3) and, for any departing person who chooses it, the exit interview's building lines (5.4) as a source for the second pass, so that the memory-failure read hears from the person what the records did not hold. A departure of the second kind is a departure like any other in all three passes; the reason the house decided is on the partners' page and is read by no pass.

Managing out ends where the succession line begins (`86akh7ref`): no departure of any kind produces a seat opened externally with the pool unread, and a lead seat opened externally after dinner's first quarter carries the written line on the partners' page (S5 P60).

### 5.2 What the house says, inside and outside

Inside. Coverage, and nothing else: at the next brief of every open period, under the change line, which seat is open, which seats hold its functions this service, and which designation carries what the node held, once, in the roster's languages, and then as the designations' line while the seat is open (S11 P25). No lead speaks a word about a departed person at a brief, a close, the leads' review beyond the count, or any room, and a word spoken is a range breach on the speaker with its destination under 2.10 (S11 17.1). No message about a departure goes to the channel or any surface (S11 P25). A person in the house who asks a lead why someone left is told that the house says coverage and that the person's words are theirs, which is the departure page's rule and not a lead's refusal. The person's own goodbye is theirs, in person, on a shift they work, recorded nowhere.
