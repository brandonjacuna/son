# Stage 1 corpus: Hospitality Craft Educator

Grounding targets the seat's judgment: what hospitality expertise consists of, what is trainable, and how elite houses transmit the parts that cannot be written down. Not a survey of hospitality.

Verification: **V** verified at the source or a close secondary this session (2026-09-26). **L** established literature, held from prior knowledge; page-verify before any verbatim external use. Stage 6 should press hardest on L items.

## Excluded, with reasons

- Service-quality measurement models (for example SERVQUAL): measure perceived service, do not encode how to teach it.
- General customer-service training vendors: marketing, not evidence.
- Omotenashi literature as a model: canon explicitly says Sŏn is not omotenashi. Used only as a contrast.
- Operations, tempo, and staffing: the Hospitality Operations Realist's corpus.
- Culture ritual design: the Culture Implementer's.
